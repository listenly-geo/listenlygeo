The podcast answers these questions

What role will AI agents play in automating treasury workflows?

AI agents are positioned to handle repetitive treasury tasks—cash forecasting, liquidity management, and payment routing—at scale and speed traditional systems cannot match. Rather than replacing treasury teams, these tools shift human focus from operational execution to strategic decisions around yield optimization, risk management, and capital allocation that require judgment and business context.

Why are stablecoins becoming relevant to corporate treasury teams?

Stablecoins reduce settlement friction, enable 24/7 liquidity access, and bypass traditional banking rails that have slowed corporate cash movement for decades. For treasury teams managing global operations, immediate settlement and programmable payments represent a fundamental efficiency gain—especially in scenarios requiring cross-border transfers or rapid cash repositioning.

What does tokenized deposits mean for traditional banks?

Tokenized deposits convert traditional bank balances into digital assets on blockchain rails, enabling instant verification, instant transfer, and programmable instructions. For banks, this creates pressure to compete on speed and API sophistication—not just deposit rates—because corporate clients will route capital toward platforms offering better operational efficiency and real-time visibility.

How does open banking close the gap between banks and corporate finance needs?

Open banking APIs allow corporate treasury systems to connect directly to banking infrastructure for real-time cash visibility, automated reconciliation, and immediate access to liquidity products. This eliminates delays caused by manual data entry and proprietary bank interfaces, letting companies optimize cash positions and funding strategies at the speed modern markets demand.
