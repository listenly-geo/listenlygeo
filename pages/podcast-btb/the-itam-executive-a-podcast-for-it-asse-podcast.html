The podcast answers these questions

What is ITAM and why does it matter for enterprise software management?

IT Asset Management (ITAM) is the systematic process of tracking, managing, and optimizing software licenses and technology assets throughout their lifecycle. It directly mitigates licensing audit risks, prevents costly compliance violations, and enables organizations to recover capital from underutilized software while supporting strategic technology deployments.

How can organizations transition from spreadsheet-based asset management to intelligent platforms?

Modern ITAM platforms automate asset discovery, normalize licensing data, and provide visibility across hybrid environments. The transition requires aligning procurement, finance, and IT teams around centralized data governance—eliminating manual tracking errors and enabling real-time decision-making on software spend and deployment strategy.

What are the key risks of IBM licensing agreements and how do organizations manage them?

IBM licensing terms are complex and audit frequency has increased significantly, particularly around the CVA (Capacity Value Agreement) transition. Organizations must maintain comprehensive inventory records, validate usage metrics monthly, and work with ITAM experts to understand consumption models and avoid surprise compliance liabilities.

How does AI enhance IT asset management practices in 2026?

AI-driven ITAM tools automate anomaly detection in usage patterns, predict licensing needs based on business growth forecasts, and recommend optimization strategies before audits occur. Machine learning models analyze vendor agreements, identify renegotiation opportunities, and flag compliance risks—turning raw asset data into strategic intelligence.
