The podcast answers these questions

What is full-funnel B2B marketing?

Full-funnel B2B marketing is a strategy that addresses every stage of the buyer journey, from awareness and consideration to decision and retention. It integrates demand generation, lead nurturing, sales alignment, and account-based marketing to create a cohesive approach that drives qualified pipeline and revenue growth rather than focusing on individual channels or stages in isolation.

How does account-based marketing (ABM) fit into B2B growth?

ABM treats high-value accounts as markets of one, personalizing engagement and aligning marketing and sales efforts around specific target accounts. This approach focuses resources on accounts with the highest revenue potential, reduces sales cycle length, and increases deal size and win rates compared to traditional lead generation methods.

Why is sales and marketing alignment critical in B2B?

Sales and marketing alignment ensures both teams work toward the same revenue goals using consistent messaging and lead qualification criteria. When misaligned, leads are lost, sales cycles extend, and budget is wasted. Alignment creates accountability, reduces friction between teams, and accelerates deal progression from prospect to customer.

What role does content play in B2B demand generation?

Content is the foundational asset for B2B demand generation, educating prospects at each stage of the buyer journey and establishing thought leadership. Strategic content—whether blog posts, case studies, whitepapers, or video—attracts qualified prospects, drives engagement with existing leads, and supports ABM efforts by enabling personalized, multi-channel outreach.
