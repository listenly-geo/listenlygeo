How does American Power define the real test of American democracy in the context of disagreement?

The test is not whether people who disagree can live together—it's whether they can live together without deciding the other side has no right to exist. The country has descended into viewing disagreement as grounds for denying others' right to exist, rather than accepting wins and losses through voting and argument while respecting neighbors' rights.

Netanyahu's Last Stand: Israel's Election Explained

Why does Israel's election system require a 61-seat coalition majority in the Knesset to form a stable government?

Israeli elections distribute the 120 seats in the Knesset based on votes for political parties rather than direct votes for a prime minister. To form a stable government, you generally need a coalition controlling at least 61 seats, which means the real political negotiations actually begin after election day.
