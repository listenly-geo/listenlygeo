The curb cut effect works because designing for the most challenging use case forces innovation that serves everyone. When a product is engineered to work for someone with significant accessibility needs, it naturally becomes more intuitive, reliable, and user-friendly for the broader population.

As Brittany Palmer explains in this episode , this principle is driving how companies in the disability tech space approach product development.
