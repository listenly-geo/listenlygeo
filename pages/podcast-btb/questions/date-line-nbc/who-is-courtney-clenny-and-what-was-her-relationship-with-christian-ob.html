Who is Courtney Clenny, and what was her relationship with Christian Obamsele?

Courtney Clenny was a 25-year-old OnlyFans model with approximately 2 million Instagram followers who earned an estimated $3 million from OnlyFans during 2020 and 2021 combined, according to prosecutors. She and Christian Obamsele began dating in 2020. Despite a glamorous public image built around fancy cars and luxurious vacations, their relationship was repeatedly disrupted by violent arguments — serious enough for building management to prepare eviction proceedings against the couple.

The relationship came to a fatal end on April 3rd, 2022. At 4:57 PM, Clenny called 911 — approximately 20 minutes after Obamsele had returned to their apartment at around 4:30 PM. When investigators interviewed her, Clenny stated she had thrown a knife at him from a distance of about 10 feet. The medical examiner's findings directly contradicted that account: the wound was far too deep to be consistent with a throw from that distance. Authorities moved quickly to charge her with murder, and prosecutors described her own words as effectively amounting to a confession. Context: The Plea Deal

Florida manslaughter sentencing guidelines carry just over nine years. At the time of her plea deal, Clenny had already served four years — meaning she faced a significantly reduced remaining sentence compared to a full murder conviction.
