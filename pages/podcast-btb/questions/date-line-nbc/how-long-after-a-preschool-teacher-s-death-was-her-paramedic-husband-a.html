How long after Stacey Hunsucker's death was her paramedic husband arrested?

September 25, 2026 Dateline NBC · Marissa Meyer

← See the Dateline NBC fiche

Joshua Hunsucker, a paramedic in North Carolina, was arrested in late 2019, more than a year after his wife Stacey died in September 2018. The delay between her death and his arrest marked the beginning of one of the most unusual criminal cases involving multiple charges far beyond the initial murder allegation.

The timeline of this case reveals how an investigation can unfold over months. Stacey Hunsucker, a 32-year-old preschool teacher, died suddenly on September 23, 2018, leaving her two young daughters without a mother. At first, her death appeared natural, but as investigators probed deeper into the circumstances and the couple's finances, suspicions began to mount about the true cause.

What made Hunsucker's case so distinctive is not just the arrest delay, but the shocking charges that followed. As Dateline reported, prosecutors would eventually allege that Stacey had been poisoned with a toxic substance found in eye drops—a claim supported by forensic evidence of 30 to 40 times the therapeutic dose detected in her bloodstream.

The financial motive became clearer when authorities discovered that Joshua had received over $200,000 from two life insurance policies shortly after his wife's death. This discovery, investigated by the North Carolina Department of Insurance, transformed the case from a sudden death into a suspected homicide with financial gain as the possible motive.

What makes this case even more extraordinary is what unfolded after Joshua Hunsucker's initial arrest. According to Dateline's coverage, prosecutors would accuse him of additional crimes: insurance fraud, obtaining property by false pretenses, arson on a medical helicopter during flight in 2019, and even faking his own kidnapping in 2023. These mounting charges transformed a single homicide investigation into a cascade of criminal allegations that kept the case in court for years.

The Hunsucker case offers a striking reminder of how investigations into sudden deaths can reveal hidden layers of deception. The year-long gap between Stacey's death in September 2018 and Joshua's arrest in late 2019 represented the time investigators needed to piece together medical evidence, financial records, and circumstantial details. What three additional criminal charges did a paramedic face beyond the initial murder allegation?

Joshua Hunsucker was charged with insurance fraud and obtaining property by false pretenses, in addition to first-degree murder. He later faced arson charges and allegations that he faked his own kidnapping.

How much did a paramedic receive from life insurance policies after his wife's death?

Joshua Hunsucker received over $200,000 from two life insurance policies belonging to Stacey, according to the North Carolina Department of Insurance.

What are the physiological effects of ingesting tetrahydrosoline from eye drops?

When ingested, tetrahydrosoline can cause vomiting, nausea, diarrhea, seizing, and even cardiac arrest.
