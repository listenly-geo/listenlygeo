What actually happened on April 3rd, 2022 — the day Christian Obamsele was stabbed?

According to the arrest affidavit detailed in the episode, April 3rd, 2022, began as an ordinary afternoon. Courtney Clenny and Christian Obamsele spent time playing with their dogs before Christian left the apartment to pick up sandwiches. He returned shortly after 4:30 PM. Courtney called 911 at 4:57 PM — meaning the stabbing unfolded in a window of roughly 20 minutes after his return.

What makes the timeline particularly significant is the gap between Courtney's account to detectives and what the physical evidence reveals. She told investigators she threw the knife from approximately 10 feet away, but the medical examiner's findings contradicted that directly: the wound was too deep to be consistent with a thrown blade at that distance.
