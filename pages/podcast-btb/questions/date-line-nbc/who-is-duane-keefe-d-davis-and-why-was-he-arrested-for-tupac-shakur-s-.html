Who is Dwayne "Keefe D" Davis and why was he arrested for Tupac Shakur's murder?

Dwayne Keith Davis, known as Keefe D, is the uncle of Orlando "Baby Lane" Anderson — the man believed to be the trigger man in Tupac Shakur's September 1996 murder. Davis was arrested in September 2023 at age 60, nearly 27 years after the killing, after investigators concluded that statements he made in his memoir Compton Street Legend and in a BET interview amounted to a near-confession: he had acknowledged being in the front seat of the car from which the fatal shots were fired.

Tupac Shakur was shot four times on September 7, 1996, following a boxing match at the MGM Grand Casino in Las Vegas. He died six days later at age 25. The case went unsolved for almost three decades, shadowed by the gang tensions between Mob Piru affiliates tied to Death Row Records and the Crips. Davis's own words ultimately provided the basis for the arrest: rather than evidence uncovered through traditional investigation, it was the Clark County Sheriff and the Clark County DA who determined that his public admissions — in print and on camera — crossed the line into self-incrimination.

"So what authorities are saying is, you said it in your own words. It's kind of tantamount to a confession."

— Dateline NBC, August 6, 2026

The episode, covered in depth on Dateline NBC, frames Davis's trial as a landmark moment — the first time a suspect has faced formal charges in one of the most famous unsolved murders in music history. The charge against Davis centers on his admitted presence and alleged orchestration of the attack, even though it is his nephew Anderson — who died in a separate shooting in 1998 — who is believed to have fired the weapon.

What plea deal did Courtney Clenny agree to and what are the potential sentencing implications?

Courtney Clenny agreed to a plea deal whose specific terms were set to be made public on August 10th. She had been charged with second-degree murder, but it appeared the deal involved a lesser charge, with Florida manslaughter sentencing guidelines carrying just over nine years — and Clenny had already served four years at the time of the plea.

What evidence did Courtney Clenny's defense team present to support her version of events?

The defense conducted an experiment using a pig carcass where someone threw a knife at it, and they argued the angle and depth of the wound was consistent with Clenny's account of throwing the knife from a distance in self-defense.

Why did investigators doubt Courtney Clenny's self-defense claim?

Investigators noted that no significant injuries consistent with the attack she described were observed on Courtney. Additionally, the medical examiner found the wound too deep to be consistent with her claim that she threw the knife from about 10 feet away.
