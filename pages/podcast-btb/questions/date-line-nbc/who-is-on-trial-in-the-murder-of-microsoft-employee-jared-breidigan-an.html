Who is on trial for Microsoft employee Jared Breidigan's murder?

September 19, 2026 Dateline NBC

← See the Dateline NBC fiche

Mario Fernandez Saldana, husband of Jared Breidigan's ex-wife Shanna Gardner, is the first of three defendants on trial for the 2022 murder of the 33-year-old Microsoft employee. Prosecutors allege that Saldana and Gardner hired a man named Henry Tennon to ambush and kill Breidigan in Jacksonville Beach, Florida, with the alleged motive rooted in a bitter custody battle over the twins the two had together.

The case stems from a long-running family conflict centered on guardianship and custody rights. As detailed in this episode of Dateline NBC, Breidigan's widow has already taken the stand and was immediately emotional during her testimony—a powerful moment that underscores the personal stakes in what prosecutors characterize as a premeditated hiring of a hitman.

The case is significant because it represents the first trial of multiple defendants charged in connection with the same murder. Saldana and Gardner's alleged roles—orchestrating the alleged hit—place them at the center of a conspiracy that prosecutors argue was born from a family law dispute that escalated to violence.

The trial's focus on the custody dispute as the alleged motive illustrates how domestic conflicts can, in this prosecution's view, transform into criminal conspiracy. The involvement of a third party, Henry Tennon, as the alleged triggerman, adds layers of complexity to the prosecution's narrative and the evidence they must present to secure convictions.

The custody battle that prosecutors say fueled the crime

At the heart of this case lies a dispute over the children Breidigan and Gardner shared. According to prosecutors, this conflict created the circumstances that allegedly led Saldana and Gardner to pursue such an extreme course of action. The alleged hiring of a hitman stands as a dramatic escalation of what had been a contentious legal matter.

This type of custody-driven motive is explored extensively in the full episode, where the emotional impact of Breidigan's widow's testimony reveals the human cost of the alleged plot. The prosecution's case rests heavily on establishing both the motive and the specific roles each defendant played in the alleged conspiracy.

With Saldana's trial now underway, the legal system is examining whether family conflict was weaponized into criminal action. The outcome of this first trial will likely set the tone for the subsequent trials of the other two defendants charged in connection with Breidigan's death.
