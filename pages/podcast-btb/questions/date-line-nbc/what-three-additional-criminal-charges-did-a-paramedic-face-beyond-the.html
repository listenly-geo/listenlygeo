Beyond Murder: What Other Criminal Charges Did the Paramedic Face?

September 24, 2026 · Dateline NBC · Marissa Meyer

← See the Dateline NBC fiche

Joshua Hunsucker was charged with insurance fraud and obtaining property by false pretenses in addition to first-degree murder, followed by arson charges related to a 2019 medical helicopter fire, witness intimidation, and obstruction of justice charges stemming from an alleged staged kidnapping.

The charges against Hunsucker expanded dramatically as investigators uncovered the full scope of his alleged criminal behavior. Beyond the initial murder charge in the death of preschool teacher Stacey Hunsucker, prosecutors built a case that painted a picture of systematic deception and criminal activity spanning years.

The insurance fraud charges stemmed directly from the death of his wife, as Hunsucker allegedly sought to benefit financially from her death through life insurance policies. The charge of obtaining property by false pretenses worked in tandem with the fraud allegations, suggesting he knowingly deceived insurance companies to claim benefits he was not legitimately entitled to receive.

The arson charge emerged from an incident in late 2019 involving a medical helicopter during flight. Hunsucker, a paramedic with access to aircraft through his professional work, allegedly started a fire that created an entirely separate criminal investigation into his conduct. This charge suggested a pattern of dangerous and reckless behavior unrelated to the original murder case.

By 2023, prosecutors added witness intimidation and obstruction of justice charges when Hunsucker allegedly staged his own kidnapping to manipulate the legal proceedings against him. This apparent desperation to interfere with the investigation demonstrated the seriousness with which authorities viewed his actions across multiple domains—financial fraud, violence, and active obstruction of the justice system itself.

The breadth of charges reflected what investigators uncovered as a pattern of behavior spanning financial crime, violence, and active attempts to evade accountability. Each charge built upon evidence that prosecutors believe showed a man willing to break multiple laws to achieve his goals and avoid consequences.
