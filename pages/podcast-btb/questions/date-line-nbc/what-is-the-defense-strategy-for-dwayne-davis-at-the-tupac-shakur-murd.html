What is the defense strategy for Dwayne Davis at the Tupac Shakur murder trial?

Dwayne Keith Davis's defense team is building its case on a single core argument: the prosecution has no physical evidence. There is no gun, no ballistics match, and no DNA — the exact types of evidence juries expect to see in a murder conviction. Without that forensic foundation, the defense contends, the entire case cannot stand.

The prosecution's most prominent piece of evidence is Davis's own words. Prosecutors point to statements Davis made in his book, arguing they amount to a confession — effectively saying, as the episode frames it, "you said it in your own words." His defense pushes back hard on that characterization: those statements, they argue, were embellishment and storytelling, not admissions of guilt. Crucially, they argue the prosecution cannot lean on those statements alone while lacking any corroborating forensic evidence. One cannot substitute for the other.

"So what authorities are saying is, you said it in your own words. It's kind of tantamount to a confession."

— Dateline NBC, August 6, 2026

The stakes of this legal battle are enormous given the timeline. Davis was arrested in September 2023 at age 60 by the Clark County Sheriff, nearly 27 years after Tupac Shakur was shot four times outside the MGM Grand Casino on September 7, 1996, and died six days later at age 25. The prosecution, led by the Clark County DA, is now asking a jury to convict based on self-incriminating statements in a book — without the physical corroboration that has become standard in modern criminal trials. Why did it take nearly 30 years to charge someone with Tupac Shakur's murder?

The case stalled partly because Orlando Anderson, the alleged shooter, died in an unrelated gang-related shooting a few years after Tupac's murder, removing the most direct suspect and leaving investigators without a key witness to build a case around.

Who is Duane Keefe D Davis and why was he arrested for Tupac Shakur's murder?

Dwayne Keith Davis, also known as Keefe D, is the uncle of Orlando "Baby Lane" Anderson, who is believed to be the trigger man in Tupac Shakur's 1996 murder. Davis was arrested in September 2023, nearly 27 years after the shooting.

What plea deal did Courtney Clenny agree to and what are the potential sentencing implications?

Courtney Clenny agreed to a plea deal whose specific terms were set to be made public on August 10th. She had been charged with second-degree murder, but the deal is expected to involve a lesser charge. Florida manslaughter sentencing guidelines carry just over nine years, and Clenny had already served four years at the time of the agreement.
