Why did investigators doubt Courtney Clenny's self-defense claim?

Investigators doubted Courtney Clenny's self-defense account for two concrete reasons. First, she showed no significant injuries consistent with the violent attack she described. Second, and more critically, the medical examiner determined that the knife wound inflicted on Christian Obamsele was far too deep — penetrating skin, muscle, and puncturing an artery — to have been caused by a knife thrown from 10 feet away, which is what Clenny told detectives.

Clenny called 911 at 4:57 PM on April 3rd, 2022 — roughly 20 minutes after Obamsele returned to their apartment. Her account to detectives centered on a thrown knife as a last-resort act of self-defense. But forensic medicine made that story physically implausible: a projectile knife, launched across a room, cannot produce the kind of wound depth — through multiple layers of tissue and into an arterial wall — that the medical examiner documented. The physical evidence and the claimed mechanism simply didn't align.
