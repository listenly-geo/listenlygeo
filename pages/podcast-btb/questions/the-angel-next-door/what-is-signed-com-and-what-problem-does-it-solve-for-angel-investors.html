What is Signed.com and what problem does it solve for angel investors?

August 21, 2026 The Angel Next Door · Zach Holman

← Voir la fiche The Angel Next Door

Signed.com is a portfolio tracking platform built specifically for angel investors who are done managing investments through spreadsheets. It tracks financial details — price per share, safe conversions, number of shares — and uses AI to parse investor updates forwarded by email, extracting metrics and comparing founders' predictions against the actual results they post the following month.

From "a couple hundred companies" to one gnarly spreadsheet — and a product built in frustration

Zach Holman has been angel investing for seven to eight years, with his first investment made around 2018. Over that period, he has invested in approximately a couple hundred companies — a volume that makes spreadsheet-based tracking not just inconvenient, but genuinely unmanageable. Holman built Signed.com directly out of his own experience hitting that wall, as he explains in The Angel Next Door.

The problem is one that host Marcia Dawood recognized immediately: her own angel portfolio spreadsheet had grown "a little gnarly" over the years. That shared frustration is precisely the pain point Signed.com targets — active angel investors whose deal count has outpaced what any manual system can handle cleanly.

What is a SAFE conversion?
A SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) is a common early-stage investment instrument. When a startup raises a priced round, SAFEs convert into equity — often at a discount or with a valuation cap. Tracking the resulting price per share and number of shares across dozens of SAFEs is one of the core data management problems Signed.com addresses.

AI that reads your founder updates — and holds them accountable a month later

Beyond financial data, Signed.com adds a layer most tools don't: automated analysis of the narrative updates that founders send their investors. An investor forwards an update email to Signed.com, and the platform's AI parses it — identifying the metrics the founder reports and any predictions they make for the month ahead.

Then, when the next update arrives, Signed.com can compare what the founder predicted against what they actually reported. That kind of systematic accountability tracking — across a portfolio of dozens or hundreds of companies — is something no spreadsheet realistically supports. It's a feature discussed directly in this episode of The Angel Next Door.

The platform also handles tax-related pain points. Signed.com launched its tax features ahead of the tax season in early 2026, helping investors wrangle the K-1s and other documents that accumulate across a large portfolio — a detail Holman flagged as a real driver of early adoption on The Angel Next Door.

"I've invested in a couple hundred companies. I think it's really hard to do that just in a spreadsheet form and trying to figure things out and where to go from this."

Signed.com positions itself as a dedicated alternative to the general-purpose tools — like Carta or AngelList — that were not built with the individual angel investor's workflow in mind. Holman's background as an early engineer at a company that scaled to a Microsoft acquisition gives him credible standing on the infrastructure side, which comes through clearly when he discusses the product on The Angel Next Door.

Key takeaways

  • Signed.com replaces spreadsheets with structured financial tracking — price per share, SAFE conversions, share counts — across an entire angel portfolio.
  • Its AI parses forwarded investor update emails, extracts reported metrics, and later checks them against the following month's actual results.
  • The platform launched in early 2026 with tax features ready, directly targeting K-1 management as a pain point for active angel investors.
