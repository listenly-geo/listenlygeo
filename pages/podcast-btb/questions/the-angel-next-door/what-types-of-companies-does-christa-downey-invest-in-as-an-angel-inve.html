What types of companies does Christa Downey target as an angel investor?

Christa Downey focuses exclusively on mission-driven companies, with her investments concentrated in three core areas: health tech, climate tech, and platforms designed to build wealth and expand access to investing. She evaluates each opportunity through a dual lens — the financial upside and the tangible real-world impact — and actively seeks investments where those two goals reinforce each other.

Downey is particularly drawn to opportunities where impact categories overlap. A company addressing both climate and health outcomes, for example, represents exactly the kind of intersection she looks for. Her portfolio includes a company working on over-the-counter birth control, climate solutions, and Play Money — a platform designed to make angel investing itself more accessible. This last investment reflects a meta-level conviction: that building tools for more people to participate in early-stage investing is itself a form of mission-driven work.

Downey has also invested as a limited partner in three venture funds, each deploying capital across a couple dozen startups — a strategy that allowed her to build broad diversification quickly. She concentrated much of her direct angel activity in 2023 and 2024 and now plans to spread her investments across additional vintage years going forward. About Christa Downey

CD
Christa Downey
Angel Investor and Coach · Wealth Reimagined

Christa Downey came to angel investing through immersion in the startup ecosystem in Ithaca, New York, centered around Cornell University. It was there that she first encountered the concept of angel investing and began asking what it would look like for someone like her to participate. Her entry point came through her friend Alyssa Miller, founder of Chloe Capital — a venture fund with an active accelerator program in New York City — through which Downey became a limited partner and began deploying capital into early-stage companies alongside a broader portfolio of co-investors.

She built her foundational knowledge through books, resources, and by listening to The Angel Next Door podcast from its earliest episodes — a detail that gives her an unusually grounded perspective on how the angel investing education space has evolved. Today, she operates under the Wealth Reimagined banner, coaching founders on the transition from visionary to CEO, with a focus on leadership development, organizational alignment, and strategic clarity. Her background spans work with nonprofits and the startup ecosystem, informing her conviction that mission and financial return are not in tension — they are complementary goals that the best early-stage investments deliver simultaneously.
