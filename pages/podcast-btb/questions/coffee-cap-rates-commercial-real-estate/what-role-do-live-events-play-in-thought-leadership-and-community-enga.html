How do live events drive thought leadership and community engagement in commercial real estate?

September 25, 2026 Coffee & Cap Rates: Commercial Real Estate Podcast · Shimon Shkury

← See the Coffee & Cap Rates: Commercial Real Estate Podcast fiche

Live events function as both a culmination of market intelligence and a platform for industry connection. Coffee and Cap Rates, held twice yearly, brings together 30 market reports, quick analysis, and industry players, while channeling all proceeds to nonprofit work — turning thought leadership into community impact.

From 50 Attendees to 300: Building Momentum Through Purpose

Coffee and Cap Rates has grown significantly since its inception, scaling from 50 attendees to over 300 industry participants. This expansion reflects both the depth of market intelligence Ariel Property Advisors produces and the genuine value the CRE community finds in gathering for substantive discussion.

The event structure mirrors Ariel's core strength: combining comprehensive market data with live dialogue. By convening 30 market reports and quick-turnaround analysis, the event becomes more than a networking gathering—it functions as a real-time knowledge forum where industry players can calibrate their understanding of the market.

Thought Leadership Tied to Mission-Driven Action

What distinguishes Coffee and Cap Rates is its commitment to external impact. 100% of event proceeds, plus matching funds, go directly to Legal Outreach, a nonprofit partnership facilitated through TD Bank. This model transforms the event from a purely commercial exercise into a vehicle for community empowerment and social mission alignment.

As Shkury explains in the episode, the event serves as a culmination of information and thought leadership while supporting nonprofit mission work and empowering clients and communities. This dual purpose—sharing expertise while funding social good—positions the event as both an industry touchstone and a demonstration of values-aligned business practice.

"We're breaking that traditional model. And so going back to culture, we work in a competitive environment that supports you, and I think a lot of people in our industry don't know what it means to work not in a silo or in a silo."

For a deeper dive into how Ariel structures its advisory practice around information sharing, the full conversation covers the firm's 15-year evolution and its role in the broader CRE ecosystem.

Key takeaways

  • Coffee and Cap Rates has grown from 50 to over 300 attendees, held twice yearly in February and July, consolidating market intelligence with live industry discussion.
  • The event combines 30 market reports and real-time analysis, positioning it as a substantive thought leadership platform rather than a standard networking event.
  • 100% of event proceeds plus matching funds are donated to Legal Outreach nonprofit, embedding social mission directly into the event structure.
  • Live events serve as a culmination point for information-first advisory models, allowing firms to demonstrate expertise while building community connection.
