What daily habit has proven essential to maintaining health and energy over a 15-year period?

23 September 2026 The Mastering Commercial Real Estate Podcast · Joe Fairless

← See the The Mastering Commercial Real Estate Podcast fiche

Every morning, Joe Fairless drinks a liter of water mixed with a scoop of wheatgrass—a habit he has maintained consistently for 15 years. The water flushes out the stagnation that accumulates during sleep, while the wheatgrass alkalizes the blood, counteracting the acidity that builds up from most people's typical diets.

What is wheatgrass?

Wheatgrass is the young grass of the wheat plant, nutrient-dense and commonly used as a dietary supplement. It is believed to have alkalizing properties that help balance blood pH levels, which some health advocates argue is disrupted by acidic foods like processed meats, refined grains, and sugary products.

The practice reflects a deliberate approach to morning routine—starting the day with hydration and nutrient support rather than diving directly into work. Fairless describes the water's flushing action as a way to reset the body after sleep, when circulation slows and cellular waste accumulates overnight.

This habit sits at the intersection of preventive health and sustained energy. As Fairless discusses in the episode, the combination addresses two physiological needs: the immediate need for hydration after eight hours of sleep, and the ongoing need to maintain blood alkalinity in the face of typical acidic dietary patterns.

The consistency of this habit over 15 years underscores a broader principle: as explored in more depth in the full conversation, sustainable success—whether in real estate or personal health—relies on small, non-negotiable daily practices that compound over time.

Beyond this single habit, Fairless's broader philosophy emphasizes that health and energy are not one-off achievements but the result of consistent, intentional behaviors. The wheatgrass-and-water ritual signals a commitment to managing the body's internal environment as deliberately as one might manage a real estate portfolio.

For anyone managing high-stress roles—whether in commercial real estate or other demanding fields—the episode offers insight into how Fairless maintains resilience and energy across multiple responsibilities, including running Ashcroft Capital and hosting the Best Ever Show podcast.

Key takeaways

  • A liter of water with wheatgrass, consumed every morning for 15 years, is Fairless's core health practice.
  • Water flushes out stagnation and cellular waste accumulated during sleep, restoring circulation.
  • Wheatgrass alkalizes the blood, offsetting the acidic effects of typical modern diets.
  • This habit represents a non-negotiable daily practice that compounds into sustained health and energy over decades.
