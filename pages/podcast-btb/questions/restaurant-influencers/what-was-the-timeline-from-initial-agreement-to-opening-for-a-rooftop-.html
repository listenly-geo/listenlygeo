What was the timeline from initial agreement to opening for a rooftop restaurant development in Dublin?

September 24, 2026 Restaurant Influencers · Alan Clancy

← See the Restaurant Influencers fiche

Alan Clancy agreed to the development in early 2021 and spent six to eight months navigating negotiations and legal processes before designs were completed mid-2023. Construction then followed on the 18,000 square foot premise atop the historic Central Bank of Ireland building in Dublin's city center.

Building from Agreement to Blueprint

The journey began with a simple handshake in early 2021, but the path to a working restaurant required navigating complex legal territory. Those first six to eight months were consumed entirely by negotiations and legal processes—the unsexy but essential groundwork that separates a conversation from a binding development agreement.

Clancy spent this period ensuring every detail of ownership, operational rights, and construction authority was properly documented on a historic building that had housed Ireland's Central Bank until its relocation in 2014. As discussed in the full episode, this wasn't a matter of simply booking a contractor—it involved layering permissions, insurance, heritage considerations, and partnership structures onto one of Dublin's most iconic locations.

Design and Tendering: The Blueprint Phase

By mid-2023—roughly two and a half years after the initial agreement—design work was complete and ready for tender. This milestone meant the vision had moved from concept into technical drawings, specifications, and cost estimates that could go to market for construction bids.

The tendering process itself adds another layer to the timeline. Clancy's team wasn't simply hiring the first contractor who walked through the door; they were selecting from competing proposals for a high-stakes, high-visibility project on an 18,000 square foot rooftop in the center of Dublin, as he explains in the episode. Before construction crews could hammer the first nail, the right builder had to be chosen.

"What are we without customers? We're empty. We're out of business. We're nobody."

Beyond the financial and legal scaffolding, Clancy's relentless focus on customer experience shaped every decision in this timeline. He wasn't just clearing hurdles; he was methodically planning an environment where every detail serves the guest, which meant taking the time to get the design right before breaking ground.
