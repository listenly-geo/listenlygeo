How much of e-commerce transactions in the United States now occur on mobile devices?

September 22, 2026 ConPulse - The Construction Podcast · Jennifer Alexander

← See the ConPulse - The Construction Podcast fiche

Mobile commerce accounts for 30% to 40% of all online purchases in the United States, a share that has grown significantly over the last five to six years. This penetration continues to accelerate as consumers increasingly shop on their phones while on the go.

The shift to mobile shopping reflects a fundamental change in consumer behavior. Rather than shopping exclusively from a desktop or laptop, customers now expect the ability to browse and purchase from anywhere—on the commute, during lunch breaks, or late at night. As Jennifer Alexander discusses in the episode, this mobile-first mindset has become central to how U.S. consumers interact with online brands.

For e-commerce businesses in the United States, mobile optimization is no longer optional—it's a core requirement. With nearly one-third to two-fifths of transactions flowing through phones and tablets, any brand that neglects mobile experience risks losing a substantial portion of potential revenue. The growth trajectory suggests this share will only increase in the years ahead.

The data reveals a clear pattern: as payment methods have become more secure and shipping logistics more seamless, friction in the mobile purchase journey has dropped. This has made it easier for consumers to complete transactions on smaller screens, driving adoption. The episode goes deeper into how successful U.S. brands have tailored their entire platforms to mobile-first design, recognizing that a growing majority of their customer base shops primarily on phones.

For brands navigating the U.S. market, understanding mobile's dominance is essential for strategy. Alexander also shares insights on how seasonal peaks like Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday create concentrated periods of mobile traffic, requiring both technical infrastructure and inventory planning to handle demand surges.

What percentage of Americans shop online and what is the penetration of internet access in U.S. households?

90% of U.S. households have internet access, which is among the highest in the world. However, only 70% of Americans actually shop online, indicating there is still a significant gap between access and adoption.

What is the current size and growth rate of the U.S. retail e-commerce market?

Retail e-commerce in the U.S. is about $900 billion and is growing at approximately 7% per year, making up 15% of total retail, while the U.S. represents 14% of total worldwide e-commerce.

What percentage of U.S. e-commerce revenue typically concentrates in the 10 days between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday?

For one company where Jennifer Alexander worked, the period just before Thanksgiving to just after Cyber Monday represented 30% of quarterly revenue in just 10 days, highlighting the critical importance of holiday seasonality.
