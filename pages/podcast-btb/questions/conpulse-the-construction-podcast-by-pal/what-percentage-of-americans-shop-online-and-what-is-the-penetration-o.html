How many Americans shop online and what is the penetration of internet access in U.S. households?

September 21, 2026 ConPulse - The Construction Podcast · Jennifer Alexander

← See the ConPulse - The Construction Podcast fiche

Internet penetration in U.S. households stands at 90%—one of the highest rates globally—yet only 70% of Americans actively shop online. This 20-percentage-point gap reveals substantial untapped market potential among households with internet connectivity who have not yet begun e-commerce transactions.

The disconnect between internet access and online shopping behavior represents a critical opportunity for e-commerce businesses. Despite near-universal internet availability, a significant portion of the U.S. population remains outside the active online shopping ecosystem, according to insights shared in this episode.

The gap between access and adoption

When Jennifer Alexander discusses the U.S. e-commerce landscape, she underscores that internet infrastructure alone does not drive online shopping. The presence of connectivity does not automatically translate into consumer behavior change. Barriers ranging from trust and payment preferences to lack of familiarity with online platforms continue to prevent millions of internet-connected Americans from engaging in e-commerce.

This gap is particularly significant given that the U.S. e-commerce market is valued at approximately $900 billion and is growing at roughly 7% annually. The additional 20% of internet-connected households—representing millions of potential customers—could substantially accelerate that growth if adoption barriers were lowered.

"Authenticity is probably the most critical piece of the pie when you're thinking about launching something in the U.S. market."

Building trust and authenticity becomes even more urgent when targeting the non-adopter segment. These consumers require different messaging, different proof points, and different reassurance than the 70% already active online. The full episode explores specific strategies for reaching these resistant audiences, including how payment flexibility, transparent customer reviews, and brand heritage can convert hesitant shoppers into regular participants.

Key takeaways

  • 90% of U.S. households have internet access, positioning the nation among the world's most connected markets.
  • Only 70% of Americans actively shop online, creating a 20-point adoption gap despite widespread connectivity.
  • The U.S. e-commerce market reaches $900 billion in annual revenue and grows at approximately 7% per year.
  • Non-adopters represent untapped potential; reaching them requires authenticity, trust-building, and targeted messaging beyond standard e-commerce tactics.
