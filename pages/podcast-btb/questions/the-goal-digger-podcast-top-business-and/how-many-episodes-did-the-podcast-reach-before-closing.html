How many episodes did the podcast reach before closing?

September 24, 2026 The Goal Digger Podcast

← See The Goal Digger Podcast fiche

The podcast reached 968 episodes before closing—not the round 1,000 that might have made for a neat narrative. Jenna Kutcher chose to stop at a number that felt aligned with her values rather than push toward an arbitrary milestone.

The decision to close the podcast was made months before the public announcement. Kutcher decided in August, though she didn't share the news publicly until Thanksgiving, giving herself time to sit with the choice before speaking it aloud. This careful pacing reflects her broader philosophy: the decision wasn't reactive or forced by external pressure.

What makes 968 episodes significant is precisely what it isn't. As explored in this episode, Kutcher was explicit about rejecting the voice that says the number itself matters more than the knowing—the belief that you have to hit a round number to make something "real" or complete.

Nearly a decade of podcast production culminated in a moment where alignment won over achievement. The podcast didn't end because it had nothing left to say, but because continuing would have meant choosing the external goal (1,000 episodes) over internal truth. That distinction shapes how the closing feels: not like failure, but like a clear-eyed choice made from a place of peace.

For anyone wrestling with when to step back from a project they've built, the specificity of 968 is the point. It's proof that you don't need permission from a round number or external benchmark to end something well. The episode explores this tension at length, offering context for why Kutcher saw the close of the podcast not as an ending, but as achieving the real goal.

"That is exactly the one that I am done listening to. The one that says that the number matters more than the knowing."

A decade of production, one intentional choice

The Goal Digger Podcast ran for nearly ten years, building a body of work that doesn't need rounding to feel complete. Kutcher has been explicit about the difference between closing from a place of fulfillment versus closing because something broke. Neither the podcast nor her audience demanded an end; she simply reached a point where continuing felt misaligned with who she wanted to be and what she wanted her time to hold.

This kind of decision—to walk away from something successful on your own terms—requires a different kind of strength than pushing through to a predetermined finish line. As Kutcher has written in her book *How Are You Really?*, the question of alignment is often deeper than the question of results. The podcast's final episode count is less about a number and more about the intention behind it.

Key takeaways

  • The Goal Digger Podcast produced 968 episodes before closing—a deliberate endpoint that rejected the pressure to hit 1,000.
  • The decision was made in August but announced publicly at Thanksgiving, reflecting Kutcher's intentional approach to major life choices.
  • Closing the podcast was a choice made from alignment, not crisis—Kutcher stepped back while the podcast was still thriving.
  • The podcast ran for nearly a decade, with each episode representing nearly ten years of consistent production and audience connection.
