What is the scope of machinery representation at Fit Show 2025?

18 September 2026 Glazing Insider · Nikki West

← See the Glazing Insider fiche

Fit Show 2025 displays the biggest machinery representation ever shown at the event, demonstrating that the show now serves every link in the fenestration supply chain. This milestone signals the event's expansion beyond traditional window and door products into a truly comprehensive showcase for fabricators and installers.

The scale of machinery on display at Fit Show 2025 represents a significant shift in how the event serves the broader home improvement sector. With 300 brands exhibiting and products ranging from windows and doors to roof lanterns, garage doors, decking, and fencing, the machinery representation reflects the show's commitment to delivering value to every participant in the supply chain.

As detailed in Glazing Insider's conversation with Nikki West, the inclusion of expanded machinery offerings is particularly important for fabricators and installers who need to evaluate equipment and systems in one location. This concentration of resources—from raw materials to finished systems—saves time and cost for businesses looking to grow their operations.

The breadth of machinery coverage also reflects an important shift in exhibitor composition: 50% of the exhibitors at Fit Show 2025 are brand new to the event, bringing fresh equipment and solutions that hadn't appeared in previous editions. This influx of new machinery and brands demonstrates how the event continues to evolve and attract innovation across the entire sector.

"If you're selling in the home improvement sector, you have to be here. And if you're buying, if you've got to source products or you want to buy anything, you've got to be at Fit Show."

The machinery representation at Fit Show 2025 also underscores the event's role as a critical business opportunity that only occurs every two years. As West explains in the episode, this concentrated offering means businesses can evaluate machinery, source new products, and build relationships with suppliers and innovators without the expense and time required to visit multiple trade shows or supplier locations.

Beyond machinery, the event's scope extends to other elements that strengthen the fenestration ecosystem, including seven systems houses exhibiting in 2025, up from just two in 2023—a direct response to exhibitor and visitor feedback about the need for integrated system solutions.

Why machinery representation matters to the entire supply chain

The emphasis on machinery at Fit Show 2025 reflects a deeper understanding of how the home improvement sector operates. Fabricators need access to reliable equipment to produce windows, doors, and other products at scale. Installers need to understand the latest systems and tools to deliver quality work. Having all these elements under one roof creates a true one-stop business opportunity that justifies the investment of time and travel.

The event's expansion into related categories—including guttering, roofing products, and decking—further demonstrates how machinery and product representation work together. A fabricator considering new equipment can also evaluate complementary materials and systems that might expand their service offering, leading to business growth.

When is Fit Show scheduled for 2027 and what is the preparation timeline?

Fit Show 2027 is scheduled for the 18th to the 20th of May 2027. Although this seems a long way away, it takes every ounce of energy to start the journey.

What international scope has Fit Show achieved for the first time?

Fit Show now has a real international presence from over 20 countries, which is a first for Fit Show. This international representation has become a key strength of the event.

How many systems houses exhibited at Fit Show 2025 compared to previous editions?

In 2023, Fit Show had two systems houses. Visitors provided feedback that they wanted more systems houses, as it was important to them. In 2025, Fit Show now has seven systems houses exhibiting.
