What prompted the acquisition of Jade Engineering and how quickly did the deal move?

4 September 2026 Glazing Insider · Adam Jones

← See the Glazing Insider fiche

When J Tools and Jade began competing for the same customers, Jade's owners were already looking to exit. Adam Jones reached out to one of Jade's founders, Dave Thomas, through a mutual rugby connection, and within 24 hours, acquisition discussions began—the entire process moved remarkably fast.

Market conditions set the stage for this move. J Tools and Jade Engineering were the two primary suppliers of jigs and fixtures in the fenestration industry, with Jade holding the larger market share. As Adam and his co-owner Sean grew J Tools' customer base by winning over some of Jade's clients, the competitive pressure became impossible to ignore. Rather than continuing as rivals, both parties recognized an opportunity to consolidate.

The personal relationship accelerated everything. Adam had crossed paths with Dave Thomas at Neaton Rugby Club, and this existing connection proved invaluable. When the conversation about acquisition came up, Adam explains in the episode that he reached out within 24 hours. Jade's owners had already been considering an exit, so the timing aligned perfectly. What might have taken months in a cold negotiation happened at remarkable speed, driven by mutual respect and a shared understanding of market dynamics.

"We feel like we want to add value and then off the back of the value that we add comes a sale for us but our customers also getting what they need at the end of it."

Beyond the speed of the deal, the episode reveals how the merged entity navigated integrating two long-established competitors and the strategic choices that followed in the post-acquisition phase.
