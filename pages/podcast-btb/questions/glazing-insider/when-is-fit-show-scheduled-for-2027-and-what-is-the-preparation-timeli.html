When is Fit Show scheduled for 2027 and what is the preparation timeline?

September 17, 2026 Glazing Insider · Nikki West

← See the Glazing Insider fiche

Fit Show 2027 is scheduled for May 18–20, 2027 at the NEC (National Exhibition Centre). Despite the dates being nearly two years away, planning and preparation are already underway, with conversations actively beginning with exhibitors about securing prime floor spots and positioning.

Campaign already in motion

The groundwork for Fit Show 2027 begins now, not in 2027. As Nikki West explains in the episode, launching a trade show of Fit Show's scale demands intensive energy and resource allocation from day one. Exhibitors are already being contacted about their participation and their preferred positioning on the floor plan.

This early engagement strategy is not unusual for biennial events held at major exhibition centres. The lengthy preparation timeline reflects the complexity of coordinating 300+ brands, managing hundreds of product launches, and ensuring optimal logistics for thousands of visitors across three days. The earlier the event team begins conversations with both returning and potential new exhibitors, the better the final show layout and attendee experience.

"If you're selling in the home improvement sector, you have to be here. And if you're buying, if you've got to source products or you want to buy anything, you've got to be at Fit Show."

Understanding the commitment required to exhibit at Fit Show, or the value of attending as a buyer, underscores why early preparation is critical. Exhibitors need time to plan booth designs, coordinate logistics, and align marketing campaigns. Buyers need advance notice to schedule attendance and plan procurement strategies. The full conversation reveals just how detailed this coordination process becomes, especially given the unprecedented growth in exhibitor diversity and international attendance in recent editions.

What international scope has Fit Show achieved for the first time?

Fit Show now has a real international presence from over 20 countries here, which is a first for Fit Show. This international representation signals the growing global appeal of the event and attracts exhibitors and visitors from across Europe and beyond.

How many systems houses exhibited at Fit Show 2025 compared to previous editions?

In 2023, Fit Show had two systems houses. Visitors provided feedback that they wanted more systems houses, as it was important to them. In 2025, this has grown to seven systems houses, demonstrating strong market demand and expanded exhibitor diversity.

What is the strategic importance of in-person trade shows despite the availability of digital product information?

There is nothing like being in person and touching, feeling products, and talking to people directly. Many visitors come to put names to faces they talk to online, and this personal connection is irreplaceable for building relationships and making purchasing decisions in the home improvement sector.
