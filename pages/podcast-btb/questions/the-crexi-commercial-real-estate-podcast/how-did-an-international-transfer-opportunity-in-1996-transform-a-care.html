How did an international transfer opportunity in 1996 transform a career in commercial real estate development?

September 3, 2026 The Crexi Commercial Real Estate Podcast · Paul Kurzawa

← See The Crexi Commercial Real Estate Podcast fiche

Paul Kurzawa was selected on his birthday in 1996 from nine candidates in Lendlease's Career Advancement Plan to work with the Armuth Group division in San Francisco, a move that coincided with Lendlease's acquisition of Equitable Real Estate and gave him the opportunity to work on the Fashion Valley redevelopment in San Diego shortly after arriving in the United States.

From Sydney to San Francisco: A Career-Defining Selection

The timing of Kurzawa's transfer proved crucial. He arrived in the United States with minimal resources—just a suitcase, a box of belongings, and $2,000—but the career advancement opportunity positioned him within Lendlease's expanding US operations at a pivotal moment. The company's recent acquisition of Equitable Real Estate created immediate demand for experienced professionals who could navigate the integration and capture emerging development projects.

His assignment to the Armuth Group in San Francisco exposed him to the Fashion Valley redevelopment project in San Diego, one of the first major assignments that would define his trajectory in commercial real estate. This early exposure to mixed-use and retail redevelopment became the foundation for decades of work in transforming properties into dynamic retail destinations. As discussed in the full episode, this period shaped his understanding of how to blend operational strategy with community-centered development.

What started as one opportunity among many candidates in 1996 became a launching pad for a career spanning more than 25 years in lifestyle, open-air, and enclosed retail environments. The career advancement plan that selected Kurzawa on his birthday proved to be a turning point, not just for his personal trajectory but for his ability to influence major retail destinations across the United States.

Kurzawa's arrival in California also marked the beginning of his work with retail titans who would later shape his career path. The Armuth Group assignment connected him directly to the mentors and networks documented in this podcast episode, networks that would prove invaluable as he moved into roles at Westfield and beyond, eventually building a reputation for transforming retail spaces into community destinations.

What early career experiences in retail marketing shaped a professional's approach to commercial real estate?

Paul Kurzawa's first job was in marketing at Penrith Plaza in Western Sydney, where his foundational retail marketing experience included getting retailers to sign off on catalog offers and other hands-on work that shaped his understanding of the retail industry from the ground up.

Key takeaways

  • Selection for Lendlease's Career Advancement Plan in 1996 required competing among nine candidates and positioned Kurzawa within a fast-expanding US division.
  • Lendlease's acquisition of Equitable Real Estate created the immediate demand and opportunities that made Kurzawa's transfer strategically valuable.
  • Early assignment to the Fashion Valley redevelopment in San Diego launched his career in retail and mixed-use property transformation.
  • Arriving with minimal resources forced practical problem-solving skills that became hallmarks of his later leadership in placemaking and community-centered development.
