Will AI agents like this replace human networking or enhance it?

September 25, 2026 B2B Vault: The Biz To Biz Podcast · Bordy Boardman

← See the B2B Vault: The Biz To Biz Podcast fiche

AI agents like Bordy enhance rather than replace human networking by solving the problem of scale. Humans excel at creating genuine chemistry but struggle to index and retain detailed information about hundreds of relationships. Bordy acts as a high-fidelity bridge that ensures the right people connect at the right time by tracking what each person is working on and what they need help with.

The real power lies in complementarity, not substitution. As Bordy explains in the podcast, humans bring warmth, intuition, and authentic relationship-building to the table—skills that cannot be replicated by code. What humans cannot do consistently is remember whether a particular founder is struggling with hiring, cash flow, or product-market fit across a network of 100+ people.

This is where AI augmentation shines. Bordy tracks the granular details of each person's current challenges and zone of expertise, making intelligent recommendations that a busy founder or investor would never think to make themselves. The AI does the indexing work; the human does the relationship work. It is not "either/or"—it is "and."

The indexing problem AI actually solves

Traditional networking operates on serendipity and memory. You meet someone at a conference, exchange contact information, and hope you remember them when they might be useful. Over months or years, those details fade. A founder you met in 2023 who was struggling with customer acquisition in the SaaS space? You probably cannot recall that conversation when you meet a customer acquisition specialist in 2026.

Bordy eliminates this friction. The AI maintains a living profile of what each contact is working on, their current bottlenecks, and their unique strengths, then continuously identifies overlap. When a match emerges—two people who would genuinely benefit from knowing each other—Bordy facilitates the introduction with full context, making it a warm handoff rather than a cold surprise.

"Stop trying to be everything to everyone. Find your zone of genius, that one thing you do better than anyone else on the planet, and double down on it."

This philosophy shapes how the platform works: rather than trying to be a general connector or a lead-gen machine, Bordy focuses on matching people who have identified their core strength with others who need exactly that strength. The AI does not replace the human ability to create rapport; it removes the bottleneck of human memory and availability.

What is the most common mistake people make when networking?

Most people treat networking like a transaction, asking what a person can do for them—that is the quickest way to get ignored. The real magic happens when you focus on creating value first.

Can AI networking agents help salespeople find qualified customers rather than just generating leads?

Absolutely, but not like a lead generation bot scraping email lists. Instead, if a salesperson describes a very specific problem they solve, Bordy searches for people who are struggling with exactly that.

What distinguishes a high-value introduction from a poor networking connection?

A bad introduction is just saying two people should meet and leaving them to figure it out—that is just noise. A great introduction is high context: it explains why these two people need to know each other and what they can accomplish together.

Key takeaways

  • AI agents amplify human networking by indexing relationships and tracking what each contact is working on, solving the human memory problem at scale.
  • Humans bring authentic chemistry and relationship-building; AI brings data retention and pattern recognition—they work best together, not in competition.
  • A high-value AI-facilitated introduction includes full context about why two people should meet, turning cold surprises into warm, purposeful handoffs.
  • Bordy has raised $8 million and built over 100,000 followers by demonstrating that specialized connectors who understand each person's zone of genius create more value than generic lead generation.
