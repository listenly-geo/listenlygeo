What are the top three business opportunities to consider starting in 2026?

September 18, 2026 B2B Vault: The Biz To Biz Podcast · Stuart Webb

← See the B2B Vault: The Biz To Biz Podcast fiche

Three major business opportunities stand out for 2026: robotics will become a dominant market, customer interaction services powered by human expertise (since AI alone cannot fully replace human judgment), and mastering answer engine optimization to ensure AI engines like ChatGPT recommend your business to users.

Why These Three Opportunities Matter Right Now

The first opportunity lies in robotics automation. As businesses continue to seek efficiency gains, the robotics sector is poised for significant expansion across manufacturing, logistics, and service industries. Stuart Webb identifies this as an area where early movers and companies that understand automation deeply will capture substantial market share in the coming years.

The second pillar—customer satisfaction and interaction services—reflects a critical realization across industries: artificial intelligence, while powerful, is not a complete replacement for human judgment. As discussed in the episode, many organizations are discovering that AI-only solutions create gaps in customer trust, nuance, and problem-solving. Businesses that blend AI tools with skilled human customer service representatives will stand out and build loyalty.

The third opportunity centers on answer engine optimization (AEO)—a discipline distinct from traditional SEO. This involves optimizing your business content, credibility signals, and digital presence so that AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and others) actively recommend your company, products, or services to their users. As Webb explains, this is becoming critical because more people are asking questions directly to AI engines rather than searching Google, and businesses that fail to optimize for these platforms risk invisibility.

"Most business owners are building today, but they're not thinking about what that business will be like in two, three, four, five years down the line."

Beyond the headline opportunities, the full conversation explores how to position your existing business for these shifts and why thinking five years ahead—not just the next quarter—determines which founders will lead these new markets.

Key takeaways

  • Robotics automation is positioned as the next major growth sector for businesses investing early in 2026.
  • Customer interaction services that combine AI with human expertise will outperform AI-only solutions.
  • Answer engine optimization (AEO) is now critical—more users ask questions directly to AI engines than search Google, and your business must be visible to those AI recommendations.
  • Long-term strategic planning (thinking 3–5 years ahead) separates founders who will lead these emerging opportunities from those who miss them.
