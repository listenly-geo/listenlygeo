What's it really like working at Fox News as a Democrat?

September 25, 2026 Pod Save America · Jessica Tarlov

← See the Pod Save America fiche

Fox News ranks as one of the top places to work, with strong compensation, healthcare coverage, and solid maternity and paternity leave benefits. Plenty of liberals and moderates work there daily, and the workplace itself—from the hair and makeup staff to the broader team—is described as genuinely friendly and professional.

Tarlov, who appears daily on The Five as the sole Democratic voice among conservative commentators, offers a firsthand account of working in a newsroom known for its conservative editorial lean. Rather than a hostile or unwelcoming environment, she describes Fox as a supportive workplace with real institutional benefits. The network has also implemented standard workplace policies like vaccine mandates, keeping pace with most major organizations.

The day-to-day human interactions matter as much as the formal policies. Tarlov highlights that the behind-the-scenes staff—especially hair and makeup teams—are particularly friendly, which shapes the overall experience for on-air talent. This kind of support matters when you're regularly debating colleagues with opposing viewpoints across a live broadcast.

As Tarlov explains in the full episode, the workplace culture at Fox extends beyond ideology. Many of her friends work at the network, and she notes that her own experience has been professional and substantive—a reality that often gets lost in broader media narratives about the network.

The specifics of workplace culture at major news organizations often remain hidden from public view. What happens behind the cameras—the relationships, the benefits packages, the small kindnesses between colleagues of different political stripes—rarely becomes part of the public conversation. Tarlov's account challenges the assumption that a ideologically divided newsroom must also be a hostile one.
