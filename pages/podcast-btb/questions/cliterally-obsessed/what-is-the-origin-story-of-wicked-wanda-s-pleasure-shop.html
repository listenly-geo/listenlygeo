What is the origin story of Wicked Wanda's pleasure shop?

Wicked Wanda's traces its roots to Madeline Dorian's mother, who started her career as a residential realtor before moving into commercial real estate. After growing frustrated with the hustle culture of that industry — and following a particularly bad experience at an old-school porn store — she partnered with a friend and decided to open their own shop. The first iteration was called Couples Romance Store, deliberately designed around couples and heterosexual clientele. Over time, it evolved into what is now known as Wicked Wanda's.

The pivot from real estate to adult retail wasn't a lateral career move — it was rooted in a desire to do better. The negative experience at an existing porn store made it clear there was a gap in the market for a welcoming, non-shame-based environment. The Couples Romance Store was the answer to that gap, and its eventual transformation into Wicked Wanda's reflected a broader and more inclusive vision for what a community-focused pleasure shop could be.

Madeline Dorian grew up around the business from its early days and joined the team in an official capacity at age 19. She now co-runs Wicked Wanda's alongside her mother, which has since expanded beyond retail into education, events, and community-building.

"I started working in the store in an official capacity and I just fell in love. I fell in love with the customers, the conversations, and then the whole entrepreneurship side as well."

— Madeline Dorian, Cliterally Obsessed
