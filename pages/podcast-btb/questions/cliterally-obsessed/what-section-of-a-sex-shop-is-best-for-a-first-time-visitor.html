Where to Start in a Sex Shop: The Best First Section for Newcomers

20 September 2026 Cliterally Obsessed · Madeline Dorian

← See the Cliterally Obsessed fiche

Start with the body care section, which includes lubricants, massage candles, and erotica — it feels most familiar and approachable. You can also explore the novelty section with communication games and books like Come As You Are, which offer a lighter entry point into the store without pressure.

Walking into a sex shop for the first time can feel overwhelming, but knowing where to begin makes all the difference. The body care aisle is purposefully designed to ease anxiety because these products feel less intimidating than others in the store. Lubricants and massage products exist within familiar wellness language, making them a natural starting point for anyone exploring what the space has to offer.

Beyond body care, the novelty section provides an educational bridge. As Madeline Dorian explains in the episode, communication games and relationship workbooks create a conversational entry point. These products signal that pleasure and intimacy are holistic topics worth exploring together, rather than just transactional purchases. Many visitors find that understanding the "why" behind products — through books and guided games — removes shame and builds genuine curiosity.

"I started working in the store in an official capacity and I just fell in love. I fell in love with the customers, the conversations, and then the whole entrepreneurship side as well."

The strategy behind these two sections reflects a deeper truth: shame decreases when familiarity increases. Products presented within the context of self-care and communication feel less isolating than walking straight to toys without context. Staff trained to observe body language — a hallmark of spaces like Wicked Wanda's — also recognize when a visitor needs subtle guidance or space to browse independently. Discover more about how staff create welcoming environments for nervous shoppers in the full episode.

One often overlooked benefit of starting in these sections is permission. By browsing body care and novelty items first, you give yourself time to acclimate to the store's energy and aesthetics. This unhurried approach transforms a visit from anxiety-driven to exploratory — exactly the mindset that leads to informed, pleasurable purchases rather than impulse buys driven by discomfort.

Building Confidence Through Incremental Exploration

First-time visitors sometimes expect to find everything they need in one section, but intimate wellness shops are designed with a journey in mind. Starting in body care and novelty isn't just about comfort — it's a scaffolding system that helps you learn your own preferences. Conversations with staff or insights from books reveal what categories actually interest you before you venture deeper into the shop.

A curious detail from the broader conversation on this episode involves how product quality and marketing have evolved significantly over the past 15 to 20 years, making modern shops far more curated and customer-conscious than they were decades ago. This evolution means the stores you walk into today are actively designed with first-time visitors in mind.

Key takeaways

  • Body care (lubricants, massage candles, erotica) feels most familiar and is the recommended starting point for first-time visitors.
  • Novelty items like communication games and books offer a lighter, educational entry into the store experience.
  • Incremental exploration through these sections builds confidence and helps you clarify your own preferences before moving deeper into the shop.
  • Taking time to browse without pressure transforms anxiety into genuine curiosity and empowered decision-making.
