Why should you avoid silicone lubricant with silicone sex toys?

September 16, 2026 Cliterally Obsessed · Madeline Dorian

← See the Cliterally Obsessed fiche

Silicone lubricant and silicone toys are fundamentally incompatible: liquid silicone breaks down the solid silicone material of the toy, causing them to degrade each other. Instead, use water-based lubricant or a quality toy cleaner, and reserve silicone lube for non-silicone toys or body application only.

The problem lies in the chemistry itself. When you apply silicone-based lubricant to a silicone toy, the two materials interact in ways that compromise the toy's structural integrity and safety. As Madeline Dorian explains in the episode, this incompatibility is one of the most common mistakes people make when choosing lubricants.

The key to avoiding this damage is understanding which lubricants work with which materials. Water-based lubricants are your safest choice for silicone toys—they're compatible with all toy materials and pose no risk of degradation. If you prefer silicone-based products for their longer-lasting glide, reserve them exclusively for non-silicone toys or for use directly on your body.

Beyond lubricant selection, maintenance matters too. A good quality toy cleaner should be part of your routine after use, as detailed further in this podcast discussion. This ensures your toys remain safe and effective over time.

For those looking deeper into lubricant choices, Madeline covers how to select products based on your toy material—a conversation that touches on water-based formulations that should be glycerin-free and paraben-free, alongside hybrid lubricants that offer different benefits.
