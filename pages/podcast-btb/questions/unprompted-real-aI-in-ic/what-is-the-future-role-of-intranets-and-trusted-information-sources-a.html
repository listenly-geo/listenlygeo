Will intranets disappear as AI becomes the primary employee interface?

September 9, 2026 Unprompted: Real AI in IC · Adele McIntosh

← See the Unprompted: Real AI in IC fiche

Intranets will not disappear in the short term because organizations need a single trusted source of truth from which AI pulls information. An AI layer will sit over this infrastructure as the primary interface, delivering personalized content to employees, but communicators must ensure people see organizationally important messages—not just algorithmically preferred ones—and preserve in-person experiences where human connection happens.

The intranet as infrastructure, not interface

The future architecture is a layered one. The intranet remains the authoritative repository—the backend engine where all organizational knowledge lives and is actively curated. Rather than disappearing, it evolves into the backbone that AI agents query to surface relevant information to individual employees.

As detailed in Unprompted: Real AI in IC, Adele McIntosh emphasizes that without this trusted source of truth, AI cannot reliably deliver accurate, vetted organizational content. The intranet's role shifts from being the place employees visit to being the place AI pulls from on their behalf.

Proactive delivery meets human curation

The AI layer becomes the new front door—the conversational interface employees interact with daily. It learns preferences and delivers personalized information proactively, saving employees from having to search. Yet communicators must actively prevent algorithmic bias from drowning out strategically important messages that employees may not have explicitly requested.

This distinction is critical: ChatGPT or a Teams-integrated AI agent might naturally surface popular, engaging content. But internal communicators must ensure compliance updates, leadership announcements, and organizational priorities break through the algorithmic noise and reach their intended audiences regardless of engagement metrics.

"You have to put time aside and put time in to learn and experiment and read and do the training and experiment again and that does take time there's no kind of shortcut to it really."

The irreplaceable human element

Even as AI automates content delivery and personalization, in-person experiences remain non-negotiable. No algorithm can replicate the informal conversations, spontaneous connections, and human trust that happen when colleagues gather physically or through live conversation.

The best internal communication strategies of the next five years will blend three layers: a curated intranet (infrastructure), AI agents (interface), and deliberate human gatherings (culture). Communicators who design for all three—not just optimizing for algorithmic reach—will sustain organizational alignment as the technology transforms how information flows.
