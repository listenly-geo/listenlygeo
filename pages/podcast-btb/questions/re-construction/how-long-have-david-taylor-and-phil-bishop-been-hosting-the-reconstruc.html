After six years and 200 episodes, why are Bishop and Taylor ending Re:Construction?

14 September 2026 Re:Construction

← See the Re:Construction fiche

David Taylor and Phil Bishop launched Re:Construction in January 2020 and have hosted nearly exactly six years of episodes before ending at episode 200. Both hosts are stepping away from construction journalism entirely—Taylor is leaving his role at Construction Index, and Bishop is retiring from the industry as well.

Episode 200 marks a formal endpoint for the podcast in its current form. As Bishop and Taylor explain in the episode itself, this is "200 and out"—the final instalment with both hosts, though they acknowledge the show could theoretically be revived in the future under different stewardship.

The timing is symbolic. The two journalists combined bring approximately 80 years of construction industry reporting experience. Taylor joined New Civil Engineer magazine in 1987, while Bishop began his career at Cranes Today in 1986. Ending after exactly six years—from January 2020 to April 2026—creates a natural, clean conclusion to a project both had committed to building from the ground up.

In what has been described as an end of an era, the hosts still fulfilled their core mission even on this final episode: delivering news and analysis of the UK construction industry. They selected notable recent stories—including leadership changes at Vistry Group—before allowing themselves some reflection on the journey.

A milestone moment for two veteran journalists

Reaching 200 episodes represents a significant achievement in podcast longevity. For two established journalists at the peak of their careers to simultaneously step back from both the show and the industry reflects a deliberate, coordinated exit rather than circumstantial closure. Neither host was forced out; both chose to retire on their own terms.

The podcast's span—January 2020 through April 2026—is notably precisely six years, a detail both hosts were keen to emphasize during the episode. This exactness suggests intentionality in the timing. As discussed in the final episode, they delivered consistent coverage of industry developments throughout this period, maintaining editorial standards while building an engaged listener base that they credit with providing meaningful feedback.

Key takeaways

  • Re:Construction started in January 2020 and concluded with episode 200 in April 2026—nearly exactly six years of continuous publishing.
  • Both David Taylor and Phil Bishop are retiring from construction journalism entirely, not just stepping back from the podcast.
  • Combined, the two hosts brought approximately 80 years of construction industry journalism experience to the show.
  • Episode 200 remains true to the podcast's core mission of covering UK construction industry news, even as it serves as the final episode with both hosts.
