What makes Benalmádena's train connection unique on the Costa del Sol?

7 September 2026 The Costa Property Podcast · Warner L.

← See The Costa Property Podcast

Benalmádena is one of the few coastal towns where the train line continues past Fuengirola instead of ending there, connecting residents directly to Málaga City Centre in just 15 minutes without requiring a car. This rail access remains a major draw for the area, offering unmatched connectivity among nearby coastal destinations.

Most towns along the Costa del Sol experience a transportation bottleneck—Fuengirola marks the end of the line for many commuters, forcing them to rely on buses or cars for further travel. Benalmádena breaks this pattern by extending rail connectivity further along the coast. This advantage translates into real daily convenience, as explained in The Costa Property Podcast.

Direct access to culture and lifestyle without a car

The 15-minute train journey to Málaga City Centre opens up an entire ecosystem of restaurants, cultural venues, and nightlife that many coastal residents simply cannot access easily. This proximity redefines what living on the coast actually means—it's no longer isolation from urban amenities, but rather a choice to live by the sea while maintaining direct access to city energy and opportunity.

What distinguishes this advantage is its practical permanence. Unlike seasonal transportation improvements, the train connection is a structural feature that shapes daily life year-round. Residents use it for work commutes, evening entertainment, and weekend cultural activities. From the airport perspective, the numbers are equally compelling: approximately 30 minutes by train from Málaga airport to Arroyo de la Miel station, meaning the total journey from landing to settling at home can take around one hour—a seamless transition that appeals strongly to part-time residents and investors.

"It's that perfect balance that each day brings in terms of, we still have that energy about it where this is our life, we go to work, we have hobbies, we've normal life with the kids, but it's also more relaxed than that life that we were used to experiencing before we made the decision to move to Spain."

Beyond the headline benefit, the train infrastructure supports a specific lifestyle choice that's rare on the Costa del Sol. Families can maintain employment or business interests in Málaga while raising children in a more relaxed coastal environment. Part-time residents can arrive at the airport and be settled within an hour. Investors see a town with genuine commuting potential, not just vacation appeal. As Warner L. explores in his detailed breakdown of why Benalmádena stands out, this transportation advantage is only one layer of what makes the town genuinely different.

What cultural events and community experiences are available to residents in Benalmádena Pueblo?

Benalmádena Pueblo hosts the Corpus Christi festival in May, where locals come together and paint all the cobbled streets in fresh petals, creating a vibrant community experience unique to the area.

Who is Benalmádena most suitable for and why is it considered underrated compared to other Costa del Sol destinations?

Benalmádena is perfect for people who want to be close to Málaga with fantastic amenities but don't want to pay Marbella prices, making it an excellent choice for those seeking value and lifestyle balance.

How does the train connection make Benalmádena particularly attractive for part-time residents or investors?

Benalmádena offers direct train access from the airport in Málaga, with approximately 30 minutes by train from Málaga airport to Arroyo de la Miel station, enabling seamless connectivity for part-time residents and investors.
