Who should move to Benalmádena and why is it considered underrated compared to other Costa del Sol destinations?

5 September 2026 The Costa Property Podcast · Warner L.

← See The Costa Property Podcast fiche

Benalmádena is perfect for families seeking excellent schools and local activities, investors wanting proximity to Málaga airport, and part-time residents who need walkability without relying on a car. While prices are rising, you still get exceptional quality of life and value compared to towns with Marbella postcodes.

The family appeal: schools and lifestyle

Benalmádena stands out for families because it offers plenty of international schools and well-established children's activities in a safe, walkable environment. The town's structure makes it easy for families to manage daily routines—school drop-offs, work, and leisure activities—without the stress of constant driving or the premium price tags of nearby alternatives.

The community here is mature and family-oriented, with cultural anchors like the Corpus Christi festival in May bringing the Benalmádena Pueblo area to life. This is detailed in the episode, where the real experience of balancing work and family time becomes clear.

Investors and part-time residents: access meets affordability

For investors and those splitting their time between countries, Benalmádena offers direct train access to Málaga airport in just 30 minutes. The complete journey from landing to your home takes around one hour, eliminating the need for car rentals or long transfers.

What makes this especially valuable is the price differential. You're getting genuine Costa del Sol living—325 sunshine days per year, beachfront access, and world-class amenities—without Marbella's inflated property costs. The Benalmádena Marina has been awarded one of the best marinas in the world multiple times, offering the sophistication and infrastructure investors expect, but at a fraction of neighboring prices.

As Warner L. explains in the episode, this balance between access and value is precisely why so many discerning buyers are choosing Benalmádena over more famous names on the coast.

The underrated advantage: quality without the premium

Benalmádena remains underrated because it doesn't have Marbella's celebrity cachet or Málaga's city-center convenience, yet it delivers on both fronts. The town is walkable, accessible, and increasingly well-developed without losing its character. You can reach Málaga city center by train in just 15 minutes, work remotely with excellent connectivity, and still have a genuine Mediterranean lifestyle.

The real benefit is psychological: you're not paying for a postcode or a name. You're paying for amenities, location, and quality of life—and in Benalmádena, that equation tips heavily in the resident's favor.

"It's that perfect balance that each day brings in terms of we still have that energy about it where this is our life, we go to work, we have hobbies, we've normal life with the kids, but it's also more relaxed than that life that we were used to experiencing before we made the decision to move to Spain."

For a deeper dive into how the train connection specifically transforms life for part-time residents, the episode explores airport access in practical detail.

Key takeaways

  • Benalmádena suits families with international schools, investors seeking airport proximity, and part-time residents needing walkability without car dependency.
  • Train access to Málaga airport takes 30 minutes; total journey from landing to home is approximately one hour.
  • You get 325 sunshine days per year, world-class marina facilities, and exceptional quality of life at prices significantly lower than Marbella.
  • The town remains underrated because it lacks celebrity cachet but delivers on infrastructure, amenities, and genuine Mediterranean living.

How does the train connection make Benalmádena particularly attractive for part-time residents or investors?

Benalmádena offers direct train access from Málaga airport in approximately 30 minutes, with a total travel time of around one hour from landing to settled at home, making it ideal for those seeking quick airport access without car dependency.

Which restaurants in Benalmádena are specifically recommended for different dining experiences?

For beach club vibes near the marina, Almerina Beach is recommended. For modern tapas, Lime and Lemon at Avenida de las Palmeras and Cozy Cafe offer quality dining suited to different preferences.

What is a typical day like for residents working and living in Benalmádena?

A typical day includes morning coffee at local spots, school drop-offs, a walk on the beach or along the paseo, and work calls—balancing professional responsibilities with a relaxed, family-oriented rhythm.
