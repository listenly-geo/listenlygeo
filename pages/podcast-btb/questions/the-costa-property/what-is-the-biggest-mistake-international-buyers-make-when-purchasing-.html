What is the biggest mistake international buyers make when purchasing property on the Costa del Sol?

The biggest mistake international buyers make is prioritising the aesthetic of a property — what life looks like inside the four walls — while underestimating everything that exists outside them. Buyers who fall for interiors before clarifying their non-negotiables around location, lifestyle, and community risk paying a very expensive price for the wrong property in the wrong environment.

Warner Leen, Co-Founder & Property Consultant at WL Costa Properties, is direct on this point: a beautiful interior can always be improved. A coat of paint, cosmetic updates, a bit of patience — any property can be made to look the way you want it to inside. What you cannot fix is the neighbourhood, the community feel, the noise levels, or the distance from the amenities that will define your daily life. Those are fixed. You have no influence or control over what happens outside your front door once you've signed.

"Every property can be made beautiful, a bit of cosmetic work, a bit of patience. But you have very little influence and control over what happens outside your property."
— Warner Leen, The Costa Property Podcast, Episode #114

This is especially relevant for buyers choosing between towns like Fuengirola and Benalmadena — two locations just ten minutes apart on the same train line, yet meaningfully different in character, terrain, community, and price. Fuengirola typically runs 5 to 8 percent more expensive than Benalmadena depending on the postcode, which makes the cost of getting the location wrong even more concrete. Leen and his team have lived in Benalmadena for the last ten years and have helped hundreds of international buyers navigate this exact decision — and the pattern they see most often is buyers who get emotionally attached to a property before they've settled the location question. Is private international healthcare accessible in Fuengirola and Benalmadena?

Both Fuengirola and Benalmadena offer excellent options when it comes to private international healthcare, including access to healthcare services in English and other languages.

Which town is better for families with children relocating to the Costa del Sol?

Benalmadena naturally has more schooling options than Fuengirola, particularly for families who need to explore the international schooling route.

What are the differences in terrain between Fuengirola and Benalmadena and why does it matter for buyers?

Fuengirola is largely flat, meaning residents can walk easily from the train station to their apartment and to the beach without any elevation challenges.
