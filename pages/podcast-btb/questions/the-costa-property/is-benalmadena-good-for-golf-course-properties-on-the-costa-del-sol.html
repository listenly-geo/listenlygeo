Is Benalmadena the right choice if you're looking for a golf course property on the Costa del Sol?

Yes — Benalmadena is the clear winner for buyers seeking a townhouse on a golf course on the Costa del Sol. This type of property is readily and easily available there, making it the natural destination for anyone with golf-front living as a priority. Fuengirola, by contrast, is largely flat and more urban in its centre, and simply does not offer the same golf course residential options.

The distinction matters more than it might first appear. When comparing two locations just ten minutes apart on the same train line, the differences in terrain and urban character directly shape what property types are available. Benalmadena's hillier topography creates the conditions for golf course developments to exist and thrive — and that shows up in the inventory available to buyers. If a golf course setting is non-negotiable for you, focusing your search on Fuengirola would mean limiting your options significantly.

Warner Leen and the team at WL Costa Properties have spent a decade living and working in Benalmadena, giving them direct knowledge of the day-to-day reality of each location — not just the listings. That ground-level familiarity is exactly the kind of context that shapes smart buying decisions. Should you try before you buy if you want a rural or village lifestyle in Spain?

Yes — the advice given in the podcast is to rent via Airbnb for a month or six weeks, and to do this multiple times at different times of year, including during quieter, off-season periods, before committing to a purchase.

What happened to buyers Roger and Stephanie who chose a remote rural property in Spain?

Roger and Stephanie, a mature couple who moved to Spain permanently to retire, chose a rustic country village property seeking an idyllic rural lifestyle. The reality of remote living proved far more challenging than expected, and their experience serves as a cautionary tale for buyers romanticising rural life on the Costa del Sol.

What is the biggest mistake international buyers make when purchasing property on the Costa del Sol?

The biggest mistake buyers make is prioritizing the aesthetic of the property and what life looks like inside the four walls, while underestimating the importance of the surrounding location, neighbourhood, and external environment.
