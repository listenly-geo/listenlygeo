Is Fuengirola more expensive than Benalmadena to buy property on the Costa del Sol?

Yes — Fuengirola is typically five to eight percent more expensive than Benalmadena, depending on the postcode. The gap is real but not dramatic: it is far smaller than the price difference you would see comparing either town to Marbella or Estepona. Fuengirola commands this premium primarily because of its flat terrain, which translates directly into better walkability and easier access on foot from the train station to the beach.

That flat layout is Fuengirola's clearest physical advantage. In Benalmadena, depending on exactly where you buy, terrain can be hillier — which affects how easily residents and renters can move between the station, the beach, and daily amenities on foot. For buyers who place walkability at the top of their checklist, the Fuengirola premium often feels justified. For those who prioritise budget efficiency or prefer a hillside lifestyle, Benalmadena remains highly competitive — and is only ten minutes further back along the same train line toward Malaga Airport.

Warner Leen and the team at WL Costa Properties have been based in Benalmadena for over ten years, advising international buyers across both towns. In episode #114 of The Costa Property Podcast, Leen unpacks exactly how to weigh this price gap against your own priorities — because the right answer depends heavily on whether you are buying a holiday home, a permanent residence, or an investment property.
