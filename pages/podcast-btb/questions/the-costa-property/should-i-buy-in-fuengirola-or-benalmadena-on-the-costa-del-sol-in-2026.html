Should I buy in Fuengirola or Benalmadena on the Costa del Sol in 2026?

The right choice between Fuengirola and Benalmadena comes down to five questions about how you plan to live there — not just where each town sits on the map. Both are on the same train line, only 10 minutes apart, and they are generating more interest from international buyers right now than anywhere else on the Costa del Sol. Answer the five questions honestly, and the decision makes itself.

  • 1 Holiday home or permanent residence? The way you use the property changes everything — from the type of community you need to how much you'll rely on local infrastructure year-round.
  • 2 Walk everywhere or drive? Your tolerance for being car-dependent is a genuine differentiator between these two towns. One rewards walkers more than the other.
  • 3 Who is this property really for? Whether it's for you alone, your family, or future rental guests shapes which town's character fits your needs.
  • 4 What does your budget actually buy in each town? Fuengirola is typically 5 to 8 percent more expensive than Benalmadena depending on the postcode — a gap that matters when you're comparing like-for-like properties.
  • 5 What do you want within a five-minute walk? Beach, restaurants, a pharmacy, a train station — define your non-negotiables first, then match them to the town.

Warner Leen and his team at WL Costa Properties have lived in Benalmadena for the last 10 years and have helped hundreds of international buyers work through exactly this choice. Their approach cuts through the usual noise: rather than ranking one town above the other, they use these five questions to surface the answer that is already there in your own priorities. The location quiz they reference takes 30 seconds to complete and is free — it is built on the same logic.

What makes the comparison genuinely useful is that neither town is a compromise. The fact that they share a rail connection means whichever you choose, the other is 10 minutes away. The real decision is about what is immediately outside your front door — and the full episode goes deep into what that looks like in practice for both towns.
