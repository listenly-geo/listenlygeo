How does the train connection make Benalmádena particularly attractive for part-time residents and investors?

4 September 2026 The Costa Property Podcast · Warner L.

← See The Costa Property Podcast fiche

Benalmádena offers direct train access from Málaga airport to Arroyo de la Miel in just 30 minutes at very low cost, eliminating the need for car rental entirely. From the station, you can walk to your apartment or take a short taxi ride, allowing you to be settled on your terrace within an hour of landing—a dramatic advantage for part-time residents who want minimal hassle and maximum relaxation time.

Why speed and convenience matter for the part-time investor

For someone splitting time between countries, airport-to-home efficiency is everything. The traditional Costa del Sol experience—renting a car and driving for hours through Marbella or Málaga—drains energy before you've even arrived at your property. As discussed in this episode of The Costa Property Podcast, Benalmádena's train infrastructure flips that equation entirely.

You land at Málaga airport, walk to the train platform, and 15 minutes later you're in Arroyo de la Miel station—the central hub of Benalmádena. No rental counter queues, no navigation stress, no unexpected toll roads. The cost is negligible compared to a full day's car hire, and you reclaim that mental energy for what actually matters: your time off.

The walking city that starts the moment you arrive

Most Costa del Sol towns force dependency on a vehicle. Benalmádena's train-centered design changes that entirely. Walking distance from Arroyo de la Miel station puts you in the heart of residential neighborhoods and the marina district, which is where most property investors are concentrating their purchases.

For part-time residents juggling logistics, this means you can check into your apartment without the friction of driving unfamiliar roads or needing to arrange transportation. The short taxi ride—or even a walk if your property is close enough—keeps the arrival ritual simple and inviting, not exhausting.

"It's that perfect balance that each day brings in terms of, we still have that energy about it where this is our life, we go to work, we have hobbies, we've normal life with the kids, but it's also more relaxed than that life that we were used to experiencing before we made the decision to move to Spain."

The deeper benefit lies in what Warner Leen calls "energy"—the mental shift from travel mode to relaxation or work mode without the exhaustion penalty. For investors managing multiple properties or remote workers splitting time between continents, that psychological reset within an hour of landing is a genuine competitive advantage. Benalmádena's train access delivers it reliably.

What are the three distinct zones within Benalmádena and what does each offer to residents?

Benalmádena is split into three parts: Benalmádena Costa with the beautiful marina awarded one of the best marinas in the world multiple times; Arroyo de la Miel, the central transport hub; and Benalmádena Pueblo, the traditional hilltop village offering heritage and cultural depth.

What is a typical day like for residents working and living in Benalmádena?

A normal day includes morning coffee, school drop-off, a walk on the beach or along the paseo to clear heads, and then client calls. The lifestyle offers a balance between productivity and Mediterranean relaxation, with work fitting seamlessly into a slower rhythm.

Which restaurants in Benalmádena are specifically recommended for different dining experiences?

For beach club vibes near the marina, Almerina Beach is recommended. For modern tapas, Lime and Lemon at Avenida de las Palmeras and Cozy Cafe offer accessible, high-quality dining in central locations.

Key takeaways

  • The 30-minute train from Málaga airport to Arroyo de la Miel is cheap and eliminates car rental friction entirely.
  • Walking distance or a short taxi ride from the station gets you home within an hour of landing, preserving energy and mental clarity.
  • For part-time residents and investors, this transport advantage removes a major logistical barrier compared to other Costa del Sol towns.
  • Train-centered access fits naturally into Benalmádena's walkable neighborhoods, especially around the marina and central Arroyo de la Miel district.
