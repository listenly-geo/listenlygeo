What are the three main obligations for e-commerce merchants placing packaging on the German market?

September 12, 2026 Just-in-Time | Your E-Commerce Logistics Podcast · Lena

← See the Just-in-Time | Your E-Commerce Logistics Podcast fiche

German packaging compliance rests on three legally binding steps: first, completing system participation through a dual system via Licenseiro.de, which ensures your packaging is tracked through collection and recycling; second, registering in Lucid (the Central Packaging Register) to receive your official EPR number; and third, submitting annual data reports of your planned packaging volumes for the following year and confirmed actual volumes after each year closes.

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)

EPR is the legal framework requiring distributors of packaging to private end consumers to take full responsibility for that packaging throughout its entire lifecycle—from production through collection, sorting, and recycling. This principle underpins all three German packaging obligations.

System Participation: Your Entry Point

The first obligation begins with licensing. Every e-commerce merchant sending packaged goods to German consumers must participate in a dual system—essentially a collective responsibility scheme managed by licensed operators. Licenseiro.de is the online platform where you register and complete this participation, connecting your business to the broader recycling infrastructure.

Without active system participation, you cannot legally distribute packaged products into Germany. This is not optional for any quantity of packaging.

Lucid Registration and Your EPR Number

The second obligation requires you to register in Lucid, Germany's official Central Packaging Register overseen by the packaging act control authority. Registration generates your unique EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) number—a critical identifier for all subsequent compliance activities.

As Lena explains in the episode, this number tracks your business in the regulatory system and must accompany all packaging data submissions. Without it, your compliance record remains incomplete.

Data Reporting: Forecasting and Actual Volumes

The third obligation is ongoing data reporting. You must submit two distinct reports annually: planned packaging volumes at year-end for the following calendar year, and confirmed actual volumes after that year has closed.

This dual-reporting structure allows German authorities to forecast waste management capacity while verifying your actual packaging placement. Inaccurate or missing reports can trigger penalties and regulatory action.

"Distributors are not only responsible for the actual product itself but also for the packaging and product components that they put into circulation, and that for the entire life cycle, right through the whole recycling process."

The practical reality is simpler than it sounds: as discussed in this podcast, many e-commerce platforms now integrate these three steps into a single workflow, automating much of the administrative burden once you understand the framework.

Regulations differ significantly country by country. In France, licensing is mandatory from the first kilogram of packaging with no quantity thresholds. Understanding these regional variations is essential if you ship across multiple European markets.

What is Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) and how does it apply to e-commerce packaging?

Extended Producer Responsibility stipulates that producers are responsible for their product till the end of its life cycle, meaning distributors are accountable for packaging throughout its entire journey from sale to recycling.

During Black Friday and Cyber Monday, merchants can communicate longer delivery times transparently since customers expect delays due to volume and discount offers. Clear communication prevents post-purchase frustration and returns.

Key takeaways

  • System participation via Licenseiro.de is your mandatory first step into German packaging compliance.
  • Lucid registration provides your official EPR number, the identifier needed for all regulatory tracking.
  • Annual data reporting requires both forward-looking forecasts and backward-looking confirmations of actual volumes placed on the market.
