What is the open rate for post-purchase tracking notifications compared to regular marketing emails?

September 8, 2026 Just-in-Time | Your E-Commerce Logistics Podcast · Arne

← See the Just-in-Time | Your E-Commerce Logistics Podcast fiche

Post-purchase tracking notifications achieve 80% open rates and 40% click-through rates, vastly outperforming regular marketing emails. This makes tracking communications one of the most effective channels for customer engagement and strategic upselling in e-commerce.

The performance gap between tracking notifications and traditional newsletters is striking. Customers actively expect and open tracking messages because they contain time-sensitive, personally relevant information about their purchase—something a standard promotional email cannot match. As Arne explains in the podcast episode, this difference fundamentally changes how brands should think about the post-purchase window.

Why tracking notifications outperform traditional email

The high engagement rates stem from a simple psychological reality: customers are actively monitoring parcel status after they purchase. A tracking notification answers a question they're already asking themselves, rather than attempting to interrupt them with an unsolicited offer. This urgency and relevance drive the 40% click-through rate, compared to typical promotional email performance which rarely exceeds single-digit percentages.

The window between checkout and delivery is also unique because customer attention is at its peak. They've just spent money and are emotionally invested in the outcome. This heightened engagement window presents an opportunity discussed in detail in the full episode—allowing brands to communicate product care, upsell related items, or gather feedback with far higher receptiveness than they'd receive weeks later.

"Within every 48 hours, customers should hear, kind of get a live sign of the parcel, if the transit takes longer, we would suggest having an interim milestone communication."

Arne's approach reflects a broader insight: the frequency and timing of tracking communications matter as much as the channel itself. The episode explores specific communication strategies for different delivery scenarios, such as when transit delays occur or exceptions require customer attention—moving beyond a single "shipped" notification into a more sophisticated dialogue.

Strategic value beyond logistics

These high engagement rates reveal something critical for e-commerce strategy: post-purchase notifications are no longer just logistical messages. They represent a prime channel for brand communication and customer loyalty. When 80% of customers are opening these emails and 40% are clicking through, merchants have an obligation to use that attention wisely—whether for customer education, feedback collection, or carefully timed upselling.

The contrast with regular newsletters—where open rates typically hover between 15–25% and click rates between 1–5%—underscores that this window of attention cannot be replicated later. Brands that treat post-purchase communications as a commodity miss a measurable competitive advantage in customer retention and lifetime value.
