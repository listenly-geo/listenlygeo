What is Mitch Matthews' core personal mission — the 'because' that drives everything he does?

Mitch Matthews' foundational "because" is built on a clear priority order: love God, love his family, and love others. Beyond that shared foundation, he has identified a specific personal purpose — helping people figure out why they are on the planet — and a distinct zone of genius: encouraging encouragers, which he describes as the space where he becomes an unstoppable force.

Host Mick Hunt asked Matthews directly about his deeper "because" — the true engine behind his work with high-capacity humans — during their conversation on Mick Unplugged. Matthews didn't hesitate. The three priorities he named aren't aspirational language; they function as an actual operating system — a ranked list he returns to when decisions get hard or when the pull of comparison threatens to knock him off course.

That last point matters. The episode opens with Matthews in a green room surrounded by people he admired, fighting a wave of impostor syndrome. His way out wasn't bravado — it was a quiet moment of reorientation back to his foundational principles. The phrase that came to him in that moment captures the spirit of his entire mission:

"Learn from, don't compare to."

— Mitch Matthews, on Mick Unplugged

This isn't a motivational slogan. It's the practical expression of a mission rooted in serving others rather than measuring himself against them. For Matthews, "encouraging encouragers" — coaches, speakers, leaders who already lift others — is the precise intersection where purpose and natural ability meet. He has spent over 25 years working in exactly that zone, building tools like the Authority Bridge program specifically to help those people amplify their impact without dismantling what they've already built.

About Mitch Matthews

MM
Mitch Matthews Coach, Speaker, and Podcast Host MitchMatthews.com · Dream Think Do

Matthews began his professional life in pharmaceutical sales — a solid career on paper, until a promotion placed him in a role that simply didn't fit. Rather than grind through it, he used that friction as a launchpad, building a coaching and speaking practice on the side. Within five months of going full-time, his new business had already surpassed the income from his pharmaceutical salary. That wasn't luck; it was the result of doing work that aligned tightly with his stated purpose.

Over the past 25 years, Matthews has coached executives, entrepreneurs, and organizational leaders, and has worked with thousands of coaches and speakers specifically. His program, the Authority Bridge, was designed for high-capacity professionals who want to add coaching or speaking to their portfolio without walking away from a career they've spent years building. He also hosts the Dream Think Do podcast, where his conversation with Mick Hunt was one of the standout episodes — prompting significant audience response and a return invitation.

His credibility on the question of personal mission isn't theoretical. The "because" he articulated on Mick Unplugged is the same framework he has applied to his own 25-year career — and the same lens through which he helps clients cut through noise, get clear on purpose, and build plans that actually hold.
