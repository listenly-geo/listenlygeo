How did Mitch Matthews discover that coaching was his calling?

Mitch Matthews discovered coaching through his wife. After being promoted into a role that was a poor fit for him in the pharmaceutical industry, she placed a magazine article in front of him — one that featured a sidebar about an entrepreneur's coach. She told him directly that coaching was exactly what he had always done: helping people get clear, get confident, and take action. Until that moment, Matthews had no idea coaching was a legitimate paid profession.

That conversation was a genuine turning point. Matthews had been doing the work of a coach for years without a name for it, and without knowing it could be a career. His wife's observation — and the article that sparked it — gave him both permission and a framework to pursue it professionally. He began building a coaching and speaking practice on the side while still employed, and within five months of going full-time, his income from coaching and speaking surpassed what he had been earning in pharmaceuticals.

That origin story shapes how Matthews talks about purpose and vocation today. He regularly helps high-capacity professionals recognize that what they already do naturally — often without being paid for it — can become the foundation of a viable coaching or speaking business. His program, the Authority Bridge, is a direct extension of that insight. About Mitch Matthews

MM
Mitch Matthews Coach, Speaker, and Podcast Host MitchMatthews.com / Dream Think Do

Matthews began his career in pharmaceutical sales — a field that offered stability but ultimately led him into a promotion that was a clear mismatch for his natural strengths. That friction is what set everything in motion: rather than stay in a role that didn't fit, he started coaching and speaking on the side and built a business that overtook his pharmaceutical salary within five months of going full-time.

Over the past 25 years, Matthews has worked with executives, entrepreneurs, and organizational leaders across a wide range of industries. He has also coached thousands of coaches and speakers, making him one of the more credible voices specifically on the question of how to turn a natural talent for helping others into a sustainable profession.

His podcast, Dream Think Do, is a direct outgrowth of that mission — a platform where he applies the same clarity-first, action-oriented approach he uses in his coaching practice. His program, the Authority Bridge, is designed for high-capacity professionals who want to add coaching or speaking as a revenue stream without dismantling their existing careers. It is, in many ways, the formalized version of the path he himself walked when his wife handed him that magazine article.
