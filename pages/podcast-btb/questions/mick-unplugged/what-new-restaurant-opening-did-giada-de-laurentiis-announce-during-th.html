What new restaurant opening did Giada De Laurentiis announce during this episode?

29 Aug 2026 Mick Unplugged · Giada De Laurentiis

← See the Mick Unplugged fiche

Giada De Laurentiis announced she was preparing to open a new restaurant in Aurora, Illinois—a town in the Chicagoland area outside Chicago—within the week of the recording. After working on and building this project for over a year, she planned to sell Giadzy products inside the restaurant, integrating her various businesses under one roof.

A year of planning culminates in Aurora

The restaurant represents a natural evolution of De Laurentiis's business model. Rather than keeping her food brand, media presence, and culinary vision separate, she saw an opportunity to create a unified space where customers could experience her philosophy firsthand.

As discussed in the episode, the Aurora location was strategic—placing the restaurant in a growing suburban market while maintaining proximity to her existing customer base. The integration of Giadzy products meant that diners could purchase the exact ingredients, pastas, and oils they'd just experienced in the kitchen, blurring the line between dining and retail.

This wasn't a rushed venture. De Laurentiis emphasized that she had invested significant time developing every detail of the project, ensuring it reflected her standards and vision for how food should be experienced—not just consumed.

"If we can be laughing and having fun and enjoying the people that we're around while we're eating, we will feel better. That piece of the longevity piece is just as important as the food you're putting on the table."

This philosophy of joy and connection—central to her earlier conversation with host Mick Hunt—extended naturally into the restaurant concept. The Aurora opening wasn't simply another business expansion; it was a physical manifestation of how she believes food should bring people together, a principle detailed further in Mick Unplugged.

What does Giada De Laurentiis say about the role of joy and social connection in food and longevity?

Giada argues that "la dolce vita"—the enjoyment of life and who you eat with—is a genuine nutritional ingredient, because the body's nervous system and digestive health are directly affected by the emotional state during eating.

What became the number one product on Giadzy and why?

Gluten-free pasta became the number one product on Giadzy, which surprised Giada since she is not gluten-free herself. The demand was driven by followers requesting the specific products she uses.

What inspired Giada De Laurentiis to launch Giadzy and how did the business evolve?

Giadzy began because fans of her Food Network shows kept asking what specific brands of olive oil and pasta she was using, but on-set rules prohibited naming them. She launched the brand during COVID as a blog before expanding into a full e-commerce marketplace and retail distribution.

Key takeaways

  • De Laurentiis spent over a year developing the Aurora, Illinois restaurant project before its imminent opening.
  • The restaurant integrates Giadzy products directly into the dining experience, allowing customers to purchase ingredients they've just eaten.
  • The location in Chicagoland's Aurora suburbs positions the restaurant to reach her established customer base while expanding into a growing market.
  • The venture reflects her philosophy that joy, connection, and the social experience of eating are inseparable from the food itself.
