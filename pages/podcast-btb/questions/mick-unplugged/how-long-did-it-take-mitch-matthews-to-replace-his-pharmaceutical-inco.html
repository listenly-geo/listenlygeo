How long did it take Mitch Matthews to replace his pharmaceutical income with his coaching and speaking business?

Mitch Matthews built his coaching and speaking business on the side of his pharmaceutical job, then launched it full-time — surpassing his previous salary in just five months. That transition happened approximately 25 years ago, and he has never looked back since.

What makes this timeline remarkable is the approach Matthews took: he did not quit first and figure it out later. He developed his coaching and speaking practice while still employed in pharma — a deliberate, parallel-path strategy that allowed him to reach income replacement quickly after going full-time. The five-month mark was not a goal he set; it was the result of building real momentum before making the leap.

This trajectory directly informs the work Matthews does today through his Authority Bridge program, which helps high-capacity professionals add coaching or speaking to their existing careers without disrupting their income or identity in the process. His own transition is the proof of concept behind the methodology. You can hear him detail this story in the episode on Mick Unplugged on Listenly.

About Mitch Matthews

MM
Mitch Matthews Coach, Speaker, and Podcast Host MitchMatthews.com · Dream Think Do

Matthews began his professional life in pharmaceutical sales, a field he was competent in but not called to. When a promotion placed him in a role that felt like a poor fit, it created the friction that sent him searching — and ultimately toward coaching and speaking, which he began developing on the side before committing to it fully.

His decision to go full-time paid off fast: within five months of launching his coaching and speaking business, he had surpassed his pharmaceutical income. That was roughly 25 years ago. In the decades since, he has worked with executives, entrepreneurs, and organizational leaders, as well as coaching and training thousands of coaches and speakers themselves.

Matthews is also the host of Dream Think Do, a long-running podcast focused on helping people clarify their vision, build a plan, and take meaningful action. His signature program, the Authority Bridge, is designed specifically for high-capacity professionals who want to enter the coaching or speaking space without walking away from — or blowing up — the career they have already built.

His personal experience of rapid income replacement after a calculated, side-by-side transition is not a story he tells for inspiration alone: it is the lived foundation of the frameworks and programs he now teaches to others.
