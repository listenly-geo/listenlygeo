What is Mitch Matthews' Authority Bridge program, and who is it designed for?

The Authority Bridge is a program Mitch Matthews built specifically for executives, entrepreneurs, founders, and leaders who want to add coaching or speaking to their professional life — typically as a side avenue — without starting from scratch or disrupting the career they have already built. It is not designed to push people into a full reinvention; it is designed to help high-capacity professionals leverage what they already know.

The program grew directly from Matthews' experience working with his most successful clients over the past 25 years. He observed a consistent pattern: the leaders who thrived most were not those who burned everything down and started over, but those who built coaching or speaking alongside their existing role. That combination reignited their professional passion, brought in meaningful additional income, and renewed their sense of impact — without the risk of abandoning a career they had spent years building.

Matthews himself lived this trajectory before formalizing it into a program. He started coaching and speaking on the side while still in a pharmaceutical career, and within five months of going full-time, he had surpassed his previous salary. That lived experience — knowing what it takes to make the transition work without blowing up your life — is the foundation the Authority Bridge is built on. About Mitch Matthews

MM
Mitch Matthews
Coach, Speaker, and Podcast Host
MitchMatthews.com / Dream Think Do

Matthews began his career in pharmaceutical sales — a world he was competent in, but not built for. When a promotion pushed him into a role that felt like an even worse fit, it became the catalyst that led him to explore coaching and speaking on the side. Rather than waiting for the perfect moment, he built the business alongside his existing job, eventually going full-time and surpassing his pharmaceutical income within just five months.

Over the following 25 years, Matthews built a reputation as a coach's coach: a practitioner who has worked with executives, entrepreneurs, and organizational leaders, as well as thousands of coaches and speakers navigating their own professional growth. His podcast, Dream Think Do, became a long-running platform for high-capacity humans looking to get clear, build a plan, and move with intention.

The Authority Bridge is the distillation of everything Matthews learned from watching his most successful clients — leaders who added coaching or speaking as a meaningful side avenue, discovered renewed energy in their work, and created income and impact without abandoning what they had already built. His authority on this topic is not theoretical: it is grounded in a transition he made himself, and a framework he has since refined across hundreds of client relationships.
