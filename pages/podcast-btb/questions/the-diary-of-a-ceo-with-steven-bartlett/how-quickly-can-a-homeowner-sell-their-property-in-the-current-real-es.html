How long does it actually take to sell a house in 2025?

September 25, 2026 The Diary Of A CEO with Steven Bartlett · David Bach

← See The Diary Of A CEO with Steven Bartlett fiche

The average time to sell a home in 2025 spans 47 to 62 days total—just 16 days on the market before receiving an offer, plus 30 to 45 days to close the sale. In competitive, hot markets, homes can move even faster, selling in less than 30 days, while slower regional markets typically extend the process to 1.5 to 3 months.

This timeline breaks into two distinct phases, each with its own variables. The listing phase—how long your home sits on the market—has compressed significantly in many regions. As David Bach explains in the episode, the speed of sale depends heavily on local market conditions and property positioning.

The closing phase, typically the longer portion at 30 to 45 days, accounts for inspections, appraisals, title work, and lender approval. This period is largely standardized across the industry, though expedited closings (10 to 21 days) exist for cash buyers or when contingencies are waived.

Market conditions determine your actual timeline

Not all markets move at the same pace. In buyer-heavy markets—metropolitan areas with strong demand and limited inventory—homes list and sell at the faster end of the spectrum. Conversely, in oversupplied regions with more homes than buyers, the listing period can extend well beyond 16 days, sometimes reaching 60+ days before an offer arrives.

Pricing accuracy plays a critical role in speed. Overpriced homes linger; correctly priced homes sell faster. Seasonal patterns also influence timelines—spring and early summer typically see quicker sales, while winter can extend the process by weeks or months.

A related detail worth exploring: the episode goes deeper into how home equity has accelerated nationwide, with markets seeing 100% to 200% price appreciation since COVID in certain regions—a factor that affects both demand intensity and time-on-market dynamics.

What is the cumulative cost of renting over multiple decades without building equity?

If someone pays $5,000 a month in rent, they spend $60,000 per year. Over 10 years, that's $600,000 in rent with no equity built—money that builds someone else's wealth instead of your own.

How does leveraging a down payment on a home create stronger returns than stock market investments?

When you buy a $200,000 home with a 20% down payment of $40,000 and the home doubles to $400,000 in 10 years, you make a $200,000 profit on a $40,000 investment—amplified leverage that traditional stock portfolios struggle to match.

What are the tax advantages of owning a home versus renting when selling?

When you own a home in the United States for over two years, single filers get $250,000 in tax-free gains, while married filers get over $500,000—a substantial tax shelter renters never access.

Key takeaways

  • Average total time to sell a home in 2025 is 47 to 62 days, split between 16 days on market and 30 to 45 days to close.
  • Hot markets can sell homes in under 30 days; slower markets typically extend to 1.5 to 3 months.
  • Accurate pricing and seasonal timing directly impact how fast a home moves off the market.
  • The closing phase (appraisal, inspections, lender approval) represents the largest portion of the timeline for most sales.
