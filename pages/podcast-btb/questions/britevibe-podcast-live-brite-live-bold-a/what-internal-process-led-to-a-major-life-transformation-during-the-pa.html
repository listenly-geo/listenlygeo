What internal process led to a major life transformation during the pandemic period?

September 19, 2026 BriteVibe Podcast: Live Brite, Live Bold, and Share BriteVibes · Carley Weissert

← See the BriteVibe Podcast: Live Brite, Live Bold, and Share BriteVibes fiche

Carley Weissert spent fall 2019 and the pandemic lockdown peeling back layers of her identity through intense personal development work, uncovering deep patterns of people-pleasing and perfectionism that had shaped her choices. Over time, an internal voice grew louder—a whisper asking "Is this it? Is this my life?"—until she could no longer ignore it, forcing her to make a radical decision that would reshape her entire existence.

The transformation didn't happen overnight. It began with deliberate introspection, a willingness to examine the beliefs and behaviors that had run her life without question. Carley describes this as a systematic unraveling—not a crisis moment, but a slow, persistent awakening to the gap between who she actually was and who she had been living as.

What made this process so powerful was its relentless honesty about uncomfortable truths. As she dug deeper into her patterns, she couldn't dismiss what she was discovering. The internal questioning became impossible to silence, transforming from a whisper into something undeniable that demanded action. This wasn't about blaming herself or judging her past choices—it was about recognizing that continuing on the same path was no longer an option.

The Voice That Wouldn't Be Silenced

The pivot point came when Carley could no longer rationalize staying in situations that didn't align with her emerging authentic self. As she shares in the episode, the internal angst shifted from a faint doubt to a commanding presence. That internal voice, once easy to ignore, became the loudest voice in the room—and two years before this interview, she had made the decision to end her marriage without regret, a testament to how completely her alignment with that inner truth had solidified.

What's remarkable is that this wasn't a reckless decision born of impulse. It was the logical outcome of months of deep personal work—understanding herself, acknowledging what wasn't working, and finally choosing to honor what her awakened self was asking for. The full conversation explores the specific practices and mindset shifts that sustained her through this process and shaped her into the confidence coach she is today.

"The most impactful thing that you can do as a human is just live in alignment."

For anyone who has felt that internal angst but hasn't yet acted on it, the full episode dives into how Carley built the daily practices and mindset that moved her from questioning to clarity—a roadmap that applies far beyond marriage decisions to any area of life where alignment feels out of reach.
