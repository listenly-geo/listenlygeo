How does Oasis Senior Advisors help families navigate the transition to senior care?

September 5, 2026 From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast · John Killeen

← See the From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast fiche

Oasis Senior Advisors is a free service that removes the stress families face when transitioning seniors to assisted living, memory care, or independent living. The team selects communities based on healthcare needs, location, budget, and specific preferences, then schedules and attends every tour with families throughout the entire decision-making process until they select a community.

When a family contacts Oasis Senior Advisors, they're usually making this decision on behalf of a loved one during an emotionally demanding time. Rather than forcing families to navigate dozens of options alone, Killeen and his team do the groundwork upfront, filtering communities according to what matters most—whether that's proximity to family, specific medical capabilities, or cost constraints.

The service operates nationwide through a franchise model, with 120 Oasis Senior Advisors owners across the country. While Killeen primarily serves Pinellas County, Florida, families relocating to other states can be connected with franchisees in their destination area. This network means a family in Florida moving to New York, for example, can access the same coordinated support in their new location.

From decades in senior services to guiding care transitions

Killeen's background shaped his approach to this work. Before becoming an Oasis Senior Advisors owner just under two years ago, he spent 30-plus years working with the senior population, including 30 years in the food service industry where he served as Regional Director of Operations managing 15 accounts across the Southeast. This hands-on experience gave him intimate knowledge of how assisted living and memory care communities actually operate.

That operational perspective proved invaluable when transitioning into senior placement. Having overseen food service across multiple facilities, Killeen understood the quality markers, staffing patterns, and day-to-day realities of senior living communities that families often can't see during brief tours. As explored in this episode of From Courtroom to Carline, his background became the foundation for a franchise model designed to simplify a genuinely overwhelming decision for families.

One concrete detail worth hearing firsthand: Killeen has toured 90 percent of assisted living and memory care communities in Pinellas County—a level of direct familiarity that informs every recommendation he makes. This insider knowledge is discussed at length in the full episode, where he explains how personal observation differs from what marketing materials suggest.

Key takeaways

  • Oasis Senior Advisors operates as a free service nationwide through 120 franchise owners, with Killeen serving Pinellas County and able to connect families relocating to other states.
  • The service filters senior living communities based on four core criteria: healthcare needs, location, budget, and any specific family preferences.
  • Advisors schedule tours and attend them alongside families from initial exploration through final community selection, reducing the emotional and logistical burden on relatives.
  • Killeen's 30-plus years in senior services, including operational roles overseeing multiple facilities, provides him with insider knowledge of community quality beyond what families can observe during standard tours.
