How did Oxalis Garcia become a legal consultant on 90 Day Fiance?

September 4, 2026 From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast · Oxalis Garcia

← See the From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast fiche

Garcia appeared on the reality TV show 90 Day Fiance as a legal consultant for one of the couples. She and her law partner initially disagreed on whether to participate, but she proceeded, using the platform to address widespread legal misconceptions among viewers.

A hesitant start with a clear mission

Garcia's decision to join 90 Day Fiance wasn't straightforward. Her law partner had reservations about the appearance, creating an internal debate before she moved forward. Despite this initial friction, Garcia saw the opportunity differently—not as entertainment, but as a chance to educate a massive audience on legal matters they typically encountered in her family law practice.

What made her appearance meaningful was her focus on correcting misconceptions rather than simply providing on-camera advice. As a family law attorney with 20 years of legal experience across three jurisdictions (Puerto Rico, Florida, and Maryland), Garcia brought real expertise to conversations that millions of viewers would witness, transforming a reality TV moment into genuine legal education.

The episode itself involved providing a legal consultation in her office, grounding the advice in her professional setting rather than letting the reality TV format dominate the substance of the conversation.

A deeper dive into Garcia's philosophy on legal media appearances, and how she balances public visibility with professional responsibility, comes through in her conversation on From Courtroom to Carline, where she discusses her candidacy and professional journey in full.

What advice does Oxalis Garcia give to someone who wants to open their own law firm?

Garcia advises aspiring firm owners to start networking early to build name recognition, and to take business administration courses, because running a firm requires skills beyond legal knowledge.
