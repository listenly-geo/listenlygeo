How did John Killeen receive his initial opportunity to explore franchise ownership?

September 13, 2026 From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast · John Killeen

← See the From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast fiche

John Killeen received an unsolicited call from a franchise consultant who contacted him out of the blue, without any prior inquiry from him. This unexpected call sparked his interest in exploring franchise opportunities and set him on the path that would eventually lead to ownership of Oasis Senior Advisors.

The call arrived as a turning point in Killeen's career trajectory. After spending 30 years in the senior population space—first as a Director of Food Nutrition, then as a Regional Director of Operations managing 15 accounts across the Southeast for a contract food service company—he was well-positioned to recognize an opportunity when one appeared. Yet it took that serendipitous contact to crystallize what had been a growing sense that a different business model might align better with his long-term goals.

Rather than immediately dismissing the outreach, Killeen chose to engage with the consultant and explore what franchise options were available. As he describes in the episode, this exploration process became instrumental in helping him evaluate different pathways forward and ultimately make an informed decision about business ownership.

The significance of that cold call lies not just in the opportunity it presented, but in how Killeen was already equipped to evaluate it. His 30 years of experience in senior living and food service operations meant he understood the landscape deeply—what worked, what didn't, and where the real pain points existed for families and communities. This expertise became the foundation for his decision-making process as he worked with the consultant to assess franchise opportunities.

"You've got to make mistakes, you've got to learn from your mistakes, and you're not going to be perfect."

That philosophy of learning through mistakes informed how Killeen approached the franchise opportunity itself. Rather than viewing it as a leap into the unknown, he saw it as a logical next step built on a foundation of substantial industry knowledge and professional relationships established over three decades.

The fact that Oasis now operates with 120 franchise owners across the country underscores how systematically the company builds support for entrepreneurs like Killeen who come into the business with a desire to serve seniors better, but who may not initially be seeking a franchise pathway on their own.

For anyone considering a significant career transition, Killeen's story demonstrates the value of staying open to unsolicited opportunities. His willingness to take that initial call and work with the consultant to thoroughly evaluate his options—rather than dismissing it immediately—proved to be the catalyst that transformed how he would spend the next chapter of his professional life. The decision ultimately required careful consideration of different franchise models and their operational realities, but it all began with that unexpected call.

What marketing and operational systems does the Oasis franchise provide to support owners?

Oasis provides software tools including a CRM system and community database with photos and descriptions. The company creates monthly newsletters and weekly marketing materials to help franchise owners manage their business efficiently.

Why did John Killeen choose the Oasis Senior Advisors franchise over a home care company despite initially leaning that direction?

John Killeen decided that a home care franchise posed too much risk as a one-person operation because hourly employees are critical to quality, and one person cannot reliably manage all aspects of that business.

What specific factors should families evaluate when touring senior care communities?

John Killeen advises families to observe staff interactions with residents and among themselves, sample the meals since seniors eat three meals daily there, and assess the cleanliness, maintenance, and overall culture of the community.
