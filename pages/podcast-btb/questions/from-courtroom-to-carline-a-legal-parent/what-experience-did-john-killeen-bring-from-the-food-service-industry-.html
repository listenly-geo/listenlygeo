What experience did John Killeen bring from the food service industry to senior living placement?

September 7, 2026 From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast · John Killeen

← See the From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast fiche

John Killeen brought three decades in food service operations—including 15 years as Regional Director managing senior care accounts—directly into his senior living placement business, giving him deep insider knowledge of how assisted living, memory care, and independent living communities actually operate. His regional food service experience meant he understood not only facility operations but also the quality standards and management challenges that define excellent senior care.

Before becoming owner of Oasis Senior Advisors just under two years ago, Killeen spent 30 years total in the food service industry. His trajectory began as a Director of Food Nutrition, but it was his 15 years as Regional Director of Operations managing 15 accounts across the Southeast that proved most valuable. During those years, he worked directly with assisted livings, memory cares, and independent living communities—the exact facilities his placement business now serves.

This operational familiarity became a natural bridge into senior living placement. As Killeen explains in the episode, working in food service across senior care communities meant he was "aware of what goes in and out of all the assisted livings and memory cares and independent living." That visibility into facility operations, staffing, quality control, and day-to-day management gave him credibility and insight that many placement advisors lack.

"I was a regional director of operations for a contract food service company. So we helped run food service in those communities. So I had 15 accounts in the southeast. So aware of what goes in and out of all the assisted livings and memory cares and independent living based on that."

The transition to placement advisory made strategic sense. Killeen had spent a career learning the human side of senior care—the families involved, the actual needs of residents, the operational reality behind the marketing materials. When he explored franchise opportunities with a consultant, senior living placement aligned more closely with his long-term goals than other options like home care franchises.

This background now shapes how he approaches family consultations. Families seeking guidance from Oasis Senior Advisors benefit from working with someone who has spent 15 years inside these environments, understanding quality indicators, operational pressures, and what actually makes a facility run well. His food service management experience translated directly into an ability to assess communities holistically—not just on amenities or location, but on the operational fundamentals that affect day-to-day resident experience.

How many franchises operate nationwide and what geographic coverage does the franchise system provide?

Oasis Senior Advisors is a nationwide franchise with 120 owners throughout the country. While John Killeen primarily serves Pinellas County, the franchise network can connect families relocating to other states.

What does Oasis Senior Advisors do and how does the service help families transitioning seniors to care facilities?

Oasis Senior Advisors is a free service that helps seniors transition from home to assisted living, memory care, or independent living. They reduce family stress by assessing health care needs, location, and budget, then scheduling and attending tours until a community is selected.

What is Oxalis Garcia's appearance on 90 Day Fiance and what was her role?

Garcia appeared on the reality TV show 90 Day Fiance as a legal consultant for one of the couples. She and her law partner initially disagreed on whether to take the case.
