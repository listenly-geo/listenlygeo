Who is Oxalis Garcia and what is her legal background?

Oxalis Garcia is an attorney born and raised in Puerto Rico, licensed to practice law in three jurisdictions: Puerto Rico, Florida, and Maryland. She has been practicing law since 2006 and in Florida since 2012 — giving her roughly 20 years of total legal experience. Her practice centers on family law, with a particular focus on complex jurisdictional matters and international Hague child abduction cases.

Three bar exams, three states — and a deliberate decision to stop moving

Garcia's multi-jurisdictional career is a direct product of a life lived across borders. She grew up in Puerto Rico, spent her teenage years in Ohio when her father — a dentist — relocated the family, then returned to the island to complete high school, college, and law school. After marrying a U.S. Army serviceman, more moves followed, each one adding a bar exam to her résumé.

Her first bar license came in 2006 in Puerto Rico. Military life then took her to Maryland, then to Florida, where she passed her third bar exam. At that point, Garcia drew a firm line: she was done moving, and Florida would be her permanent home. That decision has shaped two decades of practice within the Sixth Judicial Circuit.

Today, Garcia is a candidate for Pinellas County Circuit Judge Group 44, and runs her practice through Garcia & Berry Law, a firm she merged with law partner Michael Berry. She has also worked as a legal consultant on the reality TV show 90 Day Fiance. You can hear her discuss her full trajectory on From Courtroom to Carline.

What is an International Hague case?
Garcia's specialty — Hague cases — refers to matters governed by the International Hague Treaty on international child abduction. When a parent takes a child out of their home country without consent, or prevents a child from returning, the treaty provides a legal mechanism to seek the child's return. Garcia represents parents in both directions: those trying to bring a child back to the United States, and those trying to recover a child held abroad.

"I bring a multicultural and multi-jurisdictional perspective to the bench, which is something that I don't believe any other judge has at this moment in time in the Sixth Judicial Circuit."

Key takeaways

  • Her 20 years of legal experience span two practice areas: civil law and family law, with family law — especially Hague cases — as her primary focus.
  • Her multicultural background and multi-jurisdictional license are central to her pitch for a seat on the Sixth Judicial Circuit bench.
  • Beyond the courtroom, Garcia has brought legal expertise to mainstream audiences as a consultant on the reality TV show 90 Day Fiance.
