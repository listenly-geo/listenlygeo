How does John Maxwell leadership training complement Renee Lopez's work in college recruiting and business consulting?

September 24, 2026 From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast · Renee Lopez

← See the From Courtroom to Carline: A Legal Parenting Podcast fiche

After implementing John Maxwell's leadership principles with college teams, Renee Lopez became certified as a John Maxwell trainer, opening doors to work across multiple industries. This credential allows her to teach team dynamics, personality assessment through DISC training, and leadership development to sports teams, churches, Fortune 500 companies, schools, and medical organizations—all built on the principle that team dynamics transcend industry boundaries.

Lopez's transition into Maxwell training emerged naturally from her 14 years in college coaching. She recognized patterns in how teams function regardless of the sport or field, and Maxwell's frameworks gave her a structured methodology to scale that insight beyond athletics.

The versatility of this certification is the real advantage. As she explains in the episode, whether she's working with a Fortune 500 leadership team, a church community group, or a medical practice, the same foundational principles apply—building trust, improving communication, and developing leaders who can multiply their impact on others.

Teaching universal team dynamics across industries

The core insight driving her certification work is this: team dynamics don't change when you change the industry. A breakdown in communication matters the same way in a corporate boardroom as it does in a locker room. Personal friction undermines results everywhere.

This principle is detailed at length in this podcast discussion—Lopez describes how she applies Maxwell's DISC personality assessment tool across contexts to help leaders understand their own style and adapt to others, creating better team cohesion whether the group is selling products, healing patients, or competing for wins.

"The idea is that leadership principles are universal. Once you understand how to lead people, you can lead people in any environment."

What makes the Maxwell training particularly valuable for Lopez's consulting work is that it gives her a branded, repeatable framework. Clients aren't just getting her intuition as a former coach; they're getting access to research-backed models that have been tested across thousands of organizations. That credibility opens doors in sectors where sports expertise alone wouldn't.

The deeper layer—and the one worth hearing directly from Lopez—is how she integrates recruiting insights with leadership coaching, creating a pipeline where young athletes learn to become leaders themselves, and organizations learn to attract and develop the next generation of talent. That integration is explored more fully in the full episode.
