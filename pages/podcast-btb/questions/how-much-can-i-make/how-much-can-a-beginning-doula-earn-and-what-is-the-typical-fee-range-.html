How much can a beginning doula earn, and what is the typical fee range by experience level?

September 20, 2026 How Much Can I Make? — Real Jobs. Real Stories. Career Insights · Mary Riley

← See the How Much Can I Make? — Real Jobs. Real Stories. Career Insights fiche

Beginning doulas typically earn a few hundred dollars per birth, often used to cover basic costs like childcare and gas, while experienced doulas in rural and suburban areas charge $1,500 to $2,000+ per birth. In major cities like New York City, doulas can command $3,000 to $4,000 per birth, reflecting significant variation based on location and experience.

When starting out, many doulas accept lower fees or even work for free to build experience and establish their practice. As discussed in the episode, these early births are critical learning opportunities that help new practitioners refine their skills and grow confidence in their work.

The jump from beginner to experienced doula represents a substantial income increase. Those working outside major metropolitan areas can realistically expect to earn $1,500 to a couple thousand dollars per birth once they've built a solid reputation and client base. This typically reflects 2–5 years of consistent practice and positive client feedback.

Urban markets, particularly high-cost-of-living areas, reward expertise and established credentials with significantly higher rates. Mary Riley, who has supported over 2,500 births, notes that while New York City doulas regularly charge $3,000 to $4,000 per birth, geography remains one of the strongest determinants of earning potential in this field.

Location matters as much as experience. A beginning doula in a small town or suburban area will earn far less than even a moderately experienced doula in a major city, even if both are equally skilled. The podcast explores how doulas in different regions structure their pricing and build sustainable careers within their local markets.

Key takeaways

  • Beginning doulas charge a few hundred dollars per birth, primarily to cover immediate costs and gain experience.
  • Experienced doulas in rural and suburban markets earn $1,500 to $2,000+ per birth.
  • New York City and major metropolitan areas command $3,000 to $4,000 per birth.
  • Location and experience level are the two strongest determinants of doula earnings.

What certification and training is required to become a doula?

Doula certification typically involves one or two weekend workshops, book learning, and practice with testing. The modern process is somewhat more involved than when Mary Riley started 39 years ago, but still remains accessible without years of formal schooling.

What does a doula do during pregnancy and childbirth?

A doula provides a combination of physical and emotional support, helping people advocate for themselves within hospital situations or home births. The role is deeply personal and adapts to each client's unique needs and birth plan.

How do stage managers handle unexpected emergencies during live performances?
