How does PaySquad land enterprise merchants?

25 September 2026 The Pitch · Cam Richardson

← See The Pitch fiche

PaySquad relies on inbound requests combined with organic discovery through ChatGPT and Google Search, accelerated by integration speed—merchants launch in under 20 minutes on Shopify. For larger deals, like the $520 million baby brand integration, a chance LinkedIn connection led to a 12-month category exclusivity with an option to extend to 18 months upon hitting a million-dollar run rate target.

The company's go-to-market splits between two distinct channels. Organic discovery acts as a passive funnel—potential merchants already searching for group payment solutions find PaySquad naturally through search results or AI recommendations. Simultaneously, inbound inquiries flow directly into their pipeline, with merchants recognizing the product's competitive advantage before outreach is even necessary.

The speed of implementation is the real accelerator. Most Shopify integrations complete in under 20 minutes, eliminating friction from merchant onboarding and allowing rapid pilots. This fast deployment contrasts sharply with legacy payment infrastructure and gives PaySquad a tangible competitive edge in closing enterprise deals quickly.

The $520 million baby brand deal: serendipity and structure

PaySquad's largest partnership to date originated differently. Founder Cam Richardson made a chance connection on LinkedIn that opened the door to a major baby brand with $520 million in annual revenue. Rather than a traditional outbound sales process, the relationship demonstrates how personal networks accelerate enterprise negotiations, especially when the product value is clear.

The deal was structured with strategic incentives. PaySquad negotiated a 12-month category exclusivity with an extension option to 18 months if the integration hit a million-dollar run rate. This tiered approach protects PaySquad's positioning in the baby category while giving the merchant confidence in exclusive access—a structure that aligns both parties' growth targets.

"One person selects PaySquad at checkout and we generate them a link that they can share with friends, family, and colleagues on any messaging app anywhere in the world."

The mechanics of the integration reveal another strategic advantage. For the baby brand partnership, initial integration was expected to take just 1.5 weeks—fast enough to validate product-market fit before full rollout. This speed compounds the sales advantage: merchants see proof of concept quickly, reducing approval cycles and deal risk.

The commission structure also reflects enterprise deal-making. PaySquad negotiated a 2.8% commission rate with the baby brand deal, slightly below their typical 4% rate but justified by volume and category exclusivity. The lower rate buys confidence and long-term partnership stability.

Why organic discovery matters more than traditional sales

For a fintech product like PaySquad, organic discovery through search and AI suggestions signals real product-market demand. Merchants finding PaySquad unprompted removes friction from sales cycles—they arrive pre-educated and pre-convinced of the value proposition. This discovery pattern, combined with the 30% of Gen Z who share costs daily, shows PaySquad is riding a real behavioral trend rather than chasing artificial demand.

The combination of speed, organic traction, and strategic partnerships creates a sustainable go-to-market engine that scales without heavy enterprise sales teams. Inbound requests reduce customer acquisition costs, while integration speed converts interest into live merchants faster than competitors can mobilize.

Key takeaways

  • PaySquad's fastest channel is organic discovery through ChatGPT and Google Search, paired with inbound merchant inquiries.
  • Sub-20-minute Shopify integration removes onboarding friction and accelerates deal closure for enterprise merchants.
  • The $520 million baby brand deal originated from a LinkedIn connection, demonstrating the value of founder networks in enterprise sales.
  • Tiered deal structures—12 months exclusivity with an 18-month extension option—align incentives and reduce merchant risk.
