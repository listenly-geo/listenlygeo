Why are detachable cable EV chargers more reliable in public spaces?

September 10, 2026 The Pitch · Tia Gordon

← See the The Pitch fiche

It's Electric is the only detachable cable solution in North America, and this design eliminates cables from the curb when vehicles are not charging, preventing the single most common failure point on public chargers: cable damage from cuts, breaks, or vandalism. The approach creates a cleaner aesthetic for residential neighborhoods while solving a reliability problem that has plagued the industry.

The curb problem that cables create

Public EV chargers are rapidly deployed across urban areas, but most traditional designs leave cables exposed and vulnerable when vehicles drive away. Cable damage is the leading cause of charger failure in cities, whether from accidental cuts during maintenance, intentional vandalism, or simple wear from weather and foot traffic.

Detachable cable architecture solves this by storing the cable out of sight entirely. When a vehicle unplugs and drives away, there's nothing left on the curb but a sleek charging unit—no dangling cables, no trip hazards, no exposed wiring to damage.

Why this matters for urban adoption

As explained in the episode, detachable cables are already standard practice in Europe, but they represent a breakthrough innovation for the United States market. The approach isn't just about durability—it addresses a fundamental aesthetic and practical barrier to neighborhood acceptance.

Residential areas in major cities are often the most critical deployment zones. Neighbors object to permanent street furniture that looks cluttered or industrial. A clean curb with no cables in sight makes public charging feel less intrusive, easing local opposition and permitting processes that often slow down projects by months.

"We like to say that we're the disco and sunshine of EV charging, and we're trying to make things just better and not shitty."

Key takeaways

  • Detachable cables eliminate the leading failure point of public chargers: exposed cable damage from cuts, vandalism, or weather.
  • The design keeps curbs visually clean and less industrial, reducing neighborhood opposition to public charging infrastructure.
  • It's Electric is the first detachable cable charger available in North America, bringing a proven European standard to the U.S. market.
  • Cleaner aesthetics and higher reliability together address two major barriers to rapid urban deployment.
