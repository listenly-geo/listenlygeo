How many exhibitors are expected at the American Society of Plumbing Engineers expo?

September 25, 2026 Empowering Industry Podcast - A Production of Empowering Pumps & Equipment · Brian Henry

← See the Empowering Industry Podcast - A Production of Empowering Pumps & Equipment fiche

The American Society of Plumbing Engineers expects nearly 300 exhibitors at its upcoming expo, with significant exhibit space dedicated to the plumbing industry. The organization has also seen approximately 30 to 35 additional companies cross over from WEFTEC to participate in the ASPE event, reflecting sustained growth across consecutive conventions.

The scale of ASPE's exhibitor presence underscores the vitality of the plumbing engineering sector. As Brian Henry explains in the Empowering Industry Podcast, the organization has experienced measurable expansion over recent conventions, attracting both dedicated members and crossover participation from related industry events.

The dual-event strategy—maintaining both the core ASPE conference and drawing exhibitors from WEFTEC—reflects how industry professionals and vendors navigate multiple specialized trade platforms. This cross-pollination suggests that companies view both events as essential touchpoints for reaching their target markets in the plumbing and pumping sectors.

Beyond raw exhibitor numbers, the growth trajectory matters. The podcast discusses how ASPE has sustained momentum across multiple consecutive conventions, indicating that the organization continues to strengthen its value proposition to both exhibitors and attendees. This consistency is critical in industries where trade shows serve as central hubs for product launches, networking, and relationship maintenance.

For companies in the plumbing and pumping sectors, nearly 300 dedicated exhibitors represents a comprehensive marketplace in a single venue. The event's ability to attract exhibitors from adjacent industry expos (WEFTEC) suggests that the ASPE platform has become indispensable for vendors seeking exposure to the decision-makers, engineers, and procurement professionals who attend.
