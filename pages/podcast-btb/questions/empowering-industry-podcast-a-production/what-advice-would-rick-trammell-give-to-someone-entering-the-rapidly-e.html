What advice would Rick Trammell give to someone entering the rapidly evolving data center industry?

September 10, 2026 Empowering Industry Podcast - A Production of Empowering Pumps & Equipment · Rick Trammell

← See the Empowering Industry Podcast fiche

Trammell emphasizes that reading articles and staying informed is essential because the industry evolves faster than you can keep up. Beyond research, he recommends taking specialized classes in the data center space and broadening your understanding to encompass the entire system—not just your narrow technical specialty, whether that's racks or HVAC.

Build Knowledge Across the Whole System

The data center field moves with tremendous speed, and siloed expertise quickly becomes obsolete. Trammell stresses the importance of understanding how different components interconnect and function as a unified whole, rather than mastering only the segment you currently work in.

This holistic perspective matters because integrated systems depend on every part performing well. As Trammell explains in the podcast, the data center world is no longer about isolated equipment selections—it's about coordinated packages designed to work seamlessly together.

Stay Current Through Active Learning

Reading every relevant article you can find is non-negotiable for anyone serious about building a career in data centers. The industry landscape shifts constantly, driven by new technologies, efficiency demands, and evolving customer needs.

Beyond passive reading, Trammell advocates for hands-on engagement. Take advantage of training programs and classes specifically tailored to the data center space, and pursue opportunities to test and experiment with emerging technologies—particularly AI-driven solutions that are reshaping equipment design and deployment. This point is explored in greater depth within this episode, where Trammell shares how continuous testing accelerates expertise.

"Give them the best solution for the project, even if it's not a solution that I represent. I'll say, you know what, who makes the perfect thing for you is X, Y, or Z. Go get that product because that's what you need."

The specificity Trammell brings to his advice reflects a deeper truth: the full episode covers how rapid construction schedules in data centers demand professionals who can think across disciplines, whether mechanical, electrical, or logistical.

Key takeaways

  • Read industry articles continuously—the data center field moves faster than any individual can naturally stay current.
  • Pursue formal training and specialized classes in data center systems, not just your current role.
  • Understand the whole integrated system and how components work together, rather than mastering only your narrow specialization.
  • Test and experiment with new technologies like AI-driven cooling and efficiency innovations as they emerge.
