Does Rugby Tots offer referral discounts for existing members?

19 September 2026 The Toddle About Show · Tom Brown

← See the The Toddle About Show fiche

Yes, Rugby Tots franchisees offer a 10% refer-a-friend discount per referral, and the key advantage is that this discount stacks. A family who refers multiple new members can compound their savings over time, potentially reducing their term fees to just 50% through ongoing referrals.

Tom Brown, the Rugby Tots franchisee running classes in South Bucks and East Orsa, uses this strategy to reward loyalty and encourage word-of-mouth growth. The stackable nature of the discount means there's no cap on how much families can save if they continue to build the community around them.

How the stacking mechanic works in practice

Rather than a one-time discount, the refer-a-friend offer compounds with each successful referral. Brown explains that a family referring one friend receives 10%, and referring two friends could theoretically reach 20% off, and so on. As discussed in the full episode, Brown's example shows families paying only 50% of their regular term fees through accumulated referral discounts—a meaningful saving for families already committed to the programme.

This approach works because Rugby Tots relies heavily on local community reach. Families in South Bucks and East Orsa who love the programme naturally become ambassadors, and Brown rewards that organic growth with tangible cost reductions rather than forcing marketing spend.

"We want to build confidence in young children, we want to give opportunity for those lightbulb moments, we want to work on movement literacy to really develop a really supportive platform that children can grow in without really knowing what they're developing."

The referral discount is specific to Brown's franchises in South Bucks and East Orsa, so families should check with their local Rugby Tots class to confirm whether the same offer applies in other regions. Each franchisee has autonomy over pricing and promotions, so availability may vary by location.

Before committing to the full term, new families can also take advantage of free taster classes and a two-week money-back guarantee, which removes the risk of trying out a class before locking in fees or referral discounts.

What trial or money-back options does Rugby Tots offer new families?

Rugby Tots offers free taster classes to new families, which can be found by entering a postcode on www.rugbytots.co.uk to locate local classes and sessions near you.

What is the role of parents and caregivers in Rugby Tots classes?

Rugby Tots actively encourages grown-ups to participate in sessions alongside their children. The programme aims to give parents the opportunity to witness their child's development while enjoying the fun together.

How does Rugby Tots use imaginative play to develop physical skills in young children?

Rugby Tots uses imaginative framing to teach movement skills without children realising they are learning. For example, asking children to stomp like a rugby player or jump like a ball helps develop motor skills through play.
