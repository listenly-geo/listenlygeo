How many brands does Tracefuse work with, and how many Amazon reviews has it removed so far?

As of the time of recording, Tracefuse works with 700 brands and has removed over 16,000 Amazon reviews. Shane Barker attributes the bulk of this growth to the last two years of operation — a sharp contrast to the difficult early period of building the business from the ground up.

700 brands served
16,000+ reviews removed
~5.5 yrs years in operation

Barker is candid about the fact that Tracefuse did not start with a clear playbook. The company spent roughly the first two to three years cracking the code on its review removal methodology before achieving consistent, repeatable results. The acceleration seen in the most recent two years reflects both the maturation of that methodology and the early integration of AI into the review analysis workflow — a process Barker says began over three years ago, well before mainstream tools like Gemini and Claude entered the market. That AI layer reduced the human review workload from approximately 1,000 reviews per person per day to around 400, while improving accuracy and throughput at scale.

Tracefuse was, by Barker's account, the first company to offer a policy-compliant review removal service on Amazon. That pioneering status came at a cost: there were no benchmarks, no predecessors, and no established process to follow. The 700-brand and 16,000-review figures represent what was built without any of those advantages. "We were the first company to ever remove reviews on Amazon. Now we work with 700 brands and removed over 16,000 reviews. But we didn't start off with tons of customers — we paved the way."

— Shane Barker, Founder & CEO, Tracefuse · The Conference Room, Ep. 182

About Shane Barker

Shane Barker
Founder & CEO, Tracefuse · Founder, Shane Barker Consulting

Shane Barker is a C-level strategist and tech entrepreneur with over two decades of experience building, scaling, and productizing businesses. He is the founder and CEO of Tracefuse, the first company to offer a policy-compliant Amazon review removal service, and the founder of Shane Barker Consulting, a boutique agency that has scaled past seven figures and helped hundreds of service businesses implement systems for sustainable growth.

Barker is a member of the Forbes Coaches Council and a regular contributor to Forbes, HuffPost, and Inc. He has taught personal branding and influencer marketing at UCLA and hosted the Marketing Growth Podcast, where he interviewed leaders from companies including Moz and Socialnomics. His track record spans business models: earlier in his career, he owned and operated a bar in Chico, California, and ran a real estate company that helped clients grow from zero to $25 million.

It is that breadth of entrepreneurial experience — across hospitality, real estate, consulting, and now tech — combined with his early and deliberate adoption of AI inside Tracefuse, that gives his perspective on reputation management and business scaling its particular credibility.

How did Tracefuse's review removal methodology change over its first five and a half years?
Barker states that if you compared Tracefuse's filing methods from five and a half years ago to today, they are "almost polar opposites," largely because of continuous iteration and the integration of AI into the review process.
What mindset does Shane Barker recommend for Amazon sellers regarding platform dependency?
Barker argues that Amazon sellers must internalize that they are "renting Amazon space" — the platform does not owe them anything and will make decisions based on its own interests, not theirs.
Who were the first hires at Tracefuse and what roles did they fill?
The first hire was Susan, a trusted team member already working across Shane Barker's other businesses, who was brought on to help with operations, case management, and the day-to-day execution of the review removal process.
