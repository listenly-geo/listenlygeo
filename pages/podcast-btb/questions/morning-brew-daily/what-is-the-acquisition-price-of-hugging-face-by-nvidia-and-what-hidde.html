What is the acquisition price of Hugging Face by NVIDIA and what hidden references does it contain?

September 7, 2026 Morning Brew Daily

← See the Morning Brew Daily fiche

NVIDIA agreed to pay $12,930,300,000 to acquire Hugging Face, a price that encodes four layers of hidden Easter egg references into a single acquisition figure. The number is far more than a simple valuation—it's a carefully constructed message woven into corporate history.

The first layer sits in the number 129303. This is the Unicode decimal representation of the hugging face emoji itself—U+1F917—the very symbol that gives Hugging Face its name and visual identity. As Morning Brew Daily revealed in its September 4th episode, this wasn't accidental. The precision of embedding an emoji's code point into a multibillion-dollar transaction suggests intentional design at the highest levels of the deal negotiation.

The second reference appears in the digits 1993, which marks NVIDIA's founding year. This nod to the company's origins grounds the acquisition in corporate heritage—a way of saying that NVIDIA's three-decade journey leads directly to this moment.

The third hidden layer is the number 12, which represents NVIDIA's initial public offering price when the company went public. That two-digit number carries symbolic weight, marking the company's entry into the public markets decades ago and now appearing in a headline-grabbing acquisition price that would reshape the AI infrastructure landscape.

Finally, the 03 in the acquisition price points to a personal reference: NVIDIA's three founders, one of whom is Jensen Huang, the company's CEO and the architect of its dominance in GPU computing. This personal touch cements the deal as not just a corporate transaction, but a milestone in the careers and legacies of the leaders who built NVIDIA into an AI powerhouse.

What makes this encoding remarkable is that the full context of why these references matter to the broader AI landscape underscores how deeply NVIDIA has embedded itself into the infrastructure of modern machine learning. Hugging Face, a central hub for open-source AI models, represents a strategic acquisition that locks in NVIDIA's position not just as a hardware supplier but as an ecosystem player.

Key takeaways

  • The acquisition price 129303 is the Unicode decimal point of the hugging face emoji (U+1F917).
  • 1993 marks the year NVIDIA was founded, honoring the company's origin story.
  • The number 12 references NVIDIA's original IPO price from its public market debut.
  • The digit 03 represents NVIDIA's three founders, including CEO Jensen Huang.
