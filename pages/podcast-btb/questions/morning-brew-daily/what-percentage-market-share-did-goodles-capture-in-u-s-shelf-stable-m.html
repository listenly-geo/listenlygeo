How did Goodles grow from 0.8% to 7.8% market share in mac and cheese?

September 12, 2026 Morning Brew Daily

← See the Morning Brew Daily fiche

Goodles' U.S. market share in shelf-stable mac and cheese jumped from 0.8% to 7.8% between 2021 and June 2024, a ninefold increase that reflects rapid consumer adoption since its market entry. This growth trajectory demonstrates significant penetration into a category traditionally dominated by legacy players like Barilla.

The climb from less than 1% to nearly 8% of spending in just over three years underscores how a newer brand can reshape shelf space and consumer preference in a mature category. The numbers reveal not just market traction, but a shift in how consumers are evaluating mac and cheese—whether driven by ingredient quality, brand positioning, or product differentiation that resonates with modern grocery shoppers.

As discussed in the Morning Brew Daily episode, this kind of growth curve often signals a breakout moment when a challenger brand moves beyond early adopters and begins to capture mainstream market share. The trajectory matters as much as the endpoint: a brand that grows 10x in a few years is often worth watching for broader consumer behavior shifts in its category.

Why this growth matters for food and beverage strategy

Market share gains at this velocity—particularly in a category where shelf space is fiercely contested—suggest that Goodles has successfully differentiated itself in a way that matters to a significant slice of buyers. Whether through marketing messaging, product innovation, or retail expansion, the brand has moved from niche positioning to a measurable slice of everyday shopping baskets across the U.S.

The 7-percentage-point jump in just 36 months also reflects the reality of modern grocery competition. A brand entering in 2021 now claims enough shelf presence and sales to rank as a genuine competitor, not just a curiosity. For investors and rival manufacturers, this case study illustrates how quickly consumer packaged goods markets can consolidate around new entrants—a lesson relevant well beyond mac and cheese.

Key takeaways

  • Goodles climbed from 0.8% market share in 2021 to 7.8% by June 2024—a 875% increase in less than four years.
  • This growth occurred in a stable, mature category traditionally controlled by established brands, signaling successful brand differentiation.
  • The rapid ascent reflects both successful product positioning and effective retail distribution across U.S. grocery channels.
