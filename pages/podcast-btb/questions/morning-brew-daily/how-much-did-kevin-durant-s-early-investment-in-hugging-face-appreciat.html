This venture capital play demonstrates the explosive upside potential of backing AI infrastructure companies at their earliest stages.

The financial magnitude of this return is remarkable. For early backers, that confidence translated directly into significant wealth creation.

How does Tesla's autonomous vehicle approach differ from the rest of the industry regarding sensor technology?

Tesla uses only cameras and an AI neural net for its autonomous vehicles, while Waymo and other companies working on autonomy use LiDAR, which is pulsed laser technology that provides three-dimensional mapping.

What is Tesla's current robo-taxi fleet size compared to competitors in the autonomous vehicle market?

Tesla has just over 200 unsupervised robo-taxis in a cluster of cities in Texas and Florida, with most of its robo-taxi network having a human safety driver, while Waymo operates more than 4,000 unsupervised robo-taxis across 14 cities.
