Who is Dr. Jason Hamilton and what is he known for in rhinoplasty?

August 31, 2026 Habits and Hustle · Shani Suissa

← See the Habits and Hustle fiche

Dr. Jason Hamilton is a leading rhinoplasty specialist recognized for his rare expertise in septum perforation repair—a complex procedure that very few surgeons are willing or able to perform. He stands out in the field precisely because this particular sub-specialty is so uncommon that finding a qualified practitioner is extraordinarily difficult.

Septum perforation is one of the most challenging complications in nasal surgery. A perforated septum—a hole in the wall dividing the nasal passages—can occur as a complication of previous rhinoplasty or septoplasty, and repairing it requires specialized technical skill that most general rhinoplasty surgeons either lack or avoid attempting. The rarity of this expertise is what positions Hamilton as an exceptional resource for patients facing this problem.

According to Shani Suissa, who is sharing this recommendation in the Habits and Hustle episode based on her own experience, Hamilton comes highly regarded precisely because he has invested deeply in mastering this uncommon procedure. Suissa herself underwent rhinoplasty, septoplasty, and turbinate reduction, and subsequently developed a septum perforation that required corrective surgery—a situation that made finding the right specialist absolutely critical.

The broader context matters here: social media surgeon visibility does not correlate with actual surgical expertise, as discussed in this podcast. A surgeon's online popularity and price point often bear no relation to their ability to handle complex technical challenges like perforation repair. Hamilton's reputation is built on genuine clinical capability in a highly specialized niche, not on marketing or media presence.

For anyone considering nose surgery or facing post-operative complications, the lesson is clear: selecting the right specialist matters more than selecting the most famous one. As outlined in this episode of Habits and Hustle, choosing based on social media presence, cost, or casual recommendation can lead to outcomes that require expensive and painful corrective surgery. Hamilton's reputation in the rhinoplasty community reflects years of focused technical development in the exact procedure that most surgeons cannot or will not perform.
