How can parents heal their own wounds while simultaneously parenting children who are triggering those wounds?

September 19, 2026 Habits and Hustle · Shefali Tsabary

← See the Habits and Hustle fiche

Conscious parenting cannot be taught—it must be practiced in real time while you are being triggered by your child's behavior. The fact that something is triggering you is actually the signal that an unhealed wound exists. When your child displays anxiety, rebellion, or anger, they are signaling that they need you to stop what you are doing and deeply connect. The trigger itself becomes the doorway to your own healing.

Your child's emotional outburst or challenging behavior is not random. It is a mirror of where you still carry unresolved pain from your own childhood, your own relationships, or your own unmet needs. This is the exact moment when conscious parenting begins—not when everything is calm, but when you feel the heat rising inside you.

As Dr. Shefali Tsabary explains in the Habits and Hustle episode, the impulse to shut down, yell, or punish your child in that moment of triggered emotion is actually a missed opportunity for your own transformation. Instead of reacting from your wound, you can pause and ask yourself: "What in my child's behavior is threatening me? What am I afraid of? What old memory or belief is being activated right now?"

That pause—that breath—is where healing lives. Your child is not trying to trigger you deliberately. They are simply expressing their own developmental needs, and those needs are intersecting with your unhealed places. When you become conscious of that intersection, you begin to heal not just as a parent, but as a person.

"The conscious parent raises themselves so they do not pass on their crap to their children."

The work happens in the real moment, not in theory or self-help books. When your teenage daughter rolls her eyes at you, when your son refuses to listen, when your child's anxiety spirals—those are not failures of your parenting. They are invitations for you to meet yourself, to see where you still need to grow, to heal what was broken in you before they were born. Your child's trigger is your healing portal.

Every time you choose to pause instead of react, to ask instead of demand, to connect instead of control, you are breaking a generational pattern. Your child benefits not because you become perfect, but because you become conscious—and that consciousness is contagious, as discussed in depth in this podcast. They learn that feelings can be metabolized, that mistakes are opportunities, and that love means staying present even when it is hard.
