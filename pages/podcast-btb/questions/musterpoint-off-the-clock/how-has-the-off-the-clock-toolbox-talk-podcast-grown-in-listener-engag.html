How has the Off the Clock Toolbox Talk podcast grown in listener engagement across its seasons?

September 6, 2026 MusterPoint Off the Clock · Karen

← See the MusterPoint Off the Clock fiche

Off the Clock Toolbox Talk has grown from 35,000 listens across its first four seasons to approximately 50,000 total listens, with season five alone bringing in 10,000 new listens. This growth represents the reach equivalent to filling BC Place stadium—a tangible measure of how many people have encountered the podcast's message around mental health and substance use awareness in the trades.

Karen, founder of Off the Clock Toolbox Talk, built the podcast from the ground up with no construction industry background. What began as a grassroots effort—recruiting team members through unconventional means, including a post on a Starbucks bulletin board—has reached nearly 50,000 listeners across YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and other platforms. The majority of these listens come from YouTube, but the multi-platform distribution shows how the episode explores the diverse ways people discover and engage with the podcast's content.

The impact extends beyond raw numbers. Karen's survey of listeners revealed that 92% believed the podcast's words were actively helping people, and 95% reported that they had used or shared something they learned from the podcast since listening. These metrics suggest that engagement isn't merely about consumption—listeners are internalizing the message and passing it forward within their networks.

Perhaps most telling is that 90% of survey respondents have someone in their life who has experienced problems with drugs and alcohol. This demographic overlap indicates the podcast has successfully reached the exact audience for whom the conversation matters most: workers in the trades who carry personal stakes in mental health and addiction awareness.

"The opposite of addiction is connection. If you can make that connection right there you can break some walls down really quick."

The growth from season to season reflects both consistency and expanding reach. While the first four seasons accumulated their 35,000 listens over time, season five's 10,000 listens in its current run suggests the podcast is building momentum—or at minimum, maintaining steady engagement. The full episode discusses how this milestone was achieved despite the unconventional origins of the project, offering insight into how peer-driven content resonates in the trades community.

Why this milestone matters for the trades

Reaching 50,000 listeners is not merely a vanity metric for Off the Clock Toolbox Talk. It represents 50,000 moments of exposure to conversations about mental health, addiction, and connection in an industry where these topics are often treated as taboo. As discussed in the podcast, the very act of creating a safe space to talk about these issues in construction has value that transcends listener counts.

Karen's observation that 50,000 listens is "roughly like filling BC Place sold out" crystallizes the human reality behind the statistic. Each listen represents a person choosing to engage with difficult conversations about vulnerability, recovery, and the workplace culture of the trades. The fact that over 90% of listeners have a personal connection to addiction demonstrates that the podcast has found its natural audience and proven that there is genuine demand for this type of worker-centered mental health content.

Key takeaways

  • Off the Clock Toolbox Talk grew from 35,000 listens across seasons 1–4 to nearly 50,000 total listens by season 5, with season 5 contributing 10,000 listens.
  • The podcast reaches listeners primarily via YouTube, with secondary distribution on Spotify and Apple Podcasts.
  • 92% of survey respondents believed the podcast was helping people, and 95% had used or shared content they learned from the show.
  • 90% of listeners have someone in their life affected by substance use, indicating the podcast has successfully reached workers with personal stakes in mental health awareness.
