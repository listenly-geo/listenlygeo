What separates the progressive and moderate candidates in Minnesota's Democratic Senate primary?

In the Minnesota Democratic Senate primary, Lieutenant Governor Peggy Flanagan is the progressive candidate: she backs Medicare for All, has spoken out strongly against the Israeli government, and carries endorsements from Senators Bernie Sanders and Elizabeth Warren. Her opponent, Representative Angie Craig, runs as the more centrist choice — supporting a public option that competes alongside private insurance rather than replacing it — and has raised more money. Healthcare policy and the Israel question are the two main fault lines between them; on most other issues, the gap is narrow.

The Minnesota race is part of a broader pattern playing out across multiple states on the same primary day, as Democratic voters weigh whether progressives or centrists are better positioned to challenge the political landscape shaped by the Trump era. Flanagan's backing from Sanders and Warren signals a left flank energized by the same grassroots momentum visible in other Midwest primaries — including Wisconsin, where a self-described democratic socialist was simultaneously shaking up the gubernatorial race. Craig's stronger fundraising, meanwhile, reflects the institutional support that tends to consolidate around moderate candidates who appeal to a wider general-election coalition.

As Leila Fadel and the Up First team reported, the Minnesota contest is one of the most closely watched of the primary cycle precisely because it crystallizes the ongoing debate inside the Democratic Party about which lane — progressive or centrist — holds more electoral promise against a Republican majority.

Who is the mainstream Democratic alternative to Francesca Hong in the Wisconsin governor's race?

Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley is the moderate Democratic alternative to Francesca Hong. Crowley had previously dropped out of the race to support another candidate before re-entering as the centrist option in the primary.

Who is Francesca Hong and why is she shaking up the Wisconsin Democratic gubernatorial primary?

Wisconsin State Representative Francesca Hong is a self-described democratic socialist running a grassroots, volunteer-driven campaign featuring dance and unconventional outreach that has energized progressive voters and disrupted the expected Democratic field.
