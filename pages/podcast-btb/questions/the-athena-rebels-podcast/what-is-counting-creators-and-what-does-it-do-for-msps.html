What is Counting Creators, and what does it actually do for MSPs?

Counting Creators is an MSP finance company that handles all outsourced finance functions — from mailbox management right through to compliance work. Founded by Heather McDonnell-Alford, it was deliberately built for the MSP space because MSP finances require a model-specific understanding that generic accountants simply don't have.

Why MSP finance is different Generic accountants tend to treat revenue and costs as straightforward buckets. MSP businesses don't work that way — their financial model has enough structural nuance that without a deeper, sector-specific lens, it's easy to miss what's really going on beneath the surface numbers.

Niched by design — and within five months of launch

McDonnell-Alford didn't drift into the MSP space gradually. She niched Counting Creators into it within five months of starting the business, having originally planned to serve YouTubers and content creators.

The decision was deliberate: she saw a clear gap where MSP owners were being underserved by accountants who lacked sector knowledge. Rather than stay generalist, she moved quickly toward a community where she could make a genuine difference.

From niche firm to recognised voice in the MSP industry

Building the business was only part of the story. McDonnell-Alford also invested heavily in her personal brand — reportedly spending around 15 hours per week on LinkedIn in the early stages — which opened doors to speaking opportunities across major industry events.

Her speaking season opened at the MSP Show 2025 and closed with two stages at Pax8 Amsterdam. She has also spoken at IT Nation London, described as the biggest UK conference in the MSP and IT industry. This visibility, built through consistent presence on LinkedIn, is what brought her profile to the attention of the Athena Rebels Podcast — where she was their first ever external guest.
