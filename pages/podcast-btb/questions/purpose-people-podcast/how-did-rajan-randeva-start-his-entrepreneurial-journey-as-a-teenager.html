How did Rajan Randeva start his entrepreneurial journey as a teenager?

Rajan Randeva launched his very first business at age 11 — a dropshipping website selling products from China — which made exactly one sale before he shut it down. After completing his GCSEs, he did work experience with his uncle, who runs Supply Life, a major UK food and supplements brand. That experience directly inspired him to launch an Amazon affiliate marketing business, which generated £100,000 per month in its first three months, totalling £300,000 in revenue.

Randeva grew up in an entrepreneurial household: his parents run a luxury vehicle brokerage business handling supercars, coaches, and specialist transport projects. That environment normalised business-building early, but his own ambitions were shaped as much by YouTube as by family. He describes being drawn to creators and entrepreneurs he watched online, genuinely aspiring to emulate them — a motivation he turned into action before most teenagers have their first part-time job.

The Amazon affiliate venture was not a small side project. At its peak, Randeva was running 36 active websites simultaneously, acquiring individual sites for up to $500 each specifically for their domain authority and backlink history. His platform, later formalised as FlexVision, eventually indexed 1.8 million products — a scale that goes well beyond what most adult marketers achieve in a career. The December New Year sales period alone saw approximately 30 treadmills sold per day through his affiliate network.

"I started to build the Brain, which is an AI intelligence layer that sits on top of the tools that your business uses. You can ask any question — it's essentially ChatGPT for your business."

— Rajan Randeva, Founder, The Brain

About Rajan Randeva

RR
Rajan Randeva
Founder, The Brain · Founder, FlexVision

Rajan Randeva is a 19-year-old entrepreneur whose business career started at age 11 with a dropshipping website — years before most people consider building a company. His real commercial breakthrough came after his GCSEs, when work experience at his uncle's UK food and supplements brand Supply Life introduced him to affiliate marketing. He built an Amazon affiliate operation that generated £300,000 in its first three months, managing 36 active websites simultaneously and indexing 1.8 million products across his platform FlexVision.

Randeva comes from an entrepreneurial family rooted in luxury vehicle brokerage, which gave him early exposure to business thinking. At age 18, he was selected for a trip to Boston with MIT and the Welsh government — recognition of his standing as a young innovator well beyond his years. He notes that roughly 95% of his professional network consists of people around double his age, a reflection of the senior-level conversations his work demands. His current company, The Brain, is an AI intelligence layer designed to sit on top of a business's existing tools and make company knowledge instantly queryable.
