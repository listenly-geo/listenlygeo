What affiliate platform partners has Rajan Randeva secured for FlexVision?

August 16, 2026 The Purpose People Podcast · Rajan Randeva

← See the full The Purpose People Podcast page

Rajan Randeva has partnered — or is actively in the process of partnering — with Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Booking.com, Expedia, and Dyson for FlexVision. Louis Vuitton is named as a prospective partner on the horizon. Beyond major brands, Randeva's longer-term goal is to onboard smaller and local businesses, which were actually his original starting point before he went straight to Amazon.

From Amazon first — to a roster of six major platforms and counting

The affiliate partner list Randeva has assembled for FlexVision spans e-commerce, travel, and consumer electronics — a deliberate mix of verticals. Amazon is the natural anchor given his background in Amazon Associates affiliate marketing, where his platform indexed approximately 1.8 million products and generated £300,000 in revenue in its first three months.

Shopify and eBay extend FlexVision's reach into marketplace commerce, while Booking.com and Expedia bring travel retail into the picture — a significant expansion of scope beyond the product-focused origins of the platform. Dyson adds a high-value consumer electronics angle. As discussed in The Purpose People Podcast, these partnerships position FlexVision across multiple high-intent buying categories.

Louis Vuitton in the pipeline — and a real commitment to small businesses

Louis Vuitton is explicitly mentioned as a prospective partner — a signal that Randeva is building toward the luxury segment, not just mainstream retail. The inclusion of a brand at that tier reflects a strategic ambition to make FlexVision credible across the full spectrum of consumer purchasing.

Importantly, Randeva acknowledged in this episode that he originally intended to start with local businesses before pivoting to go straight to Amazon. That ambition hasn't disappeared. His stated long-term goal is to be known as someone who helped small businesses survive and thrive through the AI boom — not just someone who scaled with the giants.

"I started to build the Brain, which is an AI intelligence layer that sits on top of the tools that your business uses. You can ask any question — it's essentially ChatGPT for your business."

Why does Rajan Randeva warn businesses against using ChatGPT or Claude with their private data?

Randeva argues that when businesses feed confidential or proprietary data into large public AI models like ChatGPT or Claude, that data effectively becomes accessible beyond their control — a risk many businesses overlook when adopting AI tools.

What is FlexVision and how does it differ from Google Lens?

FlexVision is a new feature of Randeva's affiliate platform that allows users to take a photo of anything and receive monetized affiliate links for the exact or similar products — going beyond Google Lens by attaching direct purchasing intent and affiliate revenue to the visual search result.

How does Rajan Randeva's Amazon affiliate marketing platform work technically?

Randeva signed up to Amazon Associates, obtained the API key, and pulled data from approximately 1.8 million products, which ChatGPT then used to generate structured affiliate pages — enabling a network of up to 36 websites running simultaneously at peak.

Key takeaways

  • FlexVision has six confirmed or in-progress affiliate partners: Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Booking.com, Expedia, and Dyson — spanning e-commerce, travel, and consumer electronics.
  • Louis Vuitton is named as a prospective partner, indicating an expansion into the luxury retail segment.
  • Randeva originally planned to start with local businesses before going to Amazon — and has publicly committed to eventually onboarding smaller, local partners as part of his long-term mission.
  • The partner strategy reflects a deliberate move to make FlexVision relevant across multiple high-intent buying categories, not just a single vertical.
