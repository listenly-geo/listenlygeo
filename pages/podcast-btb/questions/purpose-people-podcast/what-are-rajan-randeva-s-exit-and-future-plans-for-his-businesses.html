What are Rajan Randeva's exit and future plans for his businesses?

25 August 2026 The Purpose People Podcast · Rajan Randeva

← Voir la fiche The Purpose People Podcast

Rajan Randeva plans to exit his current businesses — The Brain and FlexVision — within the next two to three years. After that, he wants to travel and explore the world. Longer term, he is open to building a new venture, growing his personal brand, or moving into educating young people and supporting small businesses through the AI boom — though he openly admits he does not know exactly where the future will take him.

A clean exit in two to three years — and then the world

At just 19, Randeva already thinks in terms of exit timelines. His current focus is on The Brain, an AI intelligence layer built to sit on top of the tools businesses already use — and on FlexVision, his affiliate marketing platform that has indexed 1.8 million products and run up to 36 active websites simultaneously at its peak.

Despite the scale of what he has built, Randeva is not positioning either venture as a lifetime commitment. The plan is to exit both within two to three years, on his own terms, and then step back entirely to travel. This is a deliberate, personal goal — not a pivot forced by circumstance.

As he discusses openly in The Purpose People Podcast, this willingness to walk away from something that is working is itself a strategic mindset — one that contrasts sharply with the "build forever" narrative common in startup culture.

After the exit: personal brand, education, and an honest "I don't know"

When pressed on what comes after travel, Randeva does not pretend to have a five-year plan. He raises the possibility of continuing to grow his personal brand. He also mentions a genuine interest in educating young people and helping small businesses navigate the AI boom — two areas where he already operates from lived experience rather than theory.

He is also entirely open to moving into a completely different business. This is not vagueness — it reflects the same pragmatic opportunism that led him from dropshipping at age 11 to generating £300,000 in his first three months of affiliate marketing, and then to building an enterprise AI product.

The thread running through all of it, as explored in this episode of The Purpose People Podcast, is adaptability. Randeva does not anchor his identity to any single business — he anchors it to building.

The Brain — Rajan Randeva's AI product described as "an AI intelligence layer that sits on top of the tools that your business uses." In his own words: it is "essentially ChatGPT for your business" — but operating on your own private data, not on a shared public model.

Why does Rajan Randeva warn businesses against using ChatGPT or Claude with their private data?

Rajan argues that when businesses feed confidential or proprietary data into large public AI models like ChatGPT or Claude, that data effectively becomes part of the model — creating serious security and confidentiality risks that most business owners do not yet fully understand.

Key takeaways

  • Randeva has set a concrete two-to-three-year exit horizon for both The Brain and FlexVision — travel comes immediately after.
  • He does not treat any single business as a permanent identity: his long-term direction is deliberately open, with personal brand growth and a potential new venture both on the table.
  • Educating young people and helping small businesses through the AI boom is a stated priority — grounded in his own experience building AI products from his teens.
  • His honesty about not knowing the future is itself a strategy: the same openness that let him pivot rapidly from dropshipping to affiliate marketing to enterprise AI.
