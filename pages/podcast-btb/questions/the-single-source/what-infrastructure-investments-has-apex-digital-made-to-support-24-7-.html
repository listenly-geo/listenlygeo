What infrastructure investments has Apex Digital made to support 24/7 tokenized Treasury operations?

August 21, 2026 The Single Source · Tom Bennett & Angie Walker

← Voir la fiche The Single Source

To support always-on digital transfer agency capabilities, Apex Digital undertook a major infrastructure upgrade: it opened new large-scale support centers — including a significant facility in Hyderabad — and completely redesigned its operating model. Automated onboarding, automated KYC and AML processes, and scaled investor community management were all built in response to the fundamentally different velocity and cadence that tokenized Treasury services demand compared to traditional fund administration.

Digital Transfer Agency: A transfer agency function adapted for tokenized assets — handling investor onboarding, record-keeping, and transaction settlement on a continuous basis rather than during traditional business hours. Tokenized Treasury instruments require this capability to be available around the clock, seven days a week, because the underlying blockchain networks never close.

From Traditional Hours to Always-On: Why Apex Digital Had to Rebuild from the Ground Up

The shift to tokenized Treasury operations is not simply a technology upgrade — it is a fundamental change in operating rhythm. Traditional fund administration runs on business-day cycles. Tokenized instruments, by contrast, settle instantly on-chain at any hour. Apex Digital recognized early that serving this market required an entirely new operational backbone, a point discussed in depth in The Single Source.

Angie Walker, Global Head of Commercialization for Apex Digital, frames this as a direct consequence of the product itself. US Treasuries tokenization grew by 500% in less than two years. That kind of acceleration leaves no room for legacy processes — the infrastructure has to be ready before the volume arrives, not after.

Opening a major support center in Hyderabad, alongside other worldwide locations, gave Apex Digital the geographic coverage needed to maintain genuine 24/7 service. No single time zone can staff a round-the-clock operation alone. The Hyderabad facility is part of a deliberate global footprint strategy, not a cost-reduction exercise.

Automated KYC, AML, and Onboarding — The Three Levers Apex Digital Pulled

Beyond physical infrastructure, Apex Digital automated three core processes that had previously been manual bottlenecks: investor onboarding, KYC (Know Your Customer) verification, and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) screening. Each of these processes, if left manual, would create a friction point incompatible with the speed of tokenized markets. As covered in this episode of The Single Source, the redesign was holistic — not a patch applied to existing systems.

Scaled investor community management was also added to the operating model. This matters because the investor base for tokenized products skews significantly younger — the average active retail investor in DeFi is approximately 27 years old, compared to the early 50s for traditional fund investors. Managing that community requires different tools, different communication channels, and different response times.

The scale of Apex Digital's overall tokenization push gives context to these investments. The firm grew from 31 tokenization projects in flight to 557 in a single year — approximately 2,000% growth. Supporting that pipeline without a rebuilt infrastructure would have been operationally impossible, a point that Tom Bennett and Walker both make clear in The Single Source.

"Distribution is king. Everything we do is actually all about distribution — whether that's distribution onto a chain or multiple chains, distribution to a centralized digital venue, a decentralized digital venue, or to an aggregator."

Bennett's emphasis on distribution as the central organizing principle reinforces why the infrastructure investments described by Walker matter so much. An always-on transfer agency capability is, at its core, a distribution capability — the ability to onboard investors, verify their identity, and settle their transactions at any point in the global trading day.
