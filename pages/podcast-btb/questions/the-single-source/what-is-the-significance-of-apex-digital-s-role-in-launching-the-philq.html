What role did Apex Digital play in launching PhilQ, Fidelity International's first triple-A rated tokenized liquidity fund?

Apex Digital was part of the team that launched PhilQ, described as the first-ever triple-A rated liquidity fund rated by Moody's, issued by Fidelity International approximately one month before the episode was recorded. Angie Walker positions it as a landmark example of tokenized Treasury fund issuance and confirms that capital secured into the fund is already growing very quickly.

The PhilQ launch is significant not just as an isolated product milestone, but as evidence that institutional-grade tokenized funds are arriving with the credibility markers — top-tier rating agency approval, a globally recognized issuer, and a specialist fund services partner — that the broader market has been waiting for. Walker's framing is deliberate: this is a proof point, not a pilot.

The timing matters too. US Treasuries tokenization grew by 500% in less than two years according to data cited in the same episode, and global real-world asset (RWA) tokenization has just exceeded approximately $25 billion across the RWA community. PhilQ enters a market with clear momentum, and Apex Digital's involvement as part of the launch team places it at the center of what Walker calls a defining moment for tokenized fund issuance. What is a triple-A rated liquidity fund?A triple-A rating from Moody's is the highest possible credit quality designation, indicating an extremely low risk of default and maximum stability of capital. Applying this rating to a tokenized liquidity fund — as with PhilQ — signals to institutional investors that the on-chain product meets the same rigorous standards as the most conservative traditional money market instruments. It is a key credibility threshold for mainstream adoption.

Tom Bennett Global Head of Fintech Apex Digital

Tom Bennett is Global Head of Fintech at Apex Digital, where he leads the firm's fintech strategy and market positioning in one of the fastest-moving corners of financial services. In this episode, he appears alongside Angie Walker, Global Head of Commercialization for Apex Digital, as co-authors of a major tokenization survey report produced in partnership with Mergermarket. The report draws on interviews with 100 senior managers at funds and asset managers — ranging from $500 million to $5 billion in AUM — across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC, giving Bennett and Walker an unusually broad empirical basis for their observations. Their joint commentary on the PhilQ launch carries institutional weight: Apex Digital administers approximately 16% of all global crypto fund administration and brings 13 years of experience in the sector, making Bennett a credible voice on the practical realities of tokenized fund infrastructure and distribution.

What is Apex Digital's track record in crypto fund administration, and what notable clients does it serve?

Apex has been active in crypto fund administration for 13 years and represents approximately 16% of all crypto fund administration globally, serving a significant range of institutional clients across the market.

What is Apex Digital's Apex Invest platform and what scale has it reached?

Apex Invest is a marketplace designed to match fund managers with new types of investors. According to Tom Bennett, the platform currently hosts over 100 funds and approximately $40 trillion of allocated capital.

What types of assets are currently being tokenized on the Apex Digital platform, and what emerging categories are gaining traction?

Apex Digital is tokenizing a diverse range of assets including real estate, private credit, venture capital, hedge funds, and SPVs. Angie Walker notes that emerging categories are gaining meaningful traction alongside these established asset classes.
