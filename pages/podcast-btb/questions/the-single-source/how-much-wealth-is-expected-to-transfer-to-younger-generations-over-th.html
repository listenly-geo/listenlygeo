How much wealth is expected to transfer to younger generations over the next two decades, and what does this mean for tokenized assets?

According to Angie Walker, Global Head of Commercialization at Apex Digital, $124 trillion will be passed down through the generations over the coming two decades. She argues this transfer will fundamentally reshape demand for investment access — driving younger recipients to expect broader entry into quality assets, such as private markets, far earlier in life, and in a digitally native way that matches how they already hold and transact value.

$124T Wealth expected to transfer to younger generations over 20 years
42% Of surveyed managers cite broader investor access as primary driver for tokenization

The significance of this figure goes beyond its scale. Walker's argument is structural: the incoming recipients of this wealth already predominantly hold digital wallets. They are not going to adapt to legacy fund infrastructure — they will expect investment products to meet them where they are. This is precisely why 42% of the 100 senior managers surveyed by Apex Digital and Mergermarket identified broadening investor access as the primary driver of tokenization, making it the single most cited motivation in the study.

"Distribution is king. Everything we do is actually all about distribution — whether that's distribution onto a chain or multiple chains, distribution to a centralized digital venue, a decentralized digital venue, or to an aggregator." — Angie Walker, Global Head of Commercialization, Apex Digital

This generational shift sits alongside already accelerating market momentum. Global real-world asset tokenization has just exceeded approximately $25 billion, and US Treasuries tokenization grew by 500% in less than two years — establishing proof of concept for the infrastructure that the next generation of investors will inherit and use. The $124 trillion wealth transfer is not a distant hypothesis; it is the demand-side force that gives tokenized private markets their long-term growth thesis. What is the generational gap between traditional fund investors and active digital wallet investors, and why does it matter for tokenization?
Angie Walker highlights that the average traditional fund investor is in their early 50s, while the average active retail investor in DeFi is around 27 — a gap that makes digitally native investment formats a necessity, not a preference, for the incoming generation of wealth holders.
What is the current scale of real-world asset tokenization globally, and how fast is it growing?
According to Angie Walker, global tokenization of real-world assets has just exceeded approximately $25 billion across the RWA community. US Treasuries tokenization grew by 500% in less than two years, signalling rapid mainstream acceleration.
Why did Apex Digital commission a tokenization survey with Mergermarket, and what questions was it designed to answer?
Apex Digital partnered with Mergermarket to examine how asset and fund managers are approaching tokenization in practice. The survey aimed to determine the current state of adoption, identify leading use cases, and surface the remaining barriers preventing a move from early adopters to mainstream deployment.
