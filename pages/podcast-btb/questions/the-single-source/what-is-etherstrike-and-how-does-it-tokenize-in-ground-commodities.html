What is EtherStrike and how does it tokenize in-ground commodities?

August 25, 2026 The Single Source · Matt and Kevin Hamilton

← See the full The Single Source podcast page

EtherStrike is a platform that tokenizes in-ground commodities — oil fields still beneath the surface, unmined gold reserves, untapped natural gas — by creating tokens that represent the actual physical commodity, not a paper-based financial instrument. Each token is backed one-to-one with a real unit of that resource: one token equals one barrel of oil, one ounce of gold, or one standard cubic foot of gas. The founders describe their approach as putting the "real" in real-world assets, replacing derivatives and securities with direct commodity backing.

From a gap in commodity financing to blockchain tokenization

Matt and Kevin Hamilton founded EtherStrike at the intersection of two distinct careers. Matt Hamilton works on the producer side of oil and gas, where he evaluates financing mechanisms for commodity projects. Kevin Hamilton has been in crypto since 2012, navigating multiple bear and bull market cycles. The combination surfaced a clear gap: non-producing natural resources — reserves that haven't yet been extracted — have very limited financing options available to them.

As Matt and Kevin Hamilton explain in The Single Source, most financing mechanisms they encountered on the producer side either arrive too late in the project lifecycle or rely on paper-based instruments that don't reflect the underlying asset's true value. EtherStrike was built to fill that gap, specifically targeting pre-production projects where investor access has historically been the hardest.

Their first live tokenization — called Asphalt Bluff South — covers 20 million barrels of asphalt binder located in Utah. Each of those barrels is represented by exactly one token, issued at a one-to-one ratio. This project is the clearest illustration of what "in-ground" tokenization actually means in practice: the asset exists physically, it's recoverable, and the token represents the net proceeds from that recoverable reserve.

Dynamic Reserve Resource Unit (DRRU)
The DRRU is EtherStrike's security token. It represents a one-to-one match with a specific in-ground commodity — whether a barrel of oil, an ounce of gold, or a standard cubic foot of natural gas. Its value is tied to the net proceeds from that recoverable reserve, and its price floor is defined by the net asset value of the commodity in the ground — not by market speculation.

A tiered entry structure designed around the project lifecycle

EtherStrike structures investor access in tiers that follow the natural progression of a commodity project: from discovery, through pre-production, into production. Investors who enter early — at the discovery or pre-production stage — can acquire tokens at a properly priced discount, then benefit from tier compression as the project matures.

This structure is a direct response to a real market problem: early-stage commodity exposure is extremely difficult for most investors to access. As discussed in this episode of The Single Source, the tiered model is described as the biggest value driver in EtherStrike's approach — giving investors a path from pre-production to production that was previously unavailable through traditional instruments.

The choice to focus exclusively on non-producing resources was deliberate. Producing assets already have established financing channels. The gap — and therefore the opportunity — sits in the earlier stages, where blockchain-based tokenization can open access to investors who would otherwise have no entry point.

"The floor of a traditional token is zero. There is no true floor, whereas for our tokens, the underlying asset is actually what the token represents, and so the floor is the net asset value of the commodity in the ground."

The quote above captures the core distinction EtherStrike makes between its tokens and conventional crypto assets. A standard token can theoretically fall to zero because it has no physical backing. A DRRU token, by contrast, is anchored to a commodity that physically exists in the ground — giving it a price floor defined by what that reserve is actually worth. Key takeaways

  • EtherStrike's tokens (DRRUs) are backed one-to-one with a specific in-ground commodity — one token equals one barrel of oil, one ounce of gold, or one standard cubic foot of gas.
  • The platform targets non-producing natural resources exclusively, because that is where financing gaps are largest and early investor access is most restricted.
  • Asphalt Bluff South in Utah — 20 million barrels of asphalt binder — is EtherStrike's first live tokenization, issued at a strict one-to-one ratio per barrel.
  • The DRRU token's price floor is defined by the net asset value of the in-ground commodity, not market speculation — a structural difference from conventional crypto tokens.
