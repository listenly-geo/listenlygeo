How does Krista approach hiring and team building at Anderson?

September 2, 2026 Executive Wins Podcast · Krista Rabideau

← See the Executive Wins Podcast fiche

Every new hire at Anderson—from junior administration to senior staff—completes a Strengths Deployment Inventory assessment to evaluate personality type and role fit. While this test does not determine hiring decisions alone, it provides crucial information to ensure candidates align with the specific demands of their role.

Building teams through authentic alignment

Krista Rabideau's hiring philosophy centers on matching individual strengths to organizational needs rather than forcing candidates into predetermined molds. The Strengths Deployment Inventory serves as a diagnostic tool at every level, creating a shared language about how team members work best and where they add the most value.

This approach reflects a broader commitment to authenticity in the workplace. As Rabideau has explained in the Executive Wins Podcast, creating space for people to be themselves—with their unique backgrounds, perspectives, and working styles—drives stronger results and deeper engagement across the firm.

When Rabideau joined Anderson seven years ago, the firm had only 23 to 24 people. The systematic use of personality assessments became part of a larger culture shift designed to turn a historically owner-managed boutique firm into a scaled organization. This foundation matters: without clarity on who works best where, scaling becomes chaotic.

"You can receive success, but you will never feel successful until you're actually yourself."

The full episode reveals how Rabideau weaves personality assessment results into broader team conversations, exploring the specific management practices that have kept Anderson's culture intact while tripling revenue.

Key takeaways

  • The Strengths Deployment Inventory is administered to every new hire, regardless of level, to assess fit and working style.
  • Assessment results inform role design and team dynamics but do not make the final hiring decision alone.
  • Authentic team building, where people can be themselves, is foundational to scaling an accounting firm from 24 to over 70 people.
  • Personality clarity reduces friction and accelerates onboarding by aligning individual strengths with organizational needs.
