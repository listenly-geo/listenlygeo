How did Krista shape Anderson's founding mission and values?

2026-09-01 Executive Wins Podcast · Krista Rabideau

← See the Executive Wins Podcast fiche

Krista established "building trusted relationships and custom solutions" as Anderson's core mission when she joined as Managing Director. This foundational statement remains unchanged today and drives all hiring and strategic decisions at the firm.

Defining purpose from day one

When Krista Rabideau joined Anderson seven years ago, the boutique accounting firm was historically run as an owner-managed business with no clear shared vision. One of her first orders of business was defining the firm's vision and core values, recognizing that scaling a firm requires something more than individual leadership alone.

The mission she articulated—building trusted relationships and custom solutions—became the north star that shaped how the team grew. This wasn't a marketing tagline; it became the lens through which hiring, client selection, and strategic initiatives were evaluated. As Krista explains in the episode, this foundation gave the firm permission to say no to work that didn't align with those values.

A mission that scaled with the firm

Krista's willingness to be authentically herself while building this mission resonated with the team. Despite coming from an arts background—with piercings, tattoos, and a stage management past—she brought a non-traditional perspective to an industry known for conformity. This authenticity became part of Anderson's culture itself.

The firm tripled in size over seven years not by chasing every opportunity, but by staying true to the mission Krista defined at the beginning. Every person hired, every client taken on, and every strategic decision has been filtered through whether it serves the core purpose of building trusted relationships and delivering custom solutions.

"You can receive success, but you will never feel successful until you're actually yourself."

Krista grew up in the arts world and started her career in concert production and stage management. While at Grant MacEwan University, a professor named Grant McEwen encouraged her to pursue accounting, recognizing her potential beyond her arts background.

Key takeaways

  • Krista defined Anderson's mission as "building trusted relationships and custom solutions"—a statement that remains the core decision-making filter for the entire organization.
  • This mission-driven approach allowed the firm to triple in size over seven years by staying selective about clients and hires rather than pursuing growth at any cost.
  • Authenticity in leadership—being yourself rather than conforming to industry stereotypes—became embedded in the firm's culture and reinforced the mission at every level.
  • A clear, shared vision enables scaling because decisions are no longer made by one person, but by the entire team using the same foundational values.
