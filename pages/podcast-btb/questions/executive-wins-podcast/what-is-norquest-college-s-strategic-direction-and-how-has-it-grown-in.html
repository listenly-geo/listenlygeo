How has NorQuest College transformed its strategic vision and student base?

September 25, 2026 Executive Wins Podcast · Carolyn Campbell

← See the Executive Wins Podcast fiche

NorQuest College launched its NorQuest 2030 strategic plan in 2021 with the inclusive mission "We are who we include," and has since refreshed this vision. The college has experienced explosive growth, with domestic student enrollment up 79% over the past decade, now serving approximately 10,000 full-time students and 17,000 total students—far exceeding the 5,000 full-time capacity of its original downtown campus.

This transformation reflects a deliberate shift toward accessibility and social impact. As discussed in this episode with Carolyn Campbell, NorQuest's President and CEO, the college's core purpose centers on transforming lives. When one life transforms, it ripples outward—families change, communities strengthen, and entire regions benefit from a more skilled workforce.

From a Single Downtown Campus to a Multi-Site Regional Hub

The scale of growth required NorQuest to expand its physical footprint dramatically. The original downtown campus was designed to accommodate 5,000 full-time students; today, the college serves more than double that capacity with its 10,000 full-time enrollment. This growth has driven strategic acquisitions and developments that extend NorQuest's reach across Edmonton and into the broader Alberta region.

The college now operates multiple facilities and continues to invest in new infrastructure, including the Career Skills Centre, which received $4 million in planning and development funding from the Government of Alberta. These expansions underscore a commitment to meet learners where they are and provide pathways for frontline workers, trades professionals, and career changers across diverse sectors.

Beyond enrollment numbers, the episode reveals deeper context on how NorQuest is positioning itself as a critical workforce development engine for Alberta, serving an education district that serves approximately 30,000 students daily across NorQuest College and MacEwen University combined.

"People, clarity of vision, and execution. We have the wonderful people that work here at NorQuest. They're exquisitely clear on their purpose."

For those curious about how NorQuest is physically reimagining its campuses to support this growth, the college's major campus transformations offer a concrete example of strategic planning in action.

What major physical campus transformations has NorQuest College undertaken as part of its master plan?

NorQuest College demolished an old building on the southeast corner and created Mianook Park, then acquired the Champs building on 109th Street and the Calder Bateman building to expand its physical footprint and capacity.
