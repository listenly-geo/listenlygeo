How should you evaluate an investment before committing?

2 Jul 2026 Prosperity Podcast with Nicole Bremner · Nicole Bremner

← See the Prosperity Podcast with Nicole Bremner fiche

Before committing to any investment, spend at least one hour running through the SAFER system—a five-step framework that asks you to evaluate strategy, acquisition, funding, exit options, and repeatability. This hour of structured analysis will prove invaluable long-term, and it is better to regret the projects you miss than those you invest in without proper consideration.

The SAFER system is Nicole Bremner's proven answer to a question she learned the hard way: what separates successful investors from those who face financial ruin? In the Prosperity Podcast, she walks through the exact framework designed to catch blind spots before your capital is at risk.

A fail-safe system born from failure

This system was not created in an ivory tower—it was forged in real pain. Nicole Bremner built and then nearly lost a multi-million pound property portfolio, an experience that cost her not just money but her marriage, home, friends, and sense of identity. That crisis became the blueprint for the SAFER method, a checkpoint system intended to prevent others from walking the same path.

The five questions at the heart of the system are not complex; they are deliberately straightforward. Do you have a clear strategy? Are you acquiring wisely? Can you fund this safely? Have you planned multiple exit options? Can you review and repeat what works? As Bremner outlines in the episode, each stage was built to catch the emotional and analytical traps that trap even experienced investors.

It's better to regret the projects you miss than those you invest in without proper consideration.

The one-hour time investment is not a suggestion—it is the minimum threshold before any capital moves. This single hour of disciplined questioning creates a clear separation between reactive investment decisions and intentional ones. For investors starting with 5,000 pounds or 500,000 pounds, the SAFER framework applies equally, scaled to your category and capacity.

You may wonder whether this level of scrutiny slows down opportunity. It does. That is precisely the point. Bremner explains in the full episode how this deliberate pace protects your capital and your peace of mind, drawing on real-life examples from her own portfolio decisions and the losses she witnessed in others.

Why is professional guidance essential for investment and financial management?

The rules on personal finances are complex and the fines for getting it wrong are high. Just as no one would operate on themselves without medical training, financial decisions of significant scale deserve professional oversight.

How should investors categorize themselves for tailored investment strategies?

Investors are placed into one of three categories—small, medium, or large—based on current financial position. Strategies differ significantly across categories, whether you start with 5,000 pounds or 500,000 pounds.

What personal losses did Nicole Bremner experience that led to developing this investment system?

Nicole Bremner lost her marriage, home, friends, and identity after nearly losing everything in her property portfolio. This years-long recovery, while managing ongoing legal battles, became the foundation for the SAFER system.

Key takeaways

  • Dedicate at least one full hour to the SAFER system before any investment decision—strategy, acquisition, funding, exit options, and repeatability.
  • The system is designed to catch blind spots and emotional traps that even experienced investors miss.
  • Missing an opportunity is preferable to committing capital without proper due diligence and structured evaluation.
  • The SAFER framework applies to all investor categories, from those starting with small capital to those with substantial portfolios.
