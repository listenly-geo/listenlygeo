What corporate structure should medium-pot property investors choose?

5 September 2026 Prosperity Podcast with Nicole Bremner · Nicole Bremner

← See the Prosperity Podcast with Nicole Bremner fiche

For investors with £250,000 to £500,000, setting up a holding company—a limited company that owns each individual project or special purpose vehicle—is the most tax-efficient structure. This approach limits personal liability if projects encounter difficulties and allows returns to be distributed as dividends.

Why a holding company works for medium-pot investors

A holding company sits at the top of your investment structure, owning each individual project through separate special purpose vehicles. This separation means each project is ring-fenced, so if one property development or investment encounters legal or financial problems, your personal assets and other projects remain protected.

The tax efficiency comes from how you extract returns. As Nicole Bremner explains in the episode, dividends paid through a limited company structure are typically taxed more favorably than salary or other income distributions, particularly for investors working with professional accountants who understand property investment.

Building your investment power team

At the medium-pot level, your financial complexity has grown enough to justify dedicated expertise. A property-focused accountant becomes essential to your advisory team, not optional. They'll help you structure each new project, advise on when to use special purpose vehicles versus other arrangements, and ensure your holding company strategy aligns with your overall investment goals.

This professional guidance on corporate structure is discussed in detail in the full podcast episode, where Bremner emphasizes that tax and liability decisions at this scale should never be made alone.

Key takeaways

  • A holding company structure is recommended for investors with £250,000–£500,000 to deploy.
  • Each project operates through a separate special purpose vehicle owned by the holding company, limiting liability per project.
  • Dividends paid through a limited company are typically more tax-efficient than other income distributions.
  • A property-focused accountant should be added to your advisory team to structure investments and optimize tax strategy.

What practical action should investors take before committing to any investment decision?

Investors should spend at least an hour running through the SAFER system, asking themselves five key questions about strategy, acquisition, funding, and other critical factors before proceeding with any investment.

Why is professional guidance essential for investment and financial management?

The rules on personal finances are complex and the fines for getting it wrong are high. Professional advisors help navigate tax implications, ensure regulatory compliance, and protect your assets.
