What's the size of September 2026 hard maturities in the securitized market?

September 24, 2026 The TreppWire Podcast: A Commercial Real Estate Show · Stephen Bushbaum

← See The TreppWire Podcast: A Commercial Real Estate Show fiche

$2.74 billion in securitized mortgage debt has exhausted all extension options and reaches hard maturity in September 2026. Of this cohort, approximately 50% comprises office properties, and roughly half carries debt yields below 8%—a critical threshold when refinancing at today's market rates.

Hard maturities represent the ultimate pressure point in commercial real estate finance. Unlike soft maturities that allow borrowers additional breathing room through extensions, hard maturities force immediate action: either refinance at current market rates, restructure the debt, or accept a potential default. The September 2026 maturity wall tracked by Trep signals that the synchronization of property performance, capital conditions, and credit quality will reach a critical juncture for nearly $3 billion in loans.

The composition of this maturity cohort reveals the depth of sector-specific stress. With over 50% concentrated in office properties—a sector already grappling with structural vacancy pressures and weakening fundamentals—the September maturity becomes a convergence point for leverage and weakened asset performance. Borrowers holding loans originated in the low-rate environment with yields below 8% face particularly acute pressure, as today's median coupon of 6.44% for new refinancings provides little room for pricing spread.

"Maturity is the great synchronization event that forces all of these clocks to come into sync."

The $2.74 billion figure carries additional weight when contextualized against broader market dynamics. While this represents a discrete maturity window, the loans within it already show signs of credit stress: over 50% are in special servicing, indicating either payment default or covenant violation. This overlap—hard maturity combined with active servicing status—leaves minimal room for negotiation and amplifies the urgency of resolution.

Understanding these maturities matters beyond September 2026 itself. As the episode explores in detail, the outcomes of near-term maturity events shape capital market confidence, pricing, and access for subsequent cohorts rolling into 2027 and beyond.
