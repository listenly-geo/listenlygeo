What is the central theme of World War II with Tom Hanks?

The central theme of World War II with Tom Hanks is the human cost of the war. The 20-part series makes the case that six years of global conflict revealed humanity at its absolute worst — but equally at its absolute best, with individuals willing to sacrifice their lives so that others may live.

Rather than approaching the Second World War as a political or military chronicle, the series foregrounds individual stories. The guiding premise is that every single person caught up in those six dark years had a story, and that collectively, these are the stories that make us who we are today.

This human-first lens is what separates the series from a conventional history. You can explore the full series on Listenly to hear how Hanks and the team translate this theme across all 20 episodes, covering a conflict so vast that no part of the globe was untouched and no life left unchanged.
