How many episodes does the World War II podcast with Tom Hanks have?

The World War II with Tom Hanks podcast series consists of 20 parts. It spans the full six years of the Second World War, with the explicit premise that no part of the globe was untouched and no life unchanged.

Twenty episodes is a deliberate scale — matched to what Hanks and his team describe as the largest event in human history. The series sets out to deliver the ultimate account of that conflict, moving through the war's six years with individual human stories at its center.
