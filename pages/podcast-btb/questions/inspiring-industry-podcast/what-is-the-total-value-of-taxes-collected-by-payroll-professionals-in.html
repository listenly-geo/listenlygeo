How much in taxes do UK payroll professionals collect annually?

9 September 2026 Inspiring Industry Podcast · Jason Davenport

← See the Inspiring Industry Podcast fiche

Payroll professionals in the UK collected £378 billion in taxes during the 2022 tax year, representing a massive contribution to government revenues and the funding of public services. This figure underscores the critical economic role these professionals play across the country.

The scale of this contribution becomes even clearer when you consider the scope of the workforce involved. There are over 100,000 people working in payroll across the UK, each one responsible for ensuring accurate tax collection, compliance, and the proper administration of employee compensation. It is a role that demands precision, training, and ongoing support.

According to Jason Davenport, CEO of the Chartered Institute of Payroll Professionals, this contribution was formally acknowledged at the highest levels of government. The Chancellor of the Exchequer, Jeremy Hunt, issued a thank-you to payroll professionals recognizing their vital work in maintaining the integrity of the UK tax system and supporting the economy.

Why payroll professionals deserve recognition

The responsibility placed on payroll teams extends far beyond simple number-crunching. Each decision, each calculation, and each process they manage directly impacts the financial health of businesses, the livelihoods of employees, and the resources available for public services. Getting payroll right is non-negotiable, as errors can cascade through entire organizations and create compliance issues.

Yet these professionals often work behind the scenes without the recognition afforded to other business functions. As explored in this episode, Davenport emphasizes that payroll teams must be properly trained, qualified, and supported by their employers. The stakes are simply too high for anything less than full organizational commitment to their development and wellbeing.

"Payroll professionals should be celebrated every day. Is your payroll team trained and qualified and are they supported properly?"
