How is ED Procurement Consulting Services addressing opportunities in Namibia's growing procurement landscape?

8 September 2026 The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast

← See the The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast fiche

ED Procurement Consulting Services has expanded its service portfolio to address the rapid growth of Namibia's procurement and investment opportunities across multiple sectors. Led by Veronica Munongena, the company offers procurement advisory, bid support, training, supplier development, and oil and gas procurement readiness—with particular focus on helping local SMEs and suppliers strengthen their compliance, governance, financial, and operational capabilities.

The company draws on deep expertise across public procurement, mining, telecommunications, and upstream oil and gas, positioning itself to serve businesses navigating Namibia's increasingly complex regulatory and competitive environment. This expansion comes as the country's oil and gas sector experiences accelerated development and local content requirements intensify.

Bridging the gap for SMEs and local suppliers

Namibian businesses entering or scaling within the oil and gas supply chain face significant compliance and operational hurdles. ED Procurement's service model addresses this by providing hands-on support in governance structures, financial readiness, and operational systems that procurement processes demand. As detailed in the episode, this targeted approach helps local suppliers compete effectively while meeting the stringent requirements of major operators and contractors.

The timing reflects broader momentum in Namibia's energy landscape. With major projects advancing—including GALP's Mopane exploration and appraisal campaign expected in the fourth quarter of 2026—procurement demand from local businesses will only intensify. Veronica Munongena and her team position ED Procurement to serve as a critical enabler for businesses seeking to participate in this growth.

Strategic services across the sector value chain

The range of offerings—from procurement advisory to oil and gas readiness training—reflects the varied needs across Namibia's economic ecosystem. Bid support and supplier development services directly accelerate capability building, while training and advisory work strengthen the institutional foundation that sustainable participation requires. This multifaceted model demonstrates recognition that procurement success is not a single transaction, but a continuous capability exercise.

For businesses wondering where Namibia's energy sector is heading and how to position themselves, the episode also highlights the Namibia Local Content Conference and Exhibition 2026, scheduled for 19–23 October in Windhoek—a key platform for connecting with industry players and exploring technology-driven local content opportunities.

Key takeaways

  • ED Procurement Consulting Services covers procurement advisory, bid support, training, supplier development, and oil and gas procurement readiness.
  • The company leverages expertise across public procurement, mining, telecommunications, and upstream oil and gas sectors.
  • Core focus is helping SMEs and local suppliers strengthen compliance, governance, financial, and operational systems.
  • Expansion responds to accelerating procurement demand as Namibia's oil and gas portfolio advances.
