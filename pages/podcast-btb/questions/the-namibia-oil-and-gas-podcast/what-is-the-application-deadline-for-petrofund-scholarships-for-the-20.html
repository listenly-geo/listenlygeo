When do you need to apply for PetroFund master's scholarships for the 2027 academic year?

September 22, 2026 The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast

← See The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast fiche

The application deadline for PetroFund scholarships for the 2027 academic year is October 30th, 2026. These scholarships are designed exclusively for master's programs with the goal of building specialized expertise in Namibia's upstream petroleum industry.

PetroFund represents a strategic investment in human capital development for Namibia's oil and gas sector. As the country's petroleum industry continues to mature and expand, the need for highly trained specialists in upstream operations becomes increasingly critical. The scholarships provide opportunities for Namibian professionals to pursue advanced qualifications that directly support the nation's energy sector growth.

If you're considering applying, this episode of The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast covers the full details of the PetroFund initiative and other skills-development opportunities shaping Namibia's energy workforce.

Master's-level programs funded through PetroFund focus on technical and operational expertise required for deepwater exploration, production engineering, subsea systems, and related upstream disciplines. The program reflects Namibia's commitment to ensuring local professionals are equipped to lead and manage the country's petroleum resources.

Mark your calendar for October 30th, 2026 to ensure your application reaches the selection committee on time. Early preparation is advisable, as competitive scholarships of this caliber typically attract strong candidate pools.
