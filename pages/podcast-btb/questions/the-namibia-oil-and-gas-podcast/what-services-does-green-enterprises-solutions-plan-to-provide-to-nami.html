What ICT infrastructure will Green Enterprises Solutions provide to Namibia's emerging oil and gas sector?

17 September 2026 The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast

← See The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast fiche

Green Enterprises Solutions plans to deliver turnkey ICT infrastructure that connects energy companies operating in Namibia directly with their international headquarters, spanning connectivity, cybersecurity, data sovereignty, and cloud integration. The service package includes scalable digital infrastructure alongside local technical support and training, addressing critical needs as Namibia's petroleum industry transitions from exploration into full production and development phases.

Bridging Local Operations With Global Networks

As Namibia moves toward major development investments in its oil and gas sector, Green Enterprises Solutions has positioned ICT infrastructure as foundational to operational success. The company recognizes that energy operators require seamless, secure data pathways between onshore and offshore facilities in Namibia and their corporate operations elsewhere in the world.

The infrastructure will handle multiple critical functions simultaneously: ensuring reliable connectivity across remote offshore and onshore sites, protecting sensitive operational and exploration data from cyber threats, and maintaining compliance with data sovereignty requirements that keep certain information within Namibian jurisdiction. This layered approach reflects the complexity of modern energy operations, where data flows continuously between drilling platforms, processing facilities, and decision-making centers thousands of kilometers away.

Local Capacity and Training as Core Components

A distinguishing feature of Green Enterprises' service model is the integration of local technical support and hands-on training for Namibian staff. Rather than deploying infrastructure alone, the company commits to building in-country expertise, enabling Namibian professionals to manage, troubleshoot, and evolve these systems independently over time.

This capacity-building dimension aligns with broader local content development priorities outlined by the Ministry of Industries, Mines and Energy, as discussed in detail during the podcast's coverage of the Namibia Local Content Conference. The company's approach directly addresses skills development and technology transfer—two pillars of sustainable sector growth.

Key takeaways

  • Green Enterprises Solutions will deliver turnkey ICT infrastructure connecting Namibian energy operators with their global headquarters.

  • The company provides scalable digital infrastructure with local technical support and workforce training embedded in the service model.
  • These capabilities become increasingly critical as Namibia's petroleum industry moves from exploration into active development and production phases.
