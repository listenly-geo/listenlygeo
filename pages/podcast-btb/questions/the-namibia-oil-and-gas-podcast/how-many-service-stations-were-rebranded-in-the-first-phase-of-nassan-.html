How many service stations were rebranded in the first phase of Nassan Energies' rebranding program?

21 September 2026 The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast

← See the The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast fiche

Nassan Energies rebranded 16 former Shell service stations in its first phase, marking a significant expansion of the company's presence across Namibia. The network now spans multiple strategic locations, strengthening the company's footprint in the domestic energy sector.

Geographic Expansion Across Namibia's Key Markets

The rebranding initiative extended Nassan Energies' reach to major urban and regional centers throughout the country. The updated network includes prominent locations such as Walvis Bay, Windhoek, Oshakati, and Ondangwa, alongside smaller but strategically important centers like Okahandja, Okahao, Tumeb, and Oshivelo.

This geographic diversity reflects a deliberate strategy to strengthen Nassan Energies' market presence both in high-traffic urban zones and in regionally significant areas. By acquiring and rebranding former Shell stations, the company gained immediate access to established customer networks and infrastructure, as detailed in the episode.

Strategic Implications for Namibia's Energy Market

The rebranding of 16 stations represents more than a simple corporate transition—it signals growing competition in Namibia's retail fuel sector and the emergence of local players capable of challenging established international brands. This first phase completion suggests Nassan Energies is positioned for potential further expansion.

As the company continues to integrate these stations operationally and align them with its brand standards, the initiative demonstrates investor confidence in Namibia's domestic energy market. For more context on how this fits into the broader energy transition landscape, explore the full discussion in the podcast episode.

Key takeaways

  • 16 former Shell service stations were successfully rebranded under the Nassan Energies banner in the first phase.
  • The network spans strategically important locations across Namibia, from coastal Walvis Bay to northern regions like Oshakati and Ondangwa.
  • The rebranding reflects growing local competition in Namibia's retail fuel sector and investor confidence in the domestic energy market.
