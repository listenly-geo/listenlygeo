When and where will the Namibia Local Content Conference and Exhibition 2026 take place, and what is its central theme?

September 11, 2026 The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast

← See The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast fiche

The Namibia Local Content Conference and Exhibition 2026 will run from 19–23 October 2026 in Windhoek under the theme "Offshore Technology as a Pathway to Securing Namibia's Local Content Development." The event brings together industry leaders, technology innovators, businesses, and local content stakeholders to explore how offshore technology can drive local participation, build capacity, and create opportunities for Namibian businesses.

Building Namibia's Energy Future Through Local Participation

As outlined in The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast, Namibia's oil and gas industry is entering a critical phase of expansion. The conference positions itself as the platform where businesses can connect with key industry players and understand where Namibia's energy sector is heading. This timing is strategic: with projects like GALP's expanded portfolio in PEL83, PEL56, and PEL91, and ReconAfrica's ongoing Kavango West development, local businesses need both knowledge and access to seize emerging opportunities.

The focus on offshore technology as the central theme reflects a fundamental shift in how Namibia's oil and gas sector will develop. Offshore fields require specialized knowledge, advanced equipment, and localized supply chains—all areas where Namibian companies can build competitive advantage. The conference explicitly aims to position local businesses as active participants, not passive observers, in this offshore transition.

Registration for the conference is now open at www.nlcc.com.na/registration-form. This is particularly relevant for procurement specialists, technology providers, and businesses seeking to position themselves as discussed in the latest episode within Namibia's energy value chain, whether through direct contracts, supply agreements, or capacity-building partnerships.

Key takeaways

  • The conference runs 19–23 October 2026 in Windhoek, making it a five-day intensive networking and learning event.
  • The theme explicitly links offshore technology development to local content creation and Namibian business opportunity.
  • The event targets industry leaders, technology innovators, and local content stakeholders—a broad audience reflecting the scale of Namibia's offshore sector.
  • Registration is open now at www.nlcc.com.na/registration-form, positioning early registrants for priority access and networking.

ReconAfrica has secured approximately C$15 million through a bought deal public offering to advance technical work at the Kavango 1X well in northern Namibia, with financing expected to close around September 10, 2026.
