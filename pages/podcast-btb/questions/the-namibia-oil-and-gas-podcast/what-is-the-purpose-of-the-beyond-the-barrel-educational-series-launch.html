Why did FNB and RMB Namibia launch the Beyond the Barrel educational series?

10 September 2026 The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast

← See the The Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast fiche

FNB Namibia and RMB Namibia launched Beyond the Barrel to equip Namibians with knowledge about the country's evolving energy sector and the economic opportunities emerging across the broader value chain. The educational series addresses a critical need as Namibia's oil and gas industry expands and local businesses prepare for participation in this growing landscape.

As the energy sector shapes Namibia's economic future, the Namibia Oil and Gas Podcast explores how financial institutions are positioning stakeholders to understand and engage with industry developments. Beyond the Barrel serves as a bridge between complex technical operations and the broader Namibian business community.

The series reflects a broader trend of industry readiness across Namibia. Local businesses require foundational understanding of offshore exploration, development projects, and value-chain participation to capitalize on opportunities as majors like GALP, TotalEnergies, and ReconAfrica advance their Namibian portfolios. Without this knowledge, businesses risk being sidelined from procurement, service delivery, and partnership opportunities.

This educational push aligns with significant industry momentum discussed in the latest podcast episode, where GALP completes its strategic partnership with TotalEnergies, expanding its portfolio across three offshore blocks, and ReconAfrica secures C$15 million to advance development at Kavango West. Both developments underline the urgency for local capacity building.

Beyond the Barrel complements formal industry events such as the Namibia Local Content Conference and Exhibition 2026, scheduled for 19–23 October in Windhoek. The conference brings together industry leaders, technology innovators, and local content stakeholders to explore how offshore technology can drive Namibian business participation and create sustainable opportunities—themes that the educational series will help citizens engage with on a foundational level.

For Namibian businesses looking to understand where the energy sector is heading and how to position themselves, the full episode offers additional context on industry partnerships and financing milestones that signal momentum across the sector.

Bridging knowledge gaps in Namibia's energy transition

Namibia's energy sector is at an inflection point. Multiple exploration and development projects are simultaneously advancing, from Venus progressing towards final investment decision to Mopane's appraisal campaign expected in Q4 2026. This concentration of activity creates both urgency and risk for local stakeholders unfamiliar with industry fundamentals.

Beyond the Barrel addresses this directly by making complex energy sector concepts accessible to the broader public. The initiative recognizes that sustainable local content participation requires not just regulatory compliance but genuine understanding of how offshore technology, value chains, and business models function. This foundation positions Namibian entrepreneurs and professionals to identify and pursue genuine opportunities rather than chase hollow promises.

Key takeaways

  • Beyond the Barrel is an educational series designed to help Namibians understand the country's evolving offshore energy sector and its economic implications.
  • The initiative recognizes that local business readiness depends on foundational knowledge of exploration, development, and value-chain participation in oil and gas.
  • The series supports broader industry momentum, complementing formal forums like the October 2026 Namibia Local Content Conference and Exhibition.
  • Educational initiatives like this are essential as multiple majors simultaneously advance portfolios in Namibia's three offshore blocks.
