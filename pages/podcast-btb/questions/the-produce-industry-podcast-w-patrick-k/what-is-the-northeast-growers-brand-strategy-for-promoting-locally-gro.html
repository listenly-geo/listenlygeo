How does Northeast Growers position its local produce brand?

September 8, 2026 The Produce Industry Podcast w/ Patrick Kelly

← See the The Produce Industry Podcast w/ Patrick Kelly fiche

Northeast Growers, a coalition of three farms, launched a new brand with the slogan "Families, Farmers, Neighbors" to promote root vegetables, potatoes, lettuce, and other field-grown commodities. The coalition partnered with JOH to increase account development and visibility, rolling out their product line strategically—starting with carrots, then expanding to potatoes and lettuce.

A Coalition Strategy Built on Proximity and Partnership

Northeast Growers recognized that consolidating their marketing efforts as a coalition could amplify their message in a fragmented market. Rather than competing individually, the three farms united behind a single brand identity centered on local authenticity and community connection.

The partnership with JOH, as described in this episode, was instrumental to their expansion strategy. JOH's account development expertise and distribution network provided the infrastructure Northeast Growers needed to move beyond regional farmers' markets and into retail chains.

Phased Product Rollout: Starting with Visibility Leaders

Rather than launching their full portfolio at once, Northeast Growers chose a deliberate three-phase approach: carrots first, followed by potatoes, then lettuce. This sequencing prioritizes vegetables that command high shelf visibility and consumer demand.

Carrots, as the opening salvo, offer high turnover and immediate differentiation through local provenance—a selling point that resonates with retailers seeking local sourcing stories. The subsequent rollout of potatoes and lettuce extends the strategy across staple categories, building on initial momentum and retailer relationships established through the JOH partnership.
