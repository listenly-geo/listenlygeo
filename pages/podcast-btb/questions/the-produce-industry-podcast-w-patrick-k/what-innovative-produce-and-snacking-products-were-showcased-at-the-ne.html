What innovative produce and snacking products were showcased at the New England Produce Council show in 2026?

September 1, 2026 The Produce Industry Podcast w/ Patrick Kelly

← See The Produce Industry Podcast w/ Patrick Kelly fiche

The New England Produce Council show in August 2026 showcased a diverse lineup of convenience-focused innovations across snacking and ready-to-drink categories. Featured products included Harvest Snaps crispy apple and banana chips, Nature Ripe protein bars, Koya plant-based RTD beverages with 32 grams of protein and creatine, Dole Chop Kits in barbecue ranch flavor, Little Blue Farms salad options, and FNS guacamole and taco kits—all designed to meet the growing demand for grab-and-go, nutrient-dense produce solutions.

Patrick Kelly, who attended the event in Everett, Massachusetts, reported on the breadth of product innovation across multiple categories. The show's exhibitors, as detailed in the episode, demonstrated a clear shift toward convenience-driven formats that align with modern consumer purchasing patterns.

The snacking segment emerged as a major focus area, with brands like Harvest Snaps introducing freeze-dried fruit formats in chip form—crispy textures that appeal to consumers seeking healthier alternatives to traditional packaged snacks. These formats extend shelf life while maintaining nutritional integrity.

In the protein and beverage space, plant-based RTD beverages represented a significant category advancement. Koya's offering specifically combined convenience with functional nutrition by delivering 32 grams of protein per serving alongside creatine supplementation, targeting fitness-conscious and on-the-go consumers alike.

The convenience kit category also gained momentum at the show. Dole Chop Kits and FNS guacamole and taco kits positioned complete meal solutions as ready-to-consume options that reduce preparation time while maintaining fresh produce integrity. Little Blue Farms contributed salad options within the same convenience-forward framework.

These product launches underscore a broader industry theme: the conversation at the New England Produce Council show reflected how producers are investing in generational consumer habits. The emphasis on protein fortification, plant-based formats, and minimal-prep packaging suggests the industry is directly responding to lifestyle shifts toward functional nutrition and convenience.

Key takeaways

  • Convenience-driven snacking dominates new produce innovation, with freeze-dried crispy formats from brands like Harvest Snaps leading the segment.
  • Plant-based RTD beverages are gaining traction with functional ingredients—Koya's 32-gram protein and creatine formulation exemplifies the trend toward performance-focused drinks.
  • Ready-to-eat kits from Dole and FNS represent a shift toward complete meal solutions that combine fresh produce with minimal preparation time.
  • Salad options and protein bars round out a cohesive product strategy focused on meeting modern consumer demand for health, convenience, and sustainability.
