How many TechCentro locations exist and where is the Harrisburg center modeled from?

September 20, 2026 Building PA Podcast · Robert Torres

← See the Building PA Podcast fiche

TechCentro operates six locations across Pennsylvania, with the Harrisburg center modeled directly after Lancaster's bilingual workforce development centers, which have been operating successfully for more than 10 years. The Lancaster model proved so effective that Reading, Lebanon, York, and Harrisburg each launched their own centers following the same framework.

How Lancaster's Success Became the Blueprint

Lancaster's TechCentro centers demonstrated a sustainable, replicable model for workforce development in the region. The longevity and success of Lancaster's operations—more than 10 years of continuous service—gave Robert Torres and his team confidence in adapting the same approach to Midtown Harrisburg. Rather than starting from scratch, TechCentro deliberately chose to follow a proven path.

This strategic decision to build on Lancaster's foundation reflects a broader pattern of growth across south-central Pennsylvania. Reading, Lebanon, and York each recognized the value of the Lancaster model and established their own centers, creating a coordinated network of bilingual workforce development services across multiple communities. As Robert Torres explains in the episode, this expansion was driven by genuine demand and the proven ability of the Lancaster centers to serve diverse populations effectively.

"I want people to understand how to problem solve, the critical thinking behind problem solving. That to me would be someone attractive to hire."

The expansion of TechCentro across six distinct locations demonstrates how the podcast episode discusses successful workforce models can scale when properly adapted to local communities. Each center maintains the bilingual approach and comprehensive training focus that made Lancaster's centers effective, while remaining responsive to the specific economic and demographic needs of its location.
