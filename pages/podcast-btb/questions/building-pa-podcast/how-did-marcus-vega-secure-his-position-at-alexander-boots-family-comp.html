How did Marcus Vega secure his position at Alexander, Boots Family Companies?

August 31, 2026 Building PA Podcast · Marcus Vega

← See the Building PA Podcast fiche

Marcus Vega secured his General Foreman position at Alexander, Boots Family Companies by networking with Jeff Smith at the 2025 KCA celebration, then sending a resume the next day. He received an interview request within hours and was hired after a couple of rounds of interviews.

The networking moment that changed everything

The opportunity began at the Keystone Contractors Association celebration in 2025, where Vega attended as an invited guest through his former employer, Howard Bernstein, who also nominated him for the KCA award. During the event, he connected with Jeff Smith from Alexander, Boots Family Companies, a conversation that would quickly lead to his next role.

The day after the celebration, Vega took action by sending his resume directly to Smith. The response came remarkably fast—within a few hours, he had an interview request. This rapid turnaround reflects both the value of the personal connection made and Smith's genuine interest in bringing him into the organization.

After going through a couple of rounds of interviews, Vega was hired as a General Foreman at Alexander, Boots Family Companies, a position he has now held for slightly over a year. This career move came during a time of recognition, as the timing coincided with his eventual selection for the 2026 KCA Labor Champion award, also known as the Thomas George Memorial Community Service Award.

The timing and approach offer valuable lessons in professional networking within the construction industry. As discussed in the Building PA Podcast episode, the combination of being in the right place, making a genuine connection, and following up immediately proved to be the formula that worked for Vega's transition into this new leadership role.

"I want to be a better person tomorrow than I am today, and each and every day I want to exemplify that at the job."

Vega's approach to his role extends beyond the technical aspects of construction management. His commitment to personal growth and daily exemplification of his values has made him a recognized figure in Pennsylvania's construction community. What was Marcus Vega's career progression in the construction industry?

Marcus started with basement waterproofing, then moved to drywall and framing work. His first drywall job was with Strawbridge Enterprises on the remodel of a historic property in Pennsylvania.

Key takeaways

  • Personal networking at industry events, combined with immediate follow-up, is an effective pathway to career advancement in construction.
  • A strong professional reputation and visibility within industry organizations like the KCA can open doors to leadership opportunities.
  • Marcus Vega's transition to General Foreman at Alexander, Boots Family Companies happened within hours of submitting his resume after a meaningful connection.
  • Long-term commitment to mentoring and supporting young people in the trades strengthens both individual careers and entire organizations.
