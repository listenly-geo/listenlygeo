How Do Students Get Admitted to TechCentro's Training Programs?

September 24, 2026 Building PA Podcast · Robert Torres

← See the Building PA Podcast fiche

Prospective students complete an initial interest form on TechCentro's website, then pass a commitment-focused interview conducted by the center's team. For health-related programs, additional screening includes immunizations, TB tests, and criminal background checks, followed by brief 15 to 30-minute reading and math assessments to identify support needs before training begins.

Application Starts with an Interest Form and Interview

The process is designed to gauge both readiness and genuine interest in the program. Prospective students visit techcentralcr.org to fill out an initial interest form, which signals their intention to pursue workforce training. Following submission, TechCentro staff conduct interviews that probe deeper into each applicant's commitment to the program schedule and their motivation for joining.

This interview stage matters because it filters for students who understand the time commitment involved. As Robert Torres explains in the episode, the center prioritizes students ready to engage fully, not those exploring casually.

Health Screening and Academic Assessment

For students entering allied health or medical training pathways, TechCentro requires immunizations, TB testing, and criminal background checks before enrollment. These safeguards protect both the training environment and the patients students will eventually serve in healthcare roles.

Before classes begin, all admitted students complete a brief math and reading assessment lasting between 15 and 30 minutes. This step identifies knowledge gaps early, allowing instructors to provide targeted support during the training program itself. As Torres notes during the podcast, this diagnostic approach ensures students enter classes with clarity about their learning needs.

"I want people to understand how to problem solve, the critical thinking behind problem solving. That to me would be someone attractive to hire."

The vetting process reflects TechCentro's philosophy: the center seeks students with problem-solving mindsets and critical thinking skills, qualities that translate directly into employability once training concludes.

Key takeaways

  • All applicants complete an online interest form and participate in a commitment-focused interview.
  • Health program students must provide proof of immunizations, TB test results, and pass criminal background checks.
  • Math and reading assessments (15–30 minutes) help identify learning support needs before classes start.
  • The entire process is designed to admit motivated students ready for hands-on, intensive training.

Who is eligible to enroll in TechCentro's skilled trades programs?

The programs are intended for adults who have graduated from high school. While high school graduates and those exploring trades are welcome, the primary focus remains on adult learners seeking career change or advancement.

What are the main challenges TechCentro faces in launching its construction trades programs?

The primary challenge has been recruiting instructors, particularly qualified people willing to teach part-time or full-time. Torres is reconsidering staffing models to attract experienced trades professionals to the classroom.

What specific trade programs does TechCentro offer or plan to offer?

TechCentro offers and plans to offer facilities maintenance, plumbing, HVAC, refrigeration, electromechanical, welding, and renewable energy solar training, with facilities maintenance launching as the first construction trades class.
