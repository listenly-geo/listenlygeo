Who Is Michael Threatt and What Is His Connection to Reading Rainbow?

August 15, 2026 Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! · Jason Benetti

← Voir la fiche Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me!

Michael Threatt is a librarian who was named the new host of Reading Rainbow on PBS. He grew up homeschooled and became a devoted fan of the original host LeVar Burton — a passion that eventually shaped his entire career. He is now the supervising librarian at the same library where he got his very first library card as a child.

From Homeschooled LeVar Burton Fan to Children's Librarian

Threatt's path to Reading Rainbow is a straight line drawn across decades. Growing up homeschooled, the original PBS series — and its host LeVar Burton — became a formative presence in his education. That early connection to books and broadcasting didn't fade.

He became a children's librarian, then a marketing librarian, and ultimately the supervising librarian at the very institution where he first discovered the library as a kid. His devotion to libraries is deep enough that, by the time he received his first book tattoo at age 24, he already held close to 40 library cards — and he had been collecting tattoos for almost 12 years by the time of his interview.

This story — the full-circle journey from homeschooled fan to PBS host — was highlighted in Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! as one of the standout moments from the first half of 2026.

Being named the new host of Reading Rainbow is not an abstract career milestone for Threatt. It connects directly to the show that helped shape him as a reader, a learner, and eventually a librarian. LeVar Burton hosted the original series for 23 seasons, and that legacy clearly left a deep mark.
