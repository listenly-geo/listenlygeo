How did Olympic swimmer Lilly King get proposed to by her husband?

August 22, 2026 Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! · Summer Spectacular 2026

← Voir la fiche Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me!

Lilly King was proposed to on the pool deck at Olympic trials, moments after finishing her race. Her husband — a former swim team member — managed to access the tightly secured deck despite army personnel being present, and got down on one knee. A friend had quietly tipped King off to remove her swim cap after the race so she'd look presentable, though King had no idea why until her husband appeared in front of her.

A proposal smuggled past army security — right on the pool deck

Olympic trials are notoriously locked down. Typically 100 to 150 swimmers compete in a single event for just two spots on the Olympic team, and the pool deck reflects that level of intensity: access is tightly controlled, with army personnel on site. Getting an unauthorized person onto that deck is no small feat.

King's husband, a former member of a swim team, knew the environment well enough to navigate it. He made it past security and waited for King to finish her race — then dropped to one knee in front of her. The story was shared on Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! as part of the show's 2026 summer spectacular highlights.

The one tip that made it all work: "Take your cap off"

A friend of King's was in on the plan. Before the race, that friend gave King a single, cryptic instruction: take your swim cap off as soon as you finish. The reason was purely practical — King needed to look presentable for the proposal photo that was about to happen.

King followed the advice without understanding it. She stepped out of the pool, removed her cap, and only then did her husband step forward. It's the kind of detail that gets lost in the drama of a proposal story — but on this episode of Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me!, it was front and center: the small logistical act that made a completely unexpected moment actually work.
