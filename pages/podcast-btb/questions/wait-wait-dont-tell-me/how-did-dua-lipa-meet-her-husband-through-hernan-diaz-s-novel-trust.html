How did Dua Lipa meet her husband through Hernan Diaz's novel Trust?

25 September 2026 Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! · Hernan Diaz

← See the Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me! fiche

Dua Lipa and her husband's connection started with a shared book: they bonded after one mentioned they were reading Hernan Diaz's novel Trust, and the other revealed they were too. One of the first pictures they exchanged was the cover of the book itself, turning a literary coincidence into the beginning of a real-life romance.

The meeting is a modern love story rooted in the oldest of traditions—finding someone who shares your passions. What begins as two people reaching for the same novel on a shared page becomes something far more personal: a snapshot of the book's cover, a visible proof of mutual taste, and the spark of genuine connection.

Hernan Diaz's Trust proved to be more than just a novel worth reading; it became a bridge between two people who might never have met otherwise. As Diaz discusses in the episode, the book has touched readers in unexpected ways, creating moments of recognition and common ground that extend far beyond the page.

The specificity of this encounter—exchanging pictures of a book cover as one of the first exchanges—speaks to how literature can serve as a language of compatibility. It's not just about enjoying the same story; it's about recognizing a kindred spirit through their reading choices. For Dua Lipa and her husband, Trust became a symbol of their shared sensibility, proof that they inhabited similar intellectual and emotional spaces before they ever truly met.

A deeper dive into how this remarkable connection unfolded can be found in this full episode of Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me!, where Hernan Diaz himself reflects on the unexpected impact of his work and the stories readers bring back to him.
