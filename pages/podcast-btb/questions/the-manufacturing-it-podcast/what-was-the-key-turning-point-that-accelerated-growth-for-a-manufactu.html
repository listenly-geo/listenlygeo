What was the key turning point that accelerated growth for a manufacturing IoT platform?

September 21, 2026 The Manufacturing IT Podcast · Bryan Sapot

← See The Manufacturing IT Podcast fiche

The breakthrough was creating a single turnkey solution that bundled data collection, cloud storage, and dashboards from one vendor—eliminating the need for manufacturers to coordinate with six different technology providers. This integration, achieved by 2019, made implementation fast and simple enough that adoption accelerated dramatically.

Manufacturers faced a fundamental friction point: integrating IoT platforms required piecing together solutions from multiple vendors. Getting machine data to flow from the production floor through the cloud and into actionable dashboards meant juggling separate contracts, integration work, and support relationships. As Bryan Sapot explains in the episode, this fragmentation created a deployment barrier that slowed adoption.

The shift happened when Mingo Smart Factory moved away from componentized offerings and instead delivered an end-to-end platform. Rather than selling a data connector here and a dashboard there, the team consolidated the entire workflow into a unified product. This meant a plant manager could onboard the system, connect their equipment, and see production insights—all through one interface, one contract, one support team.

The speed of implementation became a competitive advantage. Manufacturers no longer had to wait weeks for IT teams to orchestrate multiple vendor integrations. The simplified deployment path lowered perceived risk and made the business case clearer for operations teams evaluating the platform. This integrated approach is detailed at length in the episode, where Sapot describes how the company had to learn which features manufacturers actually wanted versus what the market assumed they needed.

By consolidating the vendor landscape from many to one, Mingo Smart Factory removed a major adoption bottleneck. The platform went from being a niche technical tool to a practical business solution that could get rolled out without complex internal coordination.

"It's easy to get people to do this stuff. It's hard to keep them doing it because they want help."

Adoption alone is not the full story. The episode reveals concrete results from customers like Ice Industries and Louisiana Fish Fry, where the simplified platform drove measurable gains in Overall Equipment Effectiveness and operational visibility within the first year of deployment.
