Why does clear messaging matter so much for filling your retreat?

September 4, 2026 The Retreat Leaders Podcast · Shari Leid

← See The Retreat Leaders Podcast fiche

Nail-tight messaging — clearly identifying who your retreat is for and what problem it solves — is the single most important element before worrying about platforms or tactics. Without this clarity, no marketing tool will move the needle. As evidence, Shari Leid shared that a woman in her early 70s booked her retreat without ever speaking to Shari first, trusting entirely on the resonance of her messaging and brand.

Most retreat leaders spend their time obsessing over which social media platforms to use, how to run ads, or what email sequences to deploy. Shannon Jamail and Shari Leid agree that this is backwards. Messaging comes first—everything else follows. If your ideal retreat participant doesn't instantly recognize themselves in your words, no amount of tactical effort will fill your retreat.

The proof is personal. One of Shari Leid's retreat participants was a woman in her early 70s who made the decision to book without any direct conversation or sales call. Her trust was built entirely through the clarity and authenticity of Shari's messaging and the way it spoke to her sense of belonging and connection. She saw herself in the retreat's purpose and committed.

This principle applies regardless of your experience level or business stage. As Shannon Jamail explains in the episode, even seasoned entrepreneurs and coaches often skip this foundational work, jumping straight to tactics because it feels more concrete and urgent. But retreats don't fill because of tactics. They fill because the right people recognize that your retreat was designed for them.

One detail worth exploring further: Shari also shared the specific framework she uses to diagnose connection and belonging in conversations, which ties directly into how her retreat messaging resonates with participants.

Key takeaways

  • Messaging clarity—who your retreat is for and what problem it solves—must come before any marketing platform or tactic.
  • When messaging is strong, retreat participants will book without requiring a personal sales call or direct contact.
  • Most retreat leaders waste energy on tactics while skipping the foundational work of identifying and speaking directly to their ideal participant.
  • Real retreat participants recognize themselves in authentic, clear messaging and commit based on that resonance alone.
