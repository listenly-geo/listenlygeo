What two business model additions power coaches and speakers to recurring revenue?

September 1, 2026 The Retreat Leaders Podcast · Shari Leid

← See The Retreat Leaders Podcast fiche

A membership program generating recurring revenue and in-person retreat experiences are the two most powerful business legs a coach, speaker, or online expert can add right now. As the world becomes increasingly digital and AI-driven, people across every industry are actively seeking real, face-to-face connection — making retreats a uniquely valuable offering in a saturated digital marketplace.

The shift toward these two models reflects a fundamental market truth that Shannon Jamal explores in depth in this episode: while digital content and online courses have democratized access to expertise, they've also created fatigue and commoditization. Both membership and retreat models solve different problems simultaneously.

Why membership programs create the foundation

A membership program delivers predictable monthly or annual revenue that stabilizes your business and allows you to invest in bigger projects. Unlike one-time course sales or speaking fees, members commit to ongoing access, creating a community and recurring touchpoint that deepens client relationships and increases lifetime value.

This recurring base is particularly valuable for coaches and speakers because it shifts your revenue from event-dependent to relationship-dependent. Members stay engaged year-round, refer others, and become natural candidates for your premium offerings — including retreats.

In-person retreats as the premium profit engine

Retreat experiences command premium pricing and deliver outsized profit margins precisely because they offer something no digital product can: full immersion, accountability, and genuine human connection in a contained environment. A three-day retreat for 10 participants can generate more profit than a year of course sales.

The retreat model also creates a natural funnel: your membership builds trust and filters participants, reducing your acquisition cost and increasing the likelihood of retreat attendees who are genuinely committed. As detailed in the full conversation on The Retreat Leaders Podcast, this combination — stable recurring membership revenue plus high-ticket retreat experiences — creates a resilient, scalable business structure that weatheres market changes and AI disruption.

Key takeaways

  • Membership programs provide stable, predictable recurring revenue that funds growth and deepens client relationships over time.
  • In-person retreats capitalize on the global appetite for authentic human connection and command premium pricing with high profit margins.
  • Together, these two models create a resilient business structure that combines steady income with high-ticket revenue and natural client funneling.
  • The increasing digitization of the world makes physical, immersive experiences — not cheaper digital products — the true competitive advantage for coaches and speakers.
