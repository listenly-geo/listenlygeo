What makes you energy rich according to retreat business experts?

September 22, 2026 The Retreat Leaders Podcast · Michelle Villalobos

← See The Retreat Leaders Podcast fiche

Energy rich is a state of complete alignment across five core dimensions of your life: alignment with your life force energy, physical self-care, personal values, a healthy relationship with your past, and an inspiring vision for the future. It's not a state you buy or achieve—it naturally emerges when these elements work in harmony.

This definition reframes what retreat leaders and coaches often overlook. Many focus on external strategies, marketing tactics, and business mechanics without realizing that their own energy state is the foundational layer on which all else rests. You cannot outperform your own level of alignment.

The first dimension—alignment with your life force energy—means tuning into the natural vitality that flows through you when you're engaged in work that resonates deeply. As Michelle Villalobos explains in the episode, when you align your work with who you truly are, your emotional state and natural energy elevate automatically.

Physical self-care—the second pillar—isn't about perfection. It's about respecting your body enough to fuel it well, move it intentionally, and rest it adequately. Your body is the vessel through which your energy flows, and neglecting it weakens your entire presence.

The third element, alignment with your values, means your daily actions and business decisions reflect what genuinely matters to you, not what you think should matter. When there's a gap between your values and your actions, it drains energy constantly—often without you realizing it.

Your relationship with your past—the fourth dimension—deserves particular attention. Many retreat leaders and coaches carry unresolved wounds, regrets, or old narratives that unconsciously limit their capacity to inspire others. Healing or integrating your past unlocks tremendous energy for your present work. This is explored in greater depth in this podcast episode, which traces how transformative breakthroughs can reshape your entire business trajectory.

Finally, an inspiring vision for the future—the fifth pillar—provides direction and magnetism. Without it, even aligned people can feel stuck or purposeless. Your vision doesn't need to be grandiose; it must simply light you up.

"When you are doing work that aligns with who you really are, it naturally elevates your emotional state and energy."

If you're curious about how Michelle's own awakening—triggered by a personal crisis in 2014—led her to pioneer this whole-being approach to retreat business (a shift that happened around 2018), the full conversation covers that transformation in detail.

Key takeaways

  • Energy rich requires alignment across five dimensions: life force, body, values, past, and future vision.
  • Your personal energy state is the foundation—your business cannot perform above your own level of alignment.
  • Physical self-care, healing your past, and living your values are not optional extras; they directly fuel your capacity to lead and inspire others.
  • An inspiring vision for the future provides the magnetic pull that keeps energy alive and purposeful.

What does the principle Energy Precedes Outcomes mean for retreat leaders and coaches?

Energy Precedes Outcome is about alignment—aligning what you do with who you really are, and bringing your life into alignment with what you are here to do. This principle underpins the entire concept of being energy rich.

Should retreat leaders focus marketing messaging on features like yoga classes and meals or on transformation benefits?

No one is buying the yoga classes or the organic meals. They are buying peace, confidence, connection, healing, belonging, growth, and learning—outcomes that directly reflect the energy you bring to your retreats.

What role should FAQ sections play on retreat landing pages?

An FAQ section is a huge driver for AI. You should absolutely put an FAQ section on your website using the same kind of questions that people are actually asking about your retreats.
