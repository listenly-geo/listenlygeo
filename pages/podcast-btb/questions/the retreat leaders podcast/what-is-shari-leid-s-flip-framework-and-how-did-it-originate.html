What is the FLIP framework and how did it originate?

August 29, 2026 The Retreat Leaders Podcast · Shari Leid

← See The Retreat Leaders Podcast fiche

FLIP is a four-step formula designed to help you connect with anyone in under 60 seconds. Shari Leid created it in 2023 during a transformative journey across the United States, where she shared a meal with a female stranger in every state — an experience that led her to metaphorically "flip" the cardboard box she was found in as an orphan into a table of connection and belonging.

The framework emerged from Shari Leid's deeply personal story. As she explains in The Retreat Leaders Podcast, she was orphaned as an infant and placed in a cardboard box with no known birth date or identifying information. This profound beginning shaped her lifelong commitment to human connection and the search for belonging.

In 2023, Shari Leid embarked on an ambitious cross-country project that would crystallize this mission. She traveled to all 50 states and shared a meaningful meal with a female stranger in each one. Through these intimate conversations with women she had never met, she discovered patterns of isolation and disconnection that ran deep, even among people living outwardly good lives.

From isolation to action—the symbolic "flip"

This journey became the genesis of FLIP. The framework's name itself carries profound symbolism: Shari Leid took the cardboard box that had defined her earliest days—a symbol of loss, displacement, and absence—and metaphorically transformed it into a table where real connection could happen. As she experienced across 50 states, that table was essential to combating a widespread epidemic of loneliness.

FLIP is now at the heart of Shari Leid's retreat business, which she discussed in detail on the podcast. The framework helps her create intentional spaces where women gather for three-day retreats capped at just ten participants, ensuring deep, authentic connection rather than surface-level networking.

"A lot of us have a good life, but if we don't feel connected, we're letting that good life stop us from a great life."

The FLIP framework's power lies in its simplicity and speed. Designed to establish a foundation of genuine connection in under 60 seconds, it addresses a real gap in how people interact—especially in contexts like retreats, where the quality of human connection directly determines the transformative impact of the experience. This is explored further in this episode of The Retreat Leaders Podcast, where Shari Leid unpacks how the framework has become central to her retreat business model.
