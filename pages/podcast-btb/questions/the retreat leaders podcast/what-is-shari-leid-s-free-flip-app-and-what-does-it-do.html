What does Shari Leid's free FLIP app actually do?

September 2, 2026 The Retreat Leaders Podcast · Shari Leid

← See The Retreat Leaders Podcast fiche

Shari Leid's free FLIP app guides you through four steps that diagnose exactly where your connection breaks down after a conversation. It identifies whether you've lost focus, failed to listen, slipped into control mode, or played small—pinpointing the specific barrier so you can build authentic connection.

A diagnostic tool built on real human connection work

The app is a practical companion to Shari Leid's FLIP framework, which promises to establish genuine connection with anyone in under 60 seconds. Rather than offering generic advice, the tool walks you through a reflection process designed to reveal your own patterns in real time—right after you've had a meaningful conversation.

Each of the four diagnostic steps addresses a different reason why connection falters. Whether you realize you weren't truly present, didn't listen deeply enough, defaulted to controlling the interaction, or diminished your own voice, the app helps you name what happened so you can adjust your approach the next time.

As Shari Leid explains in the episode, the framework came from her 2023 cross-country journey, where she shared a meal with a female stranger in all 50 U.S. states, directly experiencing the power of intentional presence and vulnerable listening.

Why the app exists: making connection accessible

The FLIP app removes friction from the learning process. Instead of reading a book or waiting for a coaching session, you can access the diagnostic immediately when you need it most—right after a conversation where something felt off. That timing matters: your memory is fresh, and the motivation to understand what went wrong is highest.

The fact that it's free makes the framework accessible to anyone—coaches, retreat leaders, employees, parents—who wants to understand their blind spots around connection. A deeper dive into Shari Leid's full methodology and how she teaches the FLIP framework in her retreats can be found in this podcast episode, which covers both the mindset and the mechanics behind her work.

Key takeaways

  • The FLIP app diagnoses connection breakdowns across four dimensions: focus, listening, control, and playing small.
  • It's a free, immediately accessible tool designed to work right after conversations when reflection is most valuable.
  • The framework emerged from Shari Leid's real-world experience sharing meals with women across all 50 states and her personal journey around belonging and vulnerability.
  • Using the app is a concrete first step toward building the authentic human connection that Shari argues is essential to living a great life, not just a good one.
