Can a successful retreat business be built without doing inner healing work first?

September 24, 2026 The Retreat Leaders Podcast · Michelle Villalobos

← See The Retreat Leaders Podcast fiche

You cannot lead from an open wound if that wound is directly tied to what you are teaching. When your personal healing in an area is incomplete, participants will sense the incongruency energetically—no matter how polished your messaging is. You can lead a retreat on topics outside your trauma, but teaching healing in the exact area where you remain unhealed creates a credibility gap that energy alone cannot hide.

The core issue is embodiment versus instruction. Teaching a retreat is not simply delivering content or techniques; it is inviting participants into your energetic field and your lived example. If you are standing in front of people showing them how to close a wound while that same wound remains open within you, they will feel the mismatch.

This does not mean you must be perfectly healed before starting a retreat business. It means integrity requires alignment between your message and your state. If you are building a retreat business around emotional healing, conflict resolution, or spiritual transformation in an area where you are actively struggling, that incongruency becomes the hidden curriculum participants absorb—more powerfully than any words you speak.

As Michelle Villalobos explains in the episode, you must be the embodiment of the thing you are leading. This distinction—between leading something and living it—is what separates a transactional workshop from a transformational retreat.

"When you are doing work that aligns with who you really are, it naturally elevates your emotional state and energy."

The real question is not whether you can build a retreat business without inner healing—you can, technically, if your topic is far removed from your wounds. But if you want to build a profitable, impactful retreat business rooted in authenticity, the work begins inward. Your energy and alignment will outpace any marketing strategy. As discussed at length in this podcast episode, participants do not just book retreats for the agenda—they book for the energetic transmission and the embodied wisdom of the leader.

If you are considering launching a retreat around healing, transformation, or personal growth, Michelle's full conversation with Shannon Jamail covers how to assess your own readiness and where to invest your inner work first for maximum impact and integrity.

Key takeaways

  • Embodiment precedes teaching—participants will sense incongruency between your message and your lived state, especially in areas tied to your unhealed wounds.
  • You can lead a retreat on topics outside your trauma, but teaching healing in an area where you remain unhealed creates a credibility gap no marketing can close.
  • Energy and alignment are your most powerful business assets—they transmit more powerfully than any agenda or technique you deliver.
  • Building an authentic, impactful retreat business requires inner alignment; your business cannot outperform your personal energy state.
