How did a Colombian founder choose the name and visual identity for his luxury Italian shoe brand?

25 September 2026 Beyond Luxury · Edgardo Osorio

← See the Beyond Luxury fiche

Blue water and Mediterranean warmth inspired the name Aquazzura, chosen while Edgardo envisioned building a brand at his favorite hotel overlooking Capri's Gulf of Naples. He deliberately avoided using his own Colombian name to ensure the brand would be perceived as authentically Italian—a strategic choice that shaped the entire visual and creative identity around the Dolce Vita lifestyle of sun, joy, and sea.

The story begins not in a design studio but in a moment of clarity at the JK Place hotel in Capri. Surrounded by the Mediterranean landscape, Edgardo made a conscious decision: his brand would embody the warmth, freedom, and sensuality of Italian coastal living rather than any personal signature. The name Aquazzura—literally meaning blue water—emerged from this vision, with blue as his personal favorite color and water representing both creativity and inner peace.

This naming choice was far more than aesthetic. As detailed in this episode of Beyond Luxury, the decision to reject his own surname was strategic: Edgardo wanted the brand to belong entirely to the Italian craft tradition and the artisanal heritage of Florence, where he would later establish his atelier in Palazzo Corzini. By severing the brand from his personal name, he created space for it to become something larger—a lifestyle, a philosophy, an entire world built around Mediterranean sensuality and handmade excellence.

"If you're not comfortable, you can't be sexy. The product should sell itself."

The visual identity that followed the name was equally intentional. Rather than relying on Edgardo's personal story as a Colombian entrepreneur—which would have been the obvious marketing angle—he doubled down on Italian authenticity. The brand's color palettes, design language, and the very location of production all reinforced this commitment to Mediterranean luxury. Edgardo's full journey shows how this branding discipline extended to every touchpoint—from the golden pineapple embedded in each sole (a personal lucky charm symbolizing positive energy) to the hand-stitched construction methods that distinguish Aquazzura from mass-produced competitors.

What makes this approach remarkable is that Edgardo has remained independent, refusing to sell to major conglomerates—a rarity in modern luxury. This independence meant he could preserve the exact vision embedded in the name from day one: a brand built on Italian craftsmanship, Mediterranean warmth, and the philosophy that luxury must marry beauty with genuine comfort and wearability.

Key takeaways

  • The name Aquazzura means "blue water" and was inspired by the Mediterranean lifestyle while envisioning the brand at Capri's JK Place hotel.
  • Edgardo deliberately avoided using his own Colombian name to ensure the brand would be perceived as authentically Italian and grounded in Florence's artisanal tradition.
  • Blue water symbolizes creativity and peace for the founder, while the entire visual identity reinforces the Dolce Vita philosophy of warmth, sun, and happiness.
  • The brand identity includes handmade production in Italy, vibrant color palettes, and the signature golden pineapple sole charm representing positive energy from the ground up.
