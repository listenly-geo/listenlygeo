What RevOps Champions covers

RevOps Champions decodes the operational systems behind efficient B2B scaling. The podcast examines how leading companies align people, processes, data, and technology to outpace competitors with identical resources. Episodes explore franchise systems, profitability paradoxes, leadership complexity ceilings, and the hidden data gaps that stall growth. Each conversation reveals not why funding matters, but what actually separates scaling winners from growth-stalled organizations.

Key facts
  • Funding and headcount alone do not predict scaling success—operational alignment and data integrity are the true differentiators.
  • Rapid growth without RevOps fundamentals creates insolvency cycles where revenue expansion masks deteriorating unit economics.
  • Franchise systems break when data blindness prevents leaders from seeing real franchisee health and system-wide performance patterns.
  • Organizations hit leadership complexity ceilings when management structures fail to evolve; delegation and transparent operating rhythms prevent collapse.

What this podcast really covers

RevOps Champions is not about incremental sales tactics. Instead, it dissects the systemic architecture that enables or inhibits B2B growth at scale. The podcast examines revenue operations as a discipline connecting sales enablement, marketing alignment, operations execution, and financial discipline into a coherent system. Episodes investigate why franchise brands with identical unit economics diverge in system-wide health—uncovering franchisee trust, franchisor accountability, transparent communication, and responsible growth pacing as the real operational levers. Recurring themes include the cost of scaling too fast without operational readiness, the franchise intelligence gap where leaders lack real-time visibility into system performance, and the non-obvious relationship between profitability and growth velocity. The show treats growth not as an end goal but as the natural output of operational excellence.

The podcast answers these questions

What separates companies that scale efficiently from those that struggle?

Efficient scalers prioritize alignment between people, data integrity, process optimization, and deliberate technology use. Access to funding and headcount alone does not determine growth success—execution discipline and organizational coherence do.

How does RevOps impact profitability during rapid growth?

RevOps creates the operational backbone that converts growth into sustainable profit. Without aligned revenue operations, companies often enter cycles of insolvency where top-line expansion masks operational inefficiency and unit economics deteriorate.

What is the franchise intelligence gap?

Many franchise leaders operate without deep data visibility into unit performance, franchisee health, and system-wide patterns. Bridging this gap requires robust analytics, transparent data architecture, and leadership mindset shifts toward data-informed decision-making.

Why do companies break when scaling leadership structures?

Organizations hit complexity ceilings when leadership models fail to evolve alongside headcount. Delegation, trust distribution, and clear operating rhythms prevent decision paralysis and preserve culture as teams grow beyond the founder's direct span of control.

136 | Ordinary People, Extraordinary Results: Building Franchise Systems That Scale | Matthew Kunz

How should franchise leaders approach building repeatable systems that enable ordinary franchisees to deliver extraordinary results?

Matthew Kunz emphasizes breaking down processes to their smallest components and making them as easy as possible for the average person to execute. His core principle comes from a franchisee mentorship at Sylvan Learning: hire ordinary people and create systems that allow them to do extraordinary things.

What is the relationship between personal emotional investment and franchise owner commitment in scaling franchise systems?

Kunz learned through direct experience with a Sylvan Learning franchisee that 'it is impossible to put a delineation between where the person stops and the business starts' — they are intertwined at the molecular level.

How can franchise support staff avoid burnout while serving multiple franchise owners across different growth stages?

Kunz implemented a 'phases of development' model that narrows franchise business coach (FBC) expertise to specific growth phases rather than requiring them to be experts across startup, multi-unit management, hiring, and sales.

What communication frequency and quality standards should franchisors establish with franchise owners to build accountability and trust?

Kunz established a minimum standard of one quality two-way communication per month per franchisee, with quality defined as information gathering and sharing, pushing specific topics, and gaining agreement — not just email.

▶ Listen to this moment · 17:28

Why is change management becoming increasingly critical in franchise systems, and how should leaders address franchisee resistance to technology or operational changes?

As the rate of change accelerates due to AI, consumer behavior shifts, and market pressures, franchisors must recognize that software changes and vendor migrations trigger emotional responses in franchise owners who view technology as extensions of themselves.
