What are the four main types of traders classified by holding period?

Position traders hold a position for months up to a year (364 days). Swing traders hold positions from days to weeks. Day traders hold positions throughout the day with no overnight positions. Scalpers hold positions for seconds to minutes with no overnight positions.

How does the risk and potential reward relationship differ between trading and investing?

Risk is directly correlated with reward. Trading generally includes more risk and more potential reward than investing. If someone claims there is no more risk in trading than investing, then there is no more potential reward either, meaning you are doing a lot more work for the same reward.

What is high-frequency trading and how does it differ from retail scalping?

High-frequency trading involves getting in and out of 10 positions within the blink of an eye on average, requiring super fast internet, algorithms, and a server closely located to the exchange. If you manually execute trades by clicking a mouse, keyboard, or calling your broker, you are not high-frequency trading, even if you execute 20 trades a day.

The podcast answers these questions

What is the difference between trading and investing?

Trading involves buying and selling securities over shorter timeframes to capitalize on price movements, while investing focuses on longer-term value accumulation. Trading requires active monitoring and technical analysis, whereas investing emphasizes fundamental analysis and buy-and-hold strategies aligned with financial goals.

How can small business owners improve their marketing in the digital age?

Digital marketing requires understanding your audience, leveraging multiple platforms strategically, and applying psychology principles to campaigns. Focus on channels where your target customers spend time, test messaging approaches, and measure results continuously to optimize your marketing spend and return on investment.

What investment assets should beginners start with?

Beginners typically benefit from starting with diversified assets like ETFs and index funds before advancing to individual stocks, options, or futures. Understanding risk tolerance, time horizon, and fundamental market mechanics ensures more educated decisions and reduces the impact of early learning mistakes.

How do first impressions impact business success?

First impressions establish credibility, build trust, and influence future business relationships in seconds. Mastering communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills directly affects sales conversion, partnership opportunities, and professional reputation across industries.
