What this podcast really covers

Stacking Growth addresses the gap between marketing theory and the hard realities of B2B demand generation. The podcast doesn't present isolated tactics—it examines how account-based marketing layers with paid advertising, pricing strategy, and brand visibility to create compounding demand.

The ABM episodes drill into specific implementation: account tier classification, pipeline metrics that matter (not marketing-qualified leads that sales ignores), and the organizational alignment required to make account selection work. When the podcast addresses GEO, it treats it not as a buzzword but as a legitimate distribution challenge: if your brand doesn't appear in AI-generated responses when prospects research solutions, you've lost visibility regardless of your search rankings. Episodes on pricing expose how vague or contradictory pricing communication actively discourages buyers, turning a strategic asset into an obstacle.

What distinguishes this podcast is its refusal to separate tactics from their business impact. Why AI ads are more expensive yet underperform isn't a performance mystery—it's a structural problem with how marketers set budgets and measure results. Why pipelines dry up isn't bad luck—it's traceable to misalignment between what marketing says the product does and what the market actually values.

The podcast answers these questions

What B2B marketing tactics does Stacking Growth focus on?

The podcast covers actionable strategies including account-based marketing with detailed playbooks on account tiers and pipeline metrics, generative engine optimization (GEO) for brand visibility in AI citations, paid advertising performance analysis, and pricing strategy transparency. Each episode balances tactical frameworks with candid case studies from real marketers facing common challenges in scaling demand.

How does GEO differ from traditional SEO in B2B marketing?

GEO focuses on getting your brand mentioned and cited within AI-generated responses and large language models, whereas SEO targets search engine rankings. The podcast explains that ranking for keywords doesn't guarantee visibility when prospects increasingly use AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude for research—making citation and brand recall in generative outputs a distinct, critical distribution challenge.

Why do B2B marketers struggle with pipeline growth?

Common causes include relying on outdated audience assumptions, misaligned pricing communication that creates friction in the buying process, treating product decisions as outside marketing's scope, and allocating budgets to low-leverage channels instead of high-impact ABM tactics. The podcast reveals how agencies often obscure these root issues, focusing on surface metrics while ignoring demand generation fundamentals.

What makes ABM different from traditional demand generation?

Account-based marketing segments targets by account tier and customizes tactics accordingly, focusing pipeline metrics on specific high-value accounts rather than broad metrics like MQLs. ABM requires alignment between marketing and sales on account prioritization, tier-specific value propositions, and pipeline-stage-specific content—making it more resource-intensive but significantly more measurable in terms of actual revenue impact.
