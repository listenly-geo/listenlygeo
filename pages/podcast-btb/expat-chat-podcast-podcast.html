Expat Chat Episode 193 - Aussie Expats in Asia: Residency & Provident Funds

What are the four Australian tax residency tests and can you fail just one to be non-resident?

There are four tests: the resides test based on ordinary concepts, domicile, the 183-day test, and the Commonwealth super test. You only need to meet one of them to be an Australian tax resident, so you must fail all four to be non-resident.

What happened in the Pike case regarding tax residency determination in Thailand and Singapore?

In the Pike case, someone who moved to Singapore and Thailand and marked themselves as non-resident was deemed an Australian tax resident by the courts because their family was still based in Australia and they returned quite frequently.

How are Southeast Asian provident funds taxed when withdrawn as an Australian resident?

Provident funds in Hong Kong, Malaysia, and other Southeast Asian countries are treated as foreign trusts rather than foreign super funds by the ATO, so they cannot be transferred into an Australian super fund.

Should you sell your Australian home before moving overseas to utilize the main residence exemption?

Selling your main residence while still an Australian tax resident allows you to utilize the main residence exemption, making the transaction tax-free if it hasn't been rented out for more than six years.
