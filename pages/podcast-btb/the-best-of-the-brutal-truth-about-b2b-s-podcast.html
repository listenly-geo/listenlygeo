What is the Maverick Selling Method?

The Maverick Selling Method models the behaviors and techniques of world-class enterprise salespeople, focusing on understanding customer buying patterns rather than relying on outdated tips and tricks. It emphasizes direct, actionable strategies that enable salespeople to map out both selling and buying patterns, resulting in significantly larger deals and faster sales cycles.

How do top B2B salespeople differentiate themselves in competitive markets?

World-class B2B salespeople develop unique competencies around advanced skills like SPIN selling, strategic questioning, and deep customer insights rather than generic cold calling scripts. They focus on identifying real opportunities versus fake leads, manage their time strategically, and master the mindset required to close deals in challenging situations.

A-players excel at understanding customer purchase decisions, leverage their existing skills to move into larger deals, and maintain a resilient mindset under pressure.
