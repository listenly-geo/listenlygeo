The podcast answers these questions

What are the latest Nashville commercial real estate market trends?

Nashville's commercial real estate market continues to show resilience with new headquarters announcements, retail developments, and office space recalibration. Recent episodes highlight significant projects on Second Avenue, new Costco locations, and major corporate expansions reshaping the market landscape.

How are office vacancy rates affecting Nashville's commercial real estate?

Office vacancy remains a significant challenge in Nashville, with landlords adapting strategies and some tenants initiating legal action over lease disputes. The market shows a gradual transition as development activity accelerates and adaptive reuse projects gain momentum.

What industrial and retail opportunities exist in Nashville?

Nashville's industrial and retail sectors remain active, with notable deals closing, new entertainment venues opening, and innovative concepts like indoor golf courses emerging. The market demonstrates strong buyer interest in diversified property types.

How do interest rates impact Nashville commercial real estate investment decisions?

Interest rate fluctuations directly influence transaction velocity and pricing in Nashville's commercial market. Recent episodes consistently track rate movements and their effects on deal activity, development financing, and buyer sentiment across all property sectors.
