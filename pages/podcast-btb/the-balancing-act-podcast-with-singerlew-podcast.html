The podcast answers these questions

What is transfer pricing and why does it matter for multinational companies?

Transfer pricing establishes the price at which related entities conduct transactions across borders, directly impacting tax liability and regulatory compliance. Misalignment with OECD guidelines and country-specific rules creates exposure to both penalties and double taxation for multinational operations.

How does forensic accounting help uncover financial discrepancies?

Forensic accountants use investigative techniques to trace financial transactions, identify fraud patterns, and reconstruct financial records in litigation or internal investigation contexts. Their work provides evidence-grade documentation suitable for legal proceedings and regulatory scrutiny.

What are the key steps to prepare a company for IPO readiness?

IPO preparation involves financial statement audit, governance framework alignment, revenue recognition consistency, internal control documentation, and regulatory compliance across all operational areas. Early engagement with advisors identifies structural gaps and accelerates the path to capital markets.

How do R&D tax credits reduce effective tax liability?

R&D tax credits provide direct offsets against tax liability for qualifying research, development, and technological advancement activities. Proper documentation of technical work, hourly allocation, and nexus to development projects maximizes credits while withstanding audit scrutiny.

Under California Probate Code 16061.7, when a settler dies or there is a new trustee, certain triggering events occur and the trustee is obligated to serve the beneficiaries with a trustee's notification that includes a copy of the terms of the trust.
