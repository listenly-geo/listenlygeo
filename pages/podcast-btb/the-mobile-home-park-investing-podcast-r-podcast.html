The podcast answers these questions

What makes mobile home parks superior to other real estate investments?

Mobile home parks generate higher cash-on-cash returns compared to residential fix-and-flips, multifamily apartments, and commercial retail properties. The business model benefits from recurring lot rent, reduced vacancy risk through community loyalty, and lower capital requirements for property improvements since residents typically own their homes.

How can new investors start mobile home park investing while employed full-time?

Beginning investors can build mobile home park expertise through structured learning from experienced operators, starting with smaller acquisitions that require less hands-on management, leveraging property managers to handle daily operations, and gradually scaling portfolio size. Part-time investors focus on underperforming parks with strong upside potential rather than stabilized assets.

What are the key metrics for evaluating mobile home park quality?

Critical evaluation factors include lot occupancy rate, rent collection history, tenant stability, utility cost recovery, age and condition of infrastructure, community manager effectiveness, and conversion potential for owner-occupied homes. Parks with occupancy above 85%, stable management, and utility recapture opportunities present the strongest investment profiles.

What is the vacant lot infill process in mobile home parks?

Lot infill involves identifying vacant or underutilized spaces within established parks and strategically placing new mobile homes to increase occupied lots and rental income. This value-add strategy minimizes acquisition costs since the land base already exists, reduces infrastructure development expenses, and capitalizes on existing community infrastructure and management systems.
