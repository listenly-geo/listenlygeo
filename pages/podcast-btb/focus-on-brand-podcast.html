What the episodes really reveal

The podcast answers these questions

How does AI impact B2B brand strategy?

AI reshapes B2B brand strategy by automating content generation, enabling personalized messaging at scale, and accelerating competitive differentiation. Teams now focus AI on brand execution and market positioning rather than foundational strategy work, allowing strategists to concentrate on the high-stakes decisions that define category leadership.

What makes a B2B rebrand successful?

Successful B2B rebrands start with clarity on business foundations, align internal teams through structured frameworks, and evolve identity to signal market leadership. Case studies show rebrands that pair visual evolution with strategic repositioning drive measurable growth, customer acquisition improvements, and internal team confidence.

How do mascots and visual identity drive B2B growth?

Distinctive visual identities and mascots create memorable brand moments that cut through B2B noise and build emotional connection. When paired with clear positioning, they amplify brand recall and support exponential growth trajectories in competitive categories by making B2B companies feel distinctive and approachable.

What is brand naming strategy in 2026?

Modern brand naming integrates AI insights with trademark strategy, domain availability, and scalability planning for future growth. Naming now serves as a business decision, not just creative expression, reflecting how companies plan for market expansion, category evolution, and long-term differentiation in increasingly crowded B2B segments.

How did Column 5 transition from a clothing retail business to becoming a B2B content agency?

What is the biggest shift in B2B marketing strategy between Google search optimization and AI-powered search results?

The script has been completely flipped from prioritizing top-of-funnel, high-volume content to prioritizing bottom-of-funnel and mid-funnel content. Google changed its search algorithm at the same time GPT and Anthropic rolled out, causing both search results and buyer behavior to shift dramatically.

How has buyer behavior changed regarding where B2B professionals search for solutions?

People are no longer going to Google as much to search for solutions like 'what's a good creative agency for B2B SaaS'. Instead, they are going to AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude asking 'who should I talk to'.

What is Column 5's approach to using AI tools in client work while maintaining ethical transparency?

Column 5 published an AI code of ethics addressing transparency and proactively communicating how they use AI. They use AI for efficiency tasks like data entry, data cleanup, and creating brand assets at different dimensions, but explicitly do not use it for creative work or writing blog posts for clients.

Why does being AI-forward become a competitive requirement for B2B agencies even when AI adoption may not be core to their value proposition?

Many companies, whether brand teams or marketing teams, are being issued mandates from the board to work with AI-forward agencies. This is similar to how the narrative was 'cloud computing company' a decade ago—it eventually becomes part of the background infrastructure rather than a differentiating message.

▶ Listen to this moment · 18:27

How should B2B brands balance messaging about AI capabilities without over-indexing or under-indexing on the feature?

The key is focusing on benefits versus features rather than trivializing or oversimplifying the work. Some companies genuinely need AI to be their core message due to board mandates or actual relevance, while others should treat it as part of the infrastructure without making it the primary message.
