707: How to Spot Leaders You Can Trust

What are the five ways to spot leaders you can trust according to Tony Richards?

Leaders you can trust treat waiters and waitresses with kindness and respect, move toward relationship rather than isolation, exhibit tender hearts and tenacious wills, listen eagerly and actively and openly, and take accountability by saying they screwed up and apologizing when they make mistakes.
