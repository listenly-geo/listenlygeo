What is the main premise of Norman Lamb's book 'Be More Human' regarding workplace mental health?

The core analysis is that society knows the social causes of mental distress, including workplace issues, but does very little to prevent that distress from happening in the first place.

How does medicalizing normal human responses to difficult work environments fail to address root causes of workplace mental distress?

When someone is struggling in a toxic office environment—not sleeping and becoming stressed about work—they may visit their GP, who then prescribes antidepressants and potentially takes them out of work for weeks.

What fiscal incentive did Norman Lamb propose to encourage employers to prioritize employee wellbeing?

When he chaired a mental health commission in the West Midlands in 2018, Norman Lamb's commission proposed a 'wellbeing premium' where governments could give employers businesses a discount on their national insurance if they demonstrated they were focusing on employee wellbeing in the workplace.

What does the Keep Britain Working Review identify as the main workplace health challenge in the UK?

Ill health and disability are becoming a bigger part of society due to an aging population and younger people developing mental health issues much earlier.

What are the return-to-work statistics for employees absent from work due to health reasons according to the Keep Britain Working Review?

If someone is away from work for 46 weeks, 96% return to work. Once absence reaches a year, less than 50% return.

How many vanguard employers are participating in the Keep Britain Working workplace health system initiative?

There are well over 200 vanguard employers, plus about 11 mayor authorities and all the devolved administrations. For every mayor authority involved, they are probably working with 50 or 100 SMEs in their localities.

What is the potential economic benefit of improving workforce participation for people with health conditions or disabilities in the UK?

If the UK achieved the same level of participation as some G20 competitors, there would be approximately one million more people working. Even improving the participation rate by one percentage point would add 300,000 people to the workforce, equivalent to the size of Cardiff.
