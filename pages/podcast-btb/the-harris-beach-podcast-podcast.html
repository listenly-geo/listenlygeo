What The Harris Beach Podcast covers

The Harris Beach Podcast dissects how evolving legal landscapes reshape corporate strategy, hiring practices, and workplace compliance. Episodes examine cannabis regulation, diversity imperatives, patent disputes in healthcare, and emerging tax structures affecting business operations. The show treats law not as abstract principle but as operational reality—the facts that determine whether organizations succeed or face penalties.

Key facts
  • Cannabis legalization creates dual obligations for employers: compliance with state adult-use regulations and maintenance of drug-free workplace policies that federal law may still require.
  • Cannabis businesses face federal tax code Section 280E, which prohibits deducting ordinary business expenses for companies trafficking in controlled substances, creating unique tax exposure.
  • Patent disputes in medical innovation directly determine market access timelines and competitive positioning for biotech firms—a single judgment can reshape entire product development roadmaps.
  • Diversity and inclusion in legal practice affects partnership tracks, client acquisition, institutional reputation, and talent retention within professional services firms.

Its episodes move beyond legal theory to examine practical compliance challenges: how New York's cannabis legalization affects hiring and workplace drug testing policies; how the CAR T-cell patent landscape influences biotech investment and manufacturing decisions; how the Hispanic National Bar Association's evolution reflects broader institutional shifts in professional services.

The podcast addresses three distinct professional audiences. Third, HR leaders navigating workplace policy in states undergoing rapid legal change, especially around drug-free workplace requirements, hiring discrimination, and diversity initiatives.

Beyond these core groups, the podcast serves law firm partners, especially those in corporate and employment practices, who must track developments affecting client advisories and market positioning.

The podcast answers these questions

How does employment law affect marijuana legalization in the workplace?

Employment law intersects directly with cannabis legalization, particularly regarding drug testing policies, hiring practices, and workplace safety protocols. Employers must navigate state regulations on adult-use marijuana while maintaining compliant HR policies that protect business interests and employee rights.

What legal and tax issues do cannabis businesses face?

Cannabis-related businesses confront complex tax implications under federal law, licensing requirements at state and local levels, banking restrictions, and regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions. Legal counsel specialized in cannabis law is essential for structuring operations that avoid costly penalties and tax exposure.

What role does diversity and inclusion play in modern legal practice?

Diversity and inclusion shape hiring, partnership development, client relations, and organizational culture within law firms and corporations. These principles affect talent retention, competitive advantage, and alignment with broader societal expectations of professional institutions.

How do patent disputes impact emerging medical technologies?

Patent disputes in medical innovation—such as those surrounding CAR T-cell therapies—determine market access, product development timelines, and competitive positioning for biotech companies. Intellectual property litigation directly affects investment decisions and commercialization strategy in the healthcare sector.
