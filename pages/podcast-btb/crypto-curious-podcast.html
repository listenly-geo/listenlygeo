The podcast answers these questions

What is cryptocurrency and why should beginners care?

Cryptocurrency is a digital form of money secured by blockchain technology, operating without central banks or governments. Beginners should care because crypto and blockchain are reshaping finance, investment opportunities, and how value transfers globally, making basic literacy essential for informed decision-making.

How do Bitcoin and Ethereum differ?

Bitcoin is a peer-to-peer currency designed primarily for value transfer, while Ethereum is a programmable platform enabling applications like smart contracts and DeFi. Bitcoin's scarcity drives its store-of-value narrative; Ethereum's flexibility powers an entire ecosystem of decentralized services.

What are NFTs and should investors consider them?

NFTs are unique digital assets verified on blockchain, representing ownership of digital or physical items. Investors should understand that the NFT market is highly speculative, driven by hype cycles, community value, and use cases ranging from art to gaming to real-world asset tokenization.

What does DeFi mean and how does it change traditional finance?

DeFi (Decentralized Finance) uses blockchain to replicate banking services—lending, borrowing, trading—without intermediaries. It removes geographic barriers, reduces costs, and operates 24/7, but introduces new risks around smart contract security and regulatory uncertainty.
