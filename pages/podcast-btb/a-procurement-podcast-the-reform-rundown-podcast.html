The podcast answers these questions

What are the main changes in the Procurement Act 2023?

The Procurement Act 2023 represents one of the largest regulatory overhauls in public sector procurement for decades, introducing significant changes to how government bodies manage supplier relationships and contract awards. The legislation modernises procurement rules to increase transparency, reduce bureaucracy, and enable more flexible approaches to strategic supplier engagement.

How can procurement professionals transition to strategic capability?

Transition from transactional procurement to strategic capability requires investment in process optimisation, people development, and performance management frameworks. Organizations must move beyond routine contract management to become active drivers of supply chain resilience and business value creation aligned with organizational objectives.

What role does contract management play in ecosystem development?

Contract management is foundational to building effective procurement ecosystems. Beyond enforcing terms, modern contract management leverages supplier relationships to drive innovation, ensure resilience, and create collaborative partnerships that benefit all stakeholders in the supply chain.

How should procurement adapt to supply chain volatility?

Procurement professionals must prioritize supply chain flow and system resilience over traditional footprint optimization. This involves building flexible logistics networks, diversifying supplier bases strategically, and implementing performance metrics that measure adaptability and responsiveness in volatile markets.
