What this podcast really covers

Glazing Insider operates as a structured platform for in-depth conversation with industry professionals who drive decision-making in fenestration and building envelope sectors. The podcast does not rely on industry commentary or analysis—instead, it centers entirely on the lived experience and strategic perspective of the people building and leading fenestration businesses.

Episodes address the real operational and market pressures facing glazing companies: how hardware suppliers position against competitors, why door manufacturers invest in composite technology, how certification processes shape product development, and what drives leadership strategy during periods of rapid market change. The recurring pattern across guests reveals a sector navigating consolidation, regulatory complexity, and the need for continuous innovation in materials and processes.

The podcast also captures how individual professionals enter and advance within fenestration careers, the mentorship and knowledge transfer within the industry, and the mindset required to operate in a specialized market dominated by technical expertise and long-cycle customer relationships.

Who this podcast is essential for

Glazing Insider serves three core professional audiences. First, fenestration and glazing professionals—engineers, product managers, and technical leaders within manufacturing and supply—gain direct insight into how peers solve technical and commercial challenges, and how strategy differs across competing businesses in the sector. Second, business leaders and entrepreneurs in adjacent building trades—such as construction management, architectural specification, and building materials distribution—listen to understand market drivers, competitive positioning, and innovation trends within a specialized supply chain. Third, marketing and business development professionals supporting fenestration brands use the podcast to understand the strategic priorities and language of decision-makers they aim to reach, and to track emerging competitive moves and product innovations.
