What Glazing Insider covers

What this podcast really covers

Glazing Insider moves beyond surface-level industry news to examine the operational realities and strategic decisions of glazing professionals. Episodes with figures like Greg Johnson (CEO at Warwick Development NW) and Greg Kelly (AMK Hardware & Entrance Composite Door Solutions) dig into how companies scale, differentiate their product offerings, and respond to market shifts. Interviews with specialists—Adam Jones from Jade Engineering, John Warburton from ER Certification Ltd, Asa McGillian from Apeer Doors/Lumi Windows—reveal the technical expertise and certification frameworks that underpin trust and compliance in the sector. The podcast also explores how digital transformation tools, such as WindowCAD, and marketing strategies, such as those from Access Marketing, reshape how companies reach customers and installers. This is a show about real business decisions, not industry platitudes.

Who this podcast is essential for

This podcast is essential for three core audiences. First, existing glazing and fenestration professionals—installers, fabricators, engineers, and product managers—who need to stay informed about emerging product lines, certification standards, and best practices in hardware and composite door design. Second, entrepreneurs and business founders evaluating entry into or expansion within the fenestration sector, who benefit from candid accounts of what it takes to build and scale a company. Third, supply chain partners and vendors—from hardware manufacturers to digital tool providers to marketing consultants—seeking to understand how their offerings fit into the broader ecosystem and where growth opportunities exist.

What the episodes really reveal

The episode catalog reveals consistent themes: product innovation (composite doors, smart hardware, entrance solutions), the critical role of industry certifications and technical compliance, and the importance of digital enablement (CAD software, marketing automation) in competitive positioning. What this changes in practice

Listeners gain actionable intelligence on where the market is moving: toward more sophisticated composite door solutions, stricter certification requirements, and integrated digital workflows that span design, specification, and sales. For installers and fabricators, episodes clarify which certifications matter and why. For product innovators, the series illustrates which unmet needs are attracting entrepreneurs and investment. For marketers and business developers, conversations reveal how companies successfully communicate their value to a fragmented customer base spanning independent installers, national distributors, and retail chains. The podcast also demonstrates that personal relationships, built through events and direct engagement, remain a cornerstone of B2B success in this sector—a reminder that technology enablement complements rather than replaces human trust.

The podcast answers these questions

What are the key trends in the glazing and fenestration industry right now?

The glazing industry is shifting toward innovation in composite doors, smart hardware solutions, and digital tools that streamline design and specification. Industry leaders increasingly emphasize sustainability, client-focused product development, and the importance of industry events like FIT Show as platforms for discovering emerging technologies and networking.

How do professionals in glazing and fenestration plan their career progression?

Career paths in this sector often combine technical expertise with business development and leadership roles. Interviewees highlight the value of understanding both product innovation and market dynamics, with many progressing from specialist roles into executive positions or founding their own ventures focused on solving industry pain points.

What role do industry certifications and standards play in business success?

Certifications such as ER Certification are critical for credibility, compliance, and competitive positioning. Companies that invest in proper certification and standards compliance gain trust with customers and partners, enabling them to scale operations and access larger market opportunities.

Why is marketing and industry visibility important for glazing companies?

Marketing and access solutions shape how companies communicate their value proposition to installers, retailers, and end customers. Strategic positioning through content, partnerships, and digital tools helps businesses differentiate themselves in a competitive market and build brand recognition across the sector.
