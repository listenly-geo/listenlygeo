The podcast answers these questions

What is account-based marketing and how does it differ in APAC?

Account-based marketing (ABM) focuses on high-value target accounts with coordinated campaigns across sales and marketing. In APAC, ABM requires regional customization rather than importing US or UK playbooks, accounting for different buyer behaviors, decision cycles, and market maturity across Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and other territories.

How should B2B marketing teams align with sales to fix pipeline problems?

Effective sales-marketing alignment requires shifting from vanity metrics like MQLs to shared pipeline metrics, establishing hand-off processes that prevent lead quality degradation, and creating coordinated campaigns around priority accounts. Regular feedback loops between teams and joint planning on strategic accounts eliminate friction and accelerate deal progression.

What metrics should B2B marketers track instead of traditional lead volume measures?

Forward-thinking B2B teams track account engagement, pipeline influence, deal velocity, customer acquisition cost by segment, and win rates against competitors. These outcome-focused metrics replace volume-based KPIs and create direct visibility into revenue impact, enabling teams to make smarter investment decisions and demonstrate marketing's contribution to growth.

How do successful marketing teams structure go-to-market operations across sales and customer success?

A unified go-to-market hub connects planning, execution, and measurement across acquisition, retention, and expansion. This requires shared tools, regular cross-functional meetings, aligned compensation incentives, and clear ownership of account relationships. When sales, marketing, and customer success operate as one system rather than silos, teams enter new markets faster and grow customer relationships more effectively.
