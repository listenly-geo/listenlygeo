How much does it cost to build a data center and how long does construction take?

Building a data center typically costs 150 to 200 million pounds, with expenses covering the building structure, foundations, and major equipment like generators and batteries. The entire construction process takes about two years from scratch, as equipment orders and delivery times are lengthy and complex.

Can data centers run on renewable energy sources like solar and wind power?

Many data centers already use solar and wind power, but the challenge is reliability since data centers must run 24/7/365 and the sun does not shine continuously. Companies like Pure are implementing solutions such as microgrids with biomethane and HVO (vegetable oil) generators, as demonstrated in their Dublin data center.

What backup systems do data centers have to prevent power outages from shutting them down?

Data centers have multiple backup options including generators, batteries, and UPS (uninterruptible power supply) systems that activate sequentially if one fails, ensuring they run 24/7/365 as critical national infrastructure without interruption.
