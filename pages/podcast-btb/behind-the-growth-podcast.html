What are the main reasons youth drop out of post-secondary education according to the Children's Aid Foundation's scholarship data?

According to 10 years of scholarship recipient data analyzed by the foundation, youth overwhelmingly drop out in their first year of post-secondary for concrete reasons: mental health crises, inability to pay rent and losing accommodations, or lacking basic needs like food or books.
