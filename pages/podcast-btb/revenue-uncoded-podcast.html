The podcast answers these questions

How does billing strategy impact enterprise revenue growth?

Billing strategy acts as a direct lever on revenue realization and cash flow predictability. A customer-centric approach aligns pricing models with buyer expectations, reduces churn from billing friction, and creates opportunities to upsell based on actual usage patterns rather than static contracts.

What role does AI play in modern B2B business operations?

AI reshapes how enterprises optimize billing workflows, predict customer behavior, and automate revenue operations. The current market surge around AI adoption creates both competitive pressure and strategic opportunity for leaders to integrate intelligence into existing systems before market consolidation occurs.

Why is telecom infrastructure relevant to B2B billing discussions?

Telecom represents one of the most complex billing and orchestration environments, combining BSS (Business Support Systems), OSS (Operations Support Systems), and network layers. Lessons from telecom orchestration translate directly to any B2B business managing multi-service, multi-tier offerings.

How should enterprises evaluate CRM and contact center technology integration?

Technology integration must prioritize customer experience outcomes, not feature count. New CRM and contact center systems succeed when they reduce friction in customer interactions, provide agents with real-time insights, and enable seamless transitions between sales, support, and billing touchpoints.

Why do large corporations continue using waterfall project delivery instead of adopting agile methodologies for faster deployment?

Resistance to change is the primary barrier. Large corporations maintain two-year approval cycles and require all stakeholders to be placated before proceeding, making projects predictably difficult and expensive.
