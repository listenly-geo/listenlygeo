What this podcast really covers

Resting Pitch Face operates at the intersection of search strategy, industry disruption, and brand authenticity. The podcast's recurring focus on SEO and search behavior stems not from devotion to a single tactic, but from recognition that search remains the primary discovery mechanism for B2B customers—even as the search landscape itself transforms. When construction companies face market consolidation, when financial services must navigate regulatory and competitive complexity, when automotive dealerships rethink customer journeys—the podcast demonstrates how search strategy either becomes a core business asset or a liability.

Beyond search, the show examines the human element driving marketing success: authenticity, storytelling, and transparent communication. Recent episodes reveal that AI adoption, while inevitable, threatens to commoditize mediocre marketing further. The hosts argue that differentiation emerges from brands willing to articulate genuine value, understand their audience deeply, and reject the temptation to chase every new platform or trend. This philosophy shapes every conversation—whether discussing why Google alone is insufficient for financial services or what defines brands capable of lasting influence in 2026.

Resting Pitch Face targets three distinct professional audiences. Third, marketing leadership and CMOs confronting organizational decisions around AI adoption, budget allocation, and channel strategy—the show offers the candid, results-oriented analysis typical board-level conversations demand but rarely receive from agency presentations.

The podcast answers these questions

What digital marketing strategies drive the most measurable results for B2B brands?

The podcast emphasizes that search engine optimization and audience-centered strategies remain foundational, but contemporary marketing success requires moving beyond traditional SEO. Industry-specific approaches—such as user-centered search in automotive or specialized positioning in financial services—now determine competitive advantage. Authenticity and human-centered brand building are becoming equally critical as technical performance metrics.

How is the role of AI changing marketing and brand strategy in 2026?

Artificial intelligence is reshaping how brands operate, but it is authenticity and genuine human connection that will define market leaders. The podcast explores how AI can enhance efficiency while warning against over-reliance on automation. Brands that balance AI capabilities with authentic storytelling and transparent communication will emerge as market leaders in the coming years.

Why is industry-specific marketing expertise becoming essential?

Generic marketing approaches no longer work across sectors. Construction, financial services, automotive, and travel industries each have distinct customer behaviors and search patterns. The podcast demonstrates that marketers must develop deep sector knowledge—understanding regulatory constraints, buyer journeys, and audience pain points specific to their industry—to create effective campaigns.

What mistakes do marketing teams make when scaling their digital efforts?

The podcast's 100-episode retrospective reveals that many teams over-invest in trending channels before understanding their audience, neglect foundational strategy in favor of tactical execution, and fail to measure true business impact. Sustainable growth requires disciplined testing, honest performance review, and willingness to abandon tactics that look impressive but deliver no real results.

What is the conversion rate for B2B businesses on Claude AI compared to traditional search?

Claude has on average across B2B a 15% conversion rate, compared to traditional search which ranges between 1 to 2 percent. This indicates that AI platforms are providing answers and summarizing content in a digestible way that encourages users to convert, whether that is submitting a lead or making a purchase.

How much does zero-click rate increase when an AI overview is present in search results?

Zero-click rates increase from approximately 34% without an AI overview to about 44% when one is present. This means that when a query is answered directly within the AI overview, a significant chunk of traffic that would otherwise click through to websites is lost.
