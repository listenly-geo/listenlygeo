What The Systems Engineering Podcast covers

The Systems Engineering Podcast brings together world-leading experts to discuss how organizations architect complex systems, avoid building the wrong solution, and scale engineering practices globally. Episodes feature interviews with INCOSE presidents, NASA engineers, and innovation strategists who reveal how Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE), system architecture discipline, and collaborative methodology shape the success or failure of technical programs. The podcast addresses the gap between what engineers build and what customers actually need—a distinction that separates billion-dollar innovations from costly pivots.

Key facts

  • INCOSE leadership shapes global standards that 20,000+ certified practitioners apply to system development across aerospace, defense, automotive, and technology sectors.
  • Model-Based Systems Engineering reduces integration failures by replacing document-centric processes with traceable, machine-readable design models that catch inconsistencies before implementation.
  • System architecture decisions made in the first 10% of a project determine 80% of its total cost and risk exposure—making upfront design discipline the primary lever for avoiding waste.
  • Fast, iterative, and collaborative approaches enable teams to validate whether they are solving the right problem before committing to full-scale development, cutting rework cycles by 40-60%.

The podcast answers these questions

What is Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) and why does it matter?

MBSE is a systems engineering approach that uses formal models to define, analyze, and communicate system requirements and architecture throughout the lifecycle. It replaces document-centric processes with model-centric methodologies, enabling better traceability, reduced ambiguity, and faster integration of complex system designs across distributed teams.

How does INCOSE influence modern systems engineering practices?

INCOSE (International Council on Systems Engineering) sets industry standards and certifications that guide practitioners in applying disciplined engineering methodologies. Through its frameworks, best practices, and global community, INCOSE shapes how organizations structure system development, manage stakeholder engagement, and measure engineering maturity across sectors from aerospace to software.

What role does system architecture play in preventing project failure?

System architecture defines the structural organization, interfaces, and interactions between components before implementation begins. A well-designed architecture identifies dependencies, eliminates technical debt early, and ensures that solutions actually address user needs rather than what engineers assumed was needed—preventing costly pivots and rework.

How can engineering teams become more collaborative and iterative?

Fast, iterative, and collaborative approaches break systems work into smaller cycles where teams validate assumptions with real feedback, integrate continuously, and adapt based on learning. This reduces the risk of building the wrong solution and improves cross-functional alignment between engineering, product, and operations from the start.
