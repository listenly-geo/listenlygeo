What topics does Re:Construction cover each week?

The podcast covers major UK construction industry events, regulatory changes, and sector developments. Episodes examine late payment reform, parliamentary projects like the House of Commons refurbishment, industrial relations, procurement practices, and profiles of influential figures in construction. Recent episodes have addressed power tools, plant hire profitability, and sustainability innovations like plug-in solar panels.

Who are the hosts of this construction industry podcast?

Bishop and Taylor are veteran construction industry journalists who bring deep expertise and institutional knowledge to their weekly discussions. Their background in construction journalism enables them to contextualize industry news and extract meaning from complex sector developments that shape policy and business strategy.

Why is late payment reform so frequently discussed in construction media?

Late payment is a persistent structural problem in construction that affects cash flow for contractors and suppliers. Reform discussions center on legal frameworks, government enforcement mechanisms, and their impact on small and medium-sized enterprises. The podcast explores proposed legislative changes and their real-world implications for business viability across the supply chain.

Construction news intersects with government spending, regulatory policy, and economic cycles.
