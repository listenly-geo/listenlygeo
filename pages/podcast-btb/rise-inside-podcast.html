The podcast answers these questions

What is the Beltdraulic™ technology?

Beltdraulic™ is a completely new, fluid-free, electric alternative to hydraulic cylinders developed by RISE Robotics. It delivers unprecedented efficiency, enabling manufacturers to build battery-electric equipment with better performance, extended runtime, and improved system availability compared to traditional hydraulic solutions.

Why should equipment manufacturers transition from hydraulic to electric systems?

Electric alternatives eliminate the maintenance complexity and environmental risks of fluid-based systems while reducing operating costs. Battery-electric machinery delivers superior performance with lower downtime, longer runtimes per charge, and a cleaner footprint—critical advantages for OEMs competing in increasingly regulated markets.

How do mechanical engineers integrate innovative technologies into production timelines?

True collaboration requires integrating new engineering concepts at the earliest stages of design. This prevents costly redesigns downstream and ensures that innovations like electric actuation are optimized for manufacturability, cost, and performance from inception rather than retrofitted afterward.

What role does industrial design play in commercializing new mechanical systems?

Industrial design translates engineering breakthroughs into customer-ready solutions by balancing performance, cost, aesthetics, and user experience. Effective commercialization requires mechanical engineers, designers, and commercial teams working in parallel from concept stage, ensuring the innovation delivers real market value.
