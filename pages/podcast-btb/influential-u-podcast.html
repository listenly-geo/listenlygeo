About this podcast

TQ is skillfully navigating exchanges with others. This is the podcast of Influential U®, the global education company that teaches Transactional Intelligence® (TQ). Each episode explores the hidden intelligence behind every human exchange — what actually decides an outcome between people, why expertise alone rarely produces the outcome you want, and what changes once you can see it. Hosted by John Patterson, Co-Founder and CEO of Influential U®. John is co-author, with Influential U® Co-Founder Kirkland Tibbels, of HIGH TQ: Why It Matters More Than Expertise. Take the free TQ Diagnostic™ at tqdiagnostic.com.

The Audiobook Is Live — and the Opening of HIGH TQ

What is High TQ and why does it matter more than expertise according to John Patterson?

High TQ is the capacity to navigate transactions successfully with precision, accuracy, and completeness. According to Patterson, while expertise is being commoditized by AI, how you transact is not.

▶ Listen to this moment · 4:18

When did High TQ book release and what was its initial ranking on Amazon?

High TQ came out in August 2026 and debuted at number one in the organizational change category on Amazon, holding that position for seven days. Patterson describes this ranking as fitting proof for a book about what actually decides outcomes between people.

▶ Listen to this moment · 0:30

What is the key question John Patterson asks professionals to reveal the gap in transactional intelligence?

Patterson asks: "What did you actually agree to?" This question—focusing on what was actually agreed to, not what was intended, assumed, or implied—consistently reveals what he calls the hidden intelligence behind human exchanges.

▶ Listen to this moment · 2:35

How long is the High TQ audiobook narrated by John Patterson?

The audiobook is seven hours and seventeen minutes long from start to finish. Patterson narrated it himself rather than hiring a professional narrator, deliberately making it sound uniquely human with his own voice, pauses, and imperfect takes, because the book is fundamentally about what happens between people.
