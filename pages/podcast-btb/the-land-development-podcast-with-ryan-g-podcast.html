The podcast answers these questions

What financial metrics do lenders evaluate in land development deals?

Lenders assess debt-to-equity ratios, cash reserves, development timelines, market absorption rates, and the developer's track record. Collateral strength and exit strategies are critical evaluation factors that determine loan approval and terms.

How do water rights impact project feasibility and costs?

Water rights determine a project's ability to operate and expand, directly affecting development costs and long-term viability. Scarcity or restriction can make projects economically unfeasible or require substantial remediation investments.

What environmental due diligence steps prevent costly development delays?

Early Phase I and Phase II environmental assessments identify contamination, regulatory issues, and remediation needs before acquisition. Proactive compliance prevents project delays and unexpected expense escalation.

Which legal mistakes most commonly derail real estate development projects?

Inadequate contract drafting, zoning misinterpretation, failure to secure proper permits, and poor entity structuring create liability exposure and project stalls. Legal review before commitment mitigates these risks substantially.
