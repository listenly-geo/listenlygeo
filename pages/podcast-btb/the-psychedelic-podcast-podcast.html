Daily Low-Dose Ketamine: Why Repetition May Matter More Than Intensity - Eiad Asbahi

Why did Eiad Asbahi transition from running Prescience Point hedge fund to founding Mindscape?

After 17 years managing Prescience Point and experiencing a major professional collapse when his largest investment position fell in September 2021 combined with personal divorce, Asbahi underwent deep inner healing through internal family systems therapy.

Mindscape has stripped the frills and cost out of the medication compared to other platforms that bundle in premium services most people don't use. While offering free healing collateral and high-level customer support, Mindscape's key distinction is its daily dosing protocol rather than peak experience protocols…

How does low-dose daily ketamine create space for self-observation according to Eiad Asbahi?

Ketamine creates space between your thoughts, allowing you to fall into your body and feel completely present. When thoughts slow down, you can observe them as they arise rather than subconsciously suppressing them.

What does the daily ketamine protocol at Mindscape look like in practice?

Asbahi takes ketamine daily to connect with himself rather than escape, using environmental framing like red lights and music. During experiences, the body takes over naturally—he uses a rebounder trampoline, stretches, and does vigorous movements.

How does Mindscape approach the distinction between therapeutic use and dependence on low-dose ketamine?

Asbahi challenges the narrative that addiction automatically means harm, noting that drugs have been part of human civilization for 10,000 years and Mindscape receives daily emails from patients expressing gratitude for life-changing effects.
