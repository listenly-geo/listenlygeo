The podcast answers these questions

What is driving the transformation in warehouse automation?

Warehouse automation is being driven by advances in robotics, AI-powered systems, and real-time supply chain visibility technologies. Founders and CTOs across the industry are developing solutions that enable faster, more efficient operations while reducing labor constraints and operational costs.

How are system integrators reshaping the industrial automation landscape?

System integrators are bridging the gap between raw robotics technology and practical warehouse deployment by customizing solutions for specific operational needs. They translate technical capabilities into business outcomes, making automation accessible to enterprises of all sizes.

What standards are emerging in robotics and automation?

Industry organizations like ASTM International are establishing formal standards for robotics and autonomous systems to ensure safety, interoperability, and scalability. These standards help companies implement automation with confidence while maintaining consistent performance across deployments.

How does Robot-as-a-Service change the economics of automation?

Robot-as-a-Service models eliminate large upfront capital investments by shifting robotics to an operational expense model. This approach lowers barriers to adoption for mid-market companies and enables faster scaling without long-term equipment commitments.
