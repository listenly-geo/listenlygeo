What Exit Rich covers

Exit Rich addresses the strategic and operational gaps that prevent 80% of business owners from achieving premium exits. The podcast answers these questions

Why do 8 out of 10 businesses fail to sell?

Most business owners lack an exit strategy from inception, missing critical opportunities to build value systematically. Without structured planning, proper financial documentation, and buyer-readiness infrastructure, businesses become difficult to market and qualify buyers.

What is the ST GPS Exit framework?

The ST GPS Exit is a proprietary methodology for planning and executing a business exit. It guides owners through identifying the right exit timing, preparing the business for sale, qualifying prospective buyers, and negotiating optimal deal structure to maximize proceeds.

How do the ST 6Ps influence business valuation?

The ST 6Ps framework encompasses six critical operational pillars that collectively determine enterprise value. Building a business that excels across all six dimensions creates multiple streams of value, reduces buyer risk, and justifies premium exit multiples.

What role does confidentiality play in M&A negotiations?

Maintaining strict confidentiality during the sale process prevents market disruption, protects customer and employee relationships, and preserves competitive positioning. A controlled buyer outreach strategy also ensures only qualified, serious bidders enter the process, strengthening negotiation leverage.
